ANGLE VERDICT: The hindsight/classifier literature splits cleanly on the goal-conditioning question. (A) True hindsight relabeling (HER, HER+demos, GoalGAIL, C-learning, Actionable Models) fundamentally requires a goal-conditioned critic Q(s,a,g) plus an achieved-goal extractor phi(s) and reward r(s,g); it does NOT apply as-is to a single-task critic. Retrofit is possible for BiGym (sim state exposes object poses -> phi is cheap; act at decision time with g=task-goal, use relabeled goals purely as auxiliary value-learning targets), but it multiplies critic input space and is a large change to CQN-AS. (B) Example/classifier-based methods (VICE, VICE-RAQ, MURAL, RCE, DDL, VPACE, SQIL) are NATIVELY single-task and non-goal-conditioned — this family is the direct fit. ANSWER TO 'can failure episodes become value signal without goal conditioning': yes, via four distinct mechanisms: (1) dense classifier reward — VICE/MURAL score every failure state with a graded success probability, giving nonzero TD signal on all ~300-540 steps of a failed episode instead of only terminal 0; MURAL's calibrated NML classifier is the variant that survived classifier-exploitation failures; (2) recursive classification — RCE dispenses with reward entirely and learns C(s,a)=P(future success) by TD, where success EXAMPLES (their 60 demos' success states + online successes) supply label-1 anchors and every online transition (failures included) supplies bootstrapped labels; values bounded in [0,1] so bootstrap-max optimism is capped, and in a discretized-bin critic the actor-free argmax makes RCE critic-only compatible — arguably the closest published object to 'critic-only demo-driven RL without BC'; (3) supervised distance-to-success — DDL regresses expected-steps-between-states from ALL rollouts (failures are full training signal), bootstrap-free so immune to their optimism mechanism, used as shaping on top of the true sparse reward; (4) explicit negatives — the transferable core of Actionable Models: hindsight-style positive-only data provably overestimates unexecuted actions, and their fix (regress Q of sampled unseen/perturbed actions toward the distribution minimum) is goal-free and is a direct published counter to the established sibling-bin-optimism inversion (their failure mechanism 1), strictly stronger than smoothing; VPACE's above-success-level value ceiling is the complementary anti-optimism bound to the failed Q-Transformer MC LOWER bound (caps the top of the range instead of compressing the negative half). CROSS-CUTTING FACTS: (i) Nair et al.'s canonical HER+demos recipe KEEPS a BC loss but gates it with a Q-filter (apply BC only where Q(s,a_demo)>Q(s,argmax)) — a self-annealing hinge is the field's answer to 'BC collapse when removed', not hinge deletion; it also uses resets-to-demo-states, which attacks their slow-TD-chain mechanism (2) orthogonally; (ii) classifier-reward methods add positives and therefore INTERACT BADLY with mechanism (1) if the classifier is miscalibrated (dense wrong reward inflates hijacked bins) — the negative-mining route (Actionable-Models-style artificial negatives on sibling bins, RCE's bounded targets, VPACE's ceiling) attacks inversion directly and is the safer first arm; (iii) all classifier/example methods here were validated with off-policy backbones (SAC/DDPG/QT-Opt) on sparse or reward-free manipulation, several vision-based and real-robot, so evidence transfers to their pixel BiGym regime; (iv) HER-family relabeling has known optimism bias in stochastic envs, largely moot in deterministic BiGym.

* Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) [Andrychowicz et al., NeurIPS 2017]
  core_idea: Relabel each trajectory's achieved future/final states as goals in a goal-conditioned critic, so every failed episode becomes a successful demonstration for the goal it did achieve; converts sparse 0/1 reward into dense positive TD signal across the replay buffer.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Solved sparse binary-reward Fetch push/slide/pick-place where vanilla DDPG got zero success; standard baseline for sparse manipulation since.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs achieved-goal extractor phi(s) and goal-distance reward r(s,g); no learned progress model.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Does NOT apply as-is: requires goal-conditioned Q(s,a,g), an achieved-goal extractor phi(s), and r(s,g). Retrofit path for BiGym: phi = object/prop poses from sim state, condition CQN-AS critic on g, act with g=task goal, treat relabeled goals as auxiliary value-learning tasks. Payoff would be exactly their missing failure-episode signal, and relabeling multiplies positive-reward chunk-level transitions by ~O(episode length), attacking the slow-TD-chain mechanism (2). Cost: large critic change, goal-space design, and relabeling still provides positives only — sibling-bin optimism (mechanism 1) untouched.

* HER + Demonstrations (Overcoming Exploration with Demos) [Nair, McGrew, Andrychowicz, Zaremba, Abbeel, ICRA 2018 (arXiv 1709.10089)]
  core_idea: DDPG+HER plus: demo replay buffer mixed into batches, auxiliary BC loss on demo actions gated by a Q-filter (BC applied only when Q(s,a_demo) > Q(s,pi(s))), and episode resets from demonstration states.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Order-of-magnitude speedup over HER on sparse-reward block stacking (multi-step, long-horizon); solved tasks neither RL nor BC alone could.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None beyond HER's goal machinery; Q-filter uses the critic itself. Demo-state resets need a resettable simulator (BiGym has one).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The field's canonical demo-driven sparse-RL recipe — and it KEEPS a BC term, but makes it self-annealing via the Q-filter: BC pressure vanishes state-by-state once the critic credibly prefers something else. Direct transplant to their hinge: apply the demo-action hinge only where the critic ranks the demo action below its argmax AND the critic is 'trusted' (e.g., after n successes at that state region); this is the published alternative to deleting BC and watching collapse at 5k steps. Resets-to-demo-states is critic-agnostic and directly shortens the TD chain (their mechanism 2); BiGym sim supports state resets from demo frames.

* GoalGAIL (Goal-conditioned Imitation Learning) [Ding, Florensa, Phielipp, Abbeel, NeurIPS 2019]
  core_idea: Combine HER with a GAIL discriminator: relabel both agent trajectories and demos with hindsight goals, use discriminator output as shaping reward; works with action-free or suboptimal demos.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Sparse-reward goal-reaching (four-room pointmass, Fetch pick-and-place) with few demos; outperformed HER and BC-initialized HER.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs goal extractor + adversarially trained discriminator.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Low direct fit (goal-conditioned + adversarial + actor-critic), but two transferable facts: demo trajectories themselves can be hindsight-relabeled (each intermediate demo state is a valid 'success' for its own prefix — in single-task form this is exactly their observed self-imitation channel, formalized), and discriminator-as-shaping degrades gracefully with suboptimal demos where BC does not. Mostly a bridge to the VICE/RCE family for their setting.

* VICE (Variational Inverse Control with Events) [Fu, Singh, Ghosh, Yang, Levine, NeurIPS 2018]
  core_idea: Define the task by example SUCCESS STATES only (no full demos, no goal conditioning): train a classifier with success examples as positives and policy states as adversarial negatives; its log-odds is a dense reward = probability the 'success event' occurs, grounded in an event-based control graphical model.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Vision-based pushing, reaching, insertion with no hand-designed reward; outperformed sparse-reward RL and reward-classifier-without-adversarial baselines.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs a learned success classifier updated online with policy negatives; no goal or progress model.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Natively single-task and non-goal-conditioned — directly applicable. Their 60 demos supply success-state positives for free (final frames + post-success tail; NB memory note: 96% of demo targets clip to top atom, i.e. plenty of success-state frames). Converts every failure episode into per-step graded TD signal, hitting mechanisms (2) and (3) (failures stop being zero-gradient, exploration episodes still pay). RISK: adversarial classifier is exploitable; a miscalibrated dense reward feeds mechanism (1) by inflating hijacked off-demo states — use as additive shaping on top of true sparse reward, never replacement.

* VICE-RAQ (reward learning with active queries) [Singh, Yang, Hartikainen, Finn, Levine, RSS 2019]
  core_idea: Off-policy (SAC) VICE plus active human queries: robot shows ambiguous states, human labels success/failure, labels added to classifier training set; ~50-75 binary labels suffice.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Real-robot vision-based mug pushing, cloth draping, book insertion learned in 1-4 hours without reward engineering.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Success classifier + a label oracle (env success predicate in sim).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Applicable in principle (single-task, off-policy), and the query mechanism is exactly a patch for classifier miscalibration at off-demo states — their judge-probe infrastructure could stand in for the human (automated success predicate exists in BiGym, so queries are free: label any state with the env's own success check). That collapses VICE-RAQ to 'ground-truth-labeled success classifier', the most robust variant of this family for them. Human-in-the-loop part is unnecessary in sim.

* MURAL (uncertainty-aware classifier rewards) [Li, Gupta, Reddy, Pong, Zhou, Yu, Levine, ICML 2021]
  core_idea: Replace VICE's adversarial classifier with a calibrated uncertainty-aware one via amortized Normalized Maximum Likelihood (meta-learned); reward = calibrated P(success|s), which is conservative on unfamiliar states and doubles as a count-based-like exploration signal.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Outperformed VICE and sparse-reward baselines on maze navigation, dexterous manipulation (claw, pen), and pick-place style tasks defined only by success examples.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Meta-learned NML classifier (heavier training loop than VICE); no goal conditioning.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The published fix for the exact failure that makes VICE dangerous in their setting: overconfident classifier output at off-demo states. Calibrated conservatism means hijacked/garbage states get LOW reward (aligned against mechanism-1 inversion) while genuinely near-success failure states get graded credit. Backbone-agnostic reward layer — drops onto a critic-only learner unchanged. Caveat: its exploration-bonus aspect conflicts with their finding that exploration dose taxes rollout success; use the calibration, tune down the bonus.

* RCE (Recursive Classification of Examples) [Eysenbach, Levine, Salakhutdinov, NeurIPS 2021]
  core_idea: No reward function at all: learn C(s,a) ~ P(reach success in the future) by a data-driven Bellman equation — success-example states are label-1 anchors, ordinary transitions get recursive targets gamma*w/(1+gamma*w) from the bootstrapped next-state classifier; policy maximizes C.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Outperformed SQIL/DAC/VICE-style baselines on sparse example-based manipulation (hammering, door opening, pick-and-place, bin tasks) using only success-state examples, no demos and no reward.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Success-state example buffer only; no goal extractor, no progress model; needs the recursive-classification target plumbing (n-step variant exists in the paper).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Closest published object to their goal: single-task, non-goal-conditioned, TD-based value-of-success learned directly from (a) success examples and (b) ALL transitions including failures — failure episodes are first-class training data pulling values down along executed paths. Values live in [0,1] (probabilities), giving a native ceiling on bootstrap-max optimism, and the classification targets are re-expressible on a C51 support. Published RCE uses a SAC actor only to take the soft-max over continuous actions; with coarse-to-fine discrete bins the argmax is exact, so a critic-only RCE-CQN is a coherent, novel-but-derisked arm. Limitation: still provides no signal for UNEXECUTED sibling bins — pair with explicit negatives (see Actionable Models) to close mechanism (1).

* C-learning [Eysenbach, Salakhutdinov, Levine, ICLR 2021]
  core_idea: Reframe goal-reaching as learning P(future state = g | s,a) via a classifier trained with positives from actual futures and NEGATIVES sampled from a marginal over random states; gives principled off-policy relabeling ratios.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Matched/beat HER-style Q-learning on goal-reaching control from sparse outcomes; primarily an analysis+algorithm paper.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Goal sampling distribution in original form; the transplanted negative-sampling trick needs nothing new.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Goal-conditioned, so not a direct fit — but it is the theoretical companion piece: it proves what relabeling ratio is unbiased and, crucially, institutionalizes RANDOM-NEGATIVE SAMPLING, i.e., explicit training signal that most (state, action) pairs do NOT lead to the outcome. That is the missing gradient for their unexecuted sibling bins. Single-task transplant: for each replay state, sample sibling bins uniformly and train their C51 heads toward the low atom with a small weight — the C-learning negative term without any goal.

* Actionable Models [Chebotar, Hausman, Lu, Xiao, Kalashnikov, Varley, Irpan, Eysenbach, Julian, Finn, Levine, ICML 2021]
  core_idea: Offline goal-conditioned Q-learning (QT-Opt style, critic-only with CEM argmax) from unlabeled robot data via hindsight relabeling, PLUS conservative 'artificial negative actions': because relabeled data contains only actions that DID reach the goal, Q-values of unseen actions are unconstrained and overestimate — so sampled random/perturbed actions are explicitly regressed toward the minimum value; plus goal chaining across episodes.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Real-robot vision manipulation skills learned fully offline from unlabeled data; also improved downstream sparse-task RL when used as auxiliary objective.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Original needs goal relabeling; the anti-optimism negative regularizer needs only sibling-bin sampling — no estimation machinery.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Highest mechanism-level match in the hindsight family. Their diagnosis is literally the paper's stated problem: 'hindsight relabeling... does not provide evidence for which actions are sub-optimal... resulting in over-estimation of their Q-values' — the same optimism that inverts their off-demo preferences via smoothing+bootstrap-max on sibling bins. The fix is goal-free and critic-only compatible: for each batch state, sample k unexecuted actions (sibling bins at each coarse-to-fine level) and train their distribution toward the bottom atom(s) with a conservative weight. Also proof-of-life that a CRITIC-ONLY (CEM-argmax) learner works at scale on real vision-based manipulation without an actor or BC term.

* SQIL (Soft Q Imitation Learning) [Reddy, Dragan, Levine, ICLR 2020]
  core_idea: Imitation as sparse-reward RL: fill replay with demos at r=+1, agent experience at r=0, 50/50 batches, run standard soft Q-learning; provably a regularized BC with a long-horizon sparsity prior.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Matched/beat GAIL and BC on image-based Atari, Car Racing, MuJoCo from demos alone.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None; only demo/agent buffer bookkeeping.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Critic-only compatible by construction (pure Q-learning) and BC-free in form — but it is imitation, not task RL: it rewards demo-transition membership, not env success, so agent-discovered successes earn 0 and asymptotic performance is capped at demonstrator level; known value-drift toward uniform as the agent matches demos. Relevant as the degenerate anchor of the design space: their current 'BC hinge + sparse reward' is roughly env-reward + SQIL-hardened-into-a-hinge. A soft SQIL-style +epsilon reward on demo-matching transitions (decaying with online success rate) is a way to convert the hinge from a loss-space constraint into a reward-space prior that TD can eventually override — a candidate for the 'self-annealing BC' arm alongside Nair's Q-filter.

* DDL (Dynamical Distance Learning) [Hartikainen, Geng, Haarnoja, Levine, ICLR 2020]
  core_idea: Supervised-regress d(s1,s2) = expected timesteps between states along observed rollouts; use -d(s, success-set) as a dense shaped reward (goal chosen by ~10 preference labels or a fixed goal state).
  sparse_reward_evidence: Real-world 9-DoF hand valve rotation from raw pixels with 10 preference labels and no reward function; solved sparse locomotion/manipulation benchmarks where sparse-only RL failed.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Yes — this IS a learned progress estimator (distance-to-success network trained from replay).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Single-task applicable with success-set = demo terminal states + online success frames. Key structural property for their pathology: the distance is trained by SUPERVISED regression on within-trajectory pairs — no bootstrap, no max — so it cannot manufacture optimism at unexecuted bins; failure episodes contribute full training signal (every pair of states in a failed rollout is a distance sample). Used as additive shaping on the true sparse reward it densifies the TD landscape without touching the critic's update rule. Caveats: distances are policy-dependent (nonstationary target as policy improves) and geodesic distance is not task progress around obstacles — shaping can create local optima; vision-based variant needs an embedding.

* VPACE (Value-Penalized Auxiliary Control from Examples) [Ablett, Limoyo, Sigal, Fazeli, Kelly, ICRA 2025 (arXiv 2407.03311)]
  core_idea: Example-based control (SQIL/RCE-style success-example buffers, including examples for auxiliary sub-tasks like reach/grasp/lift) plus an explicit value penalty forcing all values to stay at-or-below the value of success-example states — a learned ceiling that kills value overestimation in example-based RL.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Simulated + real robot manipulation without rewards or full demos; resolved documented value-overestimation blowups of naive scheduled auxiliary control; bounded value estimates throughout training.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs sub-task success-example buffers (sliceable from existing demos by event heuristics or manual tagging); no goal extractor or progress model.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Two directly usable pieces. (1) The above-success-level penalty is the mirror image of their failed Q-Transformer MC lower bound: instead of clamping the negative half (which compressed action gaps), it caps the TOP of the value range at what success states actually earn — a targeted brake on the bootstrap-max optimism that drives mechanism (1), while leaving the negative range free to spread action gaps. In C51 terms: penalize expected value above the success-state atomic expectation. (2) Auxiliary sub-task success examples (reach/grasp frames sliceable from their 60 demos) create intermediate value anchors along the chain, shortening effective TD distance (mechanism 2) without goal conditioning. Paper reports example-based learning beating full-trajectory and true-sparse-reward baselines in efficiency.

SURVEY VERDICT (benchmarks 2024-2026, BiGym/RoboMimic/ManiSkill sparse-reward): (1) NO published method achieves online-scaling sparse-reward manipulation RL without a strong demo anchor. Every winner couples a genuine TD critic to an imitation-derived constraint, and the one published ablation that removes the constraint from an action-chunked critic (Q-chunking's RLPD-AC, Li et al. NeurIPS 2025) collapses exactly like your no-BC run — this is now replicated external evidence that your 5k-step critic collapse is the expected default, not a bug in your stack. (2) CQN-AS's own paper never ablates REMOVING the DQfD large-margin BC loss; it only shows BC-only underperforms full CQN-AS. It samples 50% of every batch from demos and uses tiny exploration noise (eps~N(0,0.01)). So the field has never demonstrated the thing you are trying to build (critic-only BiGym RL without BC hinge); the closest claims all keep the anchor and change its FORM. (3) Scaling-with-online-steps evidence on BiGym-class tasks exists but is real-yet-anchored: CQN-AS overtakes ACT through online learning on 20/25 BiGym tasks over 100k steps while DrQ-v2+/CQN sit at 0% on many tasks; AC3 (arXiv 2508.11143, v2 Feb 2026, the only direct BiGym follow-up found that beats CQN-AS) shows steeper curves within 10k of 50k steps on 15 BiGym tasks with only 10 demos — but AC3 wins by INSTITUTIONALIZING self-imitation: its actor is updated exclusively on successful trajectories, with BC loss weight 1.0 vs Q-gradient weight 0.1, plus a learned contrastive progress reward. I.e., the current BiGym SOTA moved TOWARD self-imitation, not away from it — consistent with your 'gains ride on self-imitation' diagnosis. (4) The 'real value function' question resolves to: yes, winners learn real TD values, but the critic's job is LOCAL RANKING over an imitation-restricted proposal set, not global argmax over free action space — IBRL's critic chooses between IL-policy and RL-actor proposals (also inside the bootstrap target); QC's critic ranks best-of-N chunks sampled from a BC flow policy; Policy Decorator's critic only steers a bounded residual around a frozen BC base. These designs never ASK the critic about unexecuted far-off-demo bins, so sibling-bin optimism can't be exploited — a decode-restriction/proposal-set solution to your failure mechanism (1) that does not distort Q-values the way a hinge margin does. (5) On your failure mechanism (2) (TD chain too slow): Q-chunking's specific claim is that a chunk-level critic gives UNBIASED n-step backups (target is an n-step Q over the exact executed chunk) and that biased naive n-step (FQL-n/BFN-n) is often WORSE than 1-step — supporting chunk-level backup speedup but warning against plain n-step patches. DQC (2025 preprint) further decouples critic chunk length (long, fast credit propagation) from policy chunk length (short, reactive) via expectile distillation of a partial critic — directly relevant to your K=16 chunk with ~20-34 backups/episode. (6) Methods that do scale far beyond demos with sparse reward all buy extra signal from privileged machinery: RFCL (ICLR 2024) needs simulator state resets to demo states (reverse curriculum, 1-10 demos, solves ManiSkill2 PegInsertionSide/PlugCharger where RLPD/Cal-QL fail); HIL-SERL (2024, real-world) needs a trained binary success classifier + human interventions and then hits 100% in 1-2.5h on top of RLPD; DrS (ICRA 2024) needs stage indicators to learn dense rewards from sparse. AC3's anchor-point contrastive intrinsic reward is a lighter-weight learned-progress variant of the same idea. TAKEAWAY for your recipe: the literature's successful pattern is (a) keep a demo-fitted generative proposal distribution and let the critic ONLY rank within it (removes hinge distortion while preventing sibling-bin hijack), (b) chunk-level unbiased backups (you already have chunked critic; avoid naive n-step), (c) if you want scaling past demo ceiling, add a progress/success-classifier reward or reset-based curriculum rather than more exploration dose — published evidence says exploration-noise dose is kept tiny (CQN-AS 0.01) precisely because rollout-success poisoning is the binding constraint. ANGLE: online RL from demos WITHOUT a BC loss — what the literature says actually prevents critic collapse. Direct answer to the key question: YES, sparse-reward manipulation with demos-in-buffer and NO BC/margin term is repeatedly demonstrated: RLPD (ICML 2023) on sparse Adroit (door/pen/relocate, 22 expert demos, binary-sparse reward, no pretraining, no imitation term); SERL (ICRA 2024) and HIL-SERL (Luo et al 2024, arXiv 2410.21845) on REAL robots with binary classifier rewards reaching 100% success in 1-2.5h wall clock; IBRL (RSS 2024) on sparse 0/1 Robomimic from pixels (BC policy exists but only as action proposer, zero BC gradient into the RL objective); DDPGfD (Vecerik et al 2017) on sparse insertion; R2D3 (ICLR 2020) with a pure value-based recurrent agent (closest analog to critic-only CQN) on sparse hard-exploration tasks. The load-bearing ingredients decompose into three functional groups that map 1:1 onto the project's established failure mechanisms. (A) Anti-optimism on unexecuted actions [your mechanism 1: sibling-bin inversion]: RLPD's ablation says LayerNorm in the critic is THE crucial ingredient — without it on demo-heavy sparse Adroit, "collapsed performance, with no progress made on any task"; the stated mechanism is that LayerNorm bounds Q-value extrapolation for out-of-dataset actions, i.e., it caps exactly the smoothing+bootstrap-max optimism you diagnosed. Second lever: min-over-random-subset-of-ensemble targets (REDQ: N=10, subset M=2, keeps measured Q bias near zero even at UTD 20; DroQ gets the same with 2 critics + dropout ~0.01 + LayerNorm at SAC-level compute). Third lever, discrete-native: Q-Transformer's conservative regularizer regresses UNSEEN action bins toward the minimum attainable return (0), and notably found a CQL-style softmax penalty WORSE (over-anchors to data distribution) — this is the published antidote for empty sibling bins and is distinct from the MC lower bound you already tested (that clamps targets on EXECUTED actions; the conservative term suppresses UNEXECUTED bins — the two are orthogonal, and the paper uses both together). Theory backup: Gallici et al (ICLR 2025, PQN) prove LayerNorm + L2 stabilize off-policy TD (even without target networks); DR3 (ICLR 2022) shows TD on narrow demo-like data drives feature co-adaptation between (s,a) and (s',a') that causes exactly the fast implicit collapse you see within 5k steps, fixable by a feature dot-product regularizer, not BC. (B) Backup-speed vs erosion [your mechanism 2]: high UTD (20 state-based, ~10 pixel-based in RLPD; ~40 learner steps/env-step in DDPGfD) made safe by (A); n-step returns (n=5 in DDPGfD mix of 1-step+n-step and R2D3) shorten the sparse-reward chain; and symmetric 50/50 demo/online sampling guarantees a constant per-batch density of reward-carrying transitions so the TD chain is continuously re-anchored — RLPD found 50/50 best among ratios, but R2D3 found the demo ratio "the most sensitive hyperparameter" with SMALL ratios (1/256) optimal at scale, i.e., ratio must be tuned to the regime, and Lee et al (CoRL 2021) replace fixed ratio with density-ratio prioritization toward near-on-policy samples. Hybrid RL theory (Song et al, ICLR 2023, Hy-Q) proves mixed offline+online fitted Q-iteration is efficient WITHOUT pessimism or constraints — principled ground for pure replay-composition recipes. (C) Exploration dose taxing rollout success [your mechanism 3]: RLPD removes the entropy term from BACKUPS (not from acting) on sparse Adroit/AntMaze; IBRL replaces random exploration dose entirely — a frozen IL policy proposes an alternative action at both act-time and bootstrap-time and the critic picks argmax over {RL action, IL action}, so exploration rides on demo-competent proposals instead of noise (6.4x over RLPD on PickPlaceCan); HIL-SERL substitutes human interventions. Transfer note for CQN-AS: symmetric sampling + LayerNorm + subset-min ensemble + entropy-free targets are all critic-side and port directly; IBRL's proposal trick ports as "include the demo/BC-policy action's bin path as a forced candidate in coarse-to-fine argmax decode and in the bootstrap max," turning BC from a loss into a decode-time prior that cannot invert preferences. Caveat: WSRL (ICLR 2025) shows demo retention is not sacred — what matters is avoiding abrupt TD-target distribution shift (they warm-start with rollouts then discard offline data) — supporting the view that replay composition is a means to stable targets, not an end.

* RLPD (Reinforcement Learning with Prior Data) [Ball, Smith, Kostrikov, Levine — ICML 2023 (arXiv 2302.02948)]
  core_idea: Off-the-shelf SAC + three minimal changes: (1) symmetric sampling — every batch is exactly 50% online replay / 50% offline-demo buffer; (2) LayerNorm on critic MLPs; (3) large ensemble (E=10) with min over a random subset (Z=1-2) for targets, UTD=20 (state) / ~10 (pixels); entropy backup removed per-environment (off for sparse Adroit/AntMaze). No pretraining, no BC term, no explicit constraint privileging offline data.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Sparse Adroit (door/pen/relocate) with 22 expert demos + binary-sparse reward: exceeds prior work with no BC loss and no pretraining; ~2.5x improvement overall across benchmarks incl. AntMaze (sparse).
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No — needs only a demo buffer and env reward; no progress model, no success detector beyond the env's sparse reward.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The canonical no-BC recipe. Its stated collapse-prevention mechanism is LayerNorm bounding Q extrapolation for actions outside the dataset — the exact counter to CQN-AS mechanism 1 (unexecuted sibling bins inflated by smoothing + bootstrap-max). Ablation: without LayerNorm on demo-only sparse Adroit, 'collapsed performance, no progress on any task'; naive SAC + symmetric sampling alone diverges ('critic trying to catch up with a constantly increasing value'). All three ingredients are critic-side and port to a discrete C51 critic: LayerNorm in the critic trunk, ensemble of distributional heads with subset-min (or subset-mean-of-min) targets, 50/50 demo sampling, entropy-free targets.

* REDQ (Randomized Ensembled Double Q-learning) [Chen, Wang, Zhou, Ross — ICLR 2021 (arXiv 2101.05982)]
  core_idea: Enable UTD>>1 (G=20) for model-free Q-learning via ensemble of N=10 Q-functions with in-target minimization over a RANDOM subset of M=2 per update; explicitly measures and controls the mean and std of Q estimation bias, keeping it near zero across training.
  sparse_reward_evidence: None directly (MuJoCo dense locomotion); its role in this survey is as the load-bearing sub-component inside RLPD/SERL, which do have sparse evidence.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The mechanism that makes high update rates safe: your mechanism 2 (TD chain too slow: ~20-34 chunk-level backups per 300-540-step episode) is attacked by raising UTD, but naive UTD amplifies the optimism of mechanism 1; REDQ shows subset-min over an ensemble is the specific bias-control that de-couples update rate from divergence. Directly portable: ensemble the C51 heads, bootstrap from min (or lower-quantile) over a random pair.

* DroQ (Dropout Q-functions) [Hiraoka et al — ICLR 2022 (arXiv 2110.02034)]
  core_idea: Replace REDQ's 10-critic ensemble with 2 critics each carrying dropout (small rate, ~0.01) + LayerNorm; matches REDQ sample efficiency at high UTD with ~SAC-level compute. Performance is highly sensitive to dropout rate; with a large ensemble, LayerNorm alone suffices without dropout.
  sparse_reward_evidence: None directly (MuJoCo); inherited via RLPD-style pipelines that adopt it.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Cheap implicit-ensemble variance for a big transformer critic where a 10x ensemble is unaffordable (CQN-AS with K=16 chunks, 3 levels x 5 bins). Dropout+LayerNorm inside the critic gives the min-target machinery of (A) at 2 forward passes. Also independent evidence that LayerNorm is the non-negotiable half of the pair.

* DDPGfD [Vecerik, Hester, Scholz, ... Riedmiller (DeepMind) — arXiv 1707.08817, 2017]
  core_idea: Demos and agent transitions share one replay buffer; the demo/online mix is AUTO-TUNED by prioritized experience replay (demos get a small constant priority bonus, never evicted); mix of 1-step and n-step (n=5) returns; multiple learner updates per env step (~40); L2 regularization on actor and critic. No BC loss, no reward shaping.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Four simulated sparse-reward insertion tasks (outperforms DDPG and hand-shaped-reward DDPG) + a real-robot clip-insertion task, sparse reward only.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The original existence proof of the whole angle: sparse-reward manipulation solved with demos in buffer and pure TD. Its two portable ideas: (1) PER as an adaptive alternative to a fixed 50/50 ratio — priorities automatically re-weight demo transitions when the critic's TD error on them grows (i.e., exactly when erosion starts), which is a self-correcting brake on your erosion-injection race; (2) n-step returns to shorten the sparse credit chain at chunk level.

* R2D3 (Recurrent Replay Distributed DQN from Demonstrations) [Paine, Gulcehre, Wang, ... — ICLR 2020 (arXiv 1909.01387)]
  core_idea: Value-based (critic-only, dueling, recurrent) agent with TWO buffers — demo and agent — sampled at batch level by Bernoulli(demo-ratio rho); n=5-step double Q-learning; NO margin/BC loss (deliberately drops DQfD's imitation losses). Key finding: demo ratio is the most sensitive hyperparameter, and surprisingly SMALL ratios (down to 1/256) were optimal on the hardest tasks; too-high ratio overfits to demos and fails.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Eight 'Hard-Eight' sparse hard-exploration 3D tasks with highly variable initial conditions; solved by R2D3 where DQfD-style baselines and no-demo agents got zero reward.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The closest published analog to CQN-AS's regime: a pure Q-network (no actor) learning sparse hard-exploration tasks from demos with no imitation term at all. Its demo-ratio result cuts against blind 50/50 transplantation: the correct ratio depends on scale/regime and should be treated as a first-class knob (cf. pending Wave-3 D2 ratio-schedule arm). Caveat for synthesis: R2D3 ran at massive scale (256 actors, billions of frames), so it proves feasibility of no-BC value-only learning, not sample efficiency at 100k steps.

* IBRL (Imitation Bootstrapped Reinforcement Learning) [Hu, Mirchandani, Sadigh — RSS 2024 (arXiv 2311.02198)]
  core_idea: Train a standalone IL policy on demos, FREEZE it, then run online RL (TD3-style, ensemble critics, high UTD, 'actor dropout') where the IL policy proposes an alternative action at (a) act time and (b) bootstrap-target time; the agent/target takes whichever of {RL action, IL action} has higher Q. Zero BC gradient in the RL loss; imitation influences only the argmax candidate set.
  sparse_reward_evidence: 7 sparse 0/1-reward continuous-control tasks from pixels (3 Robomimic + 4 Meta-World); 6.4x higher success than RLPD on Robomimic PickPlaceCan; also real-robot results; beats RLPD and MoDem.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No progress model; needs a trained IL policy (which the project already has de facto via demos/BC infrastructure).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The cleanest published alternative to a BC hinge: imitation is moved from the LOSS to the DECODE/BOOTSTRAP CANDIDATE SET, so the critic is never forced to rank demo actions high (no preference inversion pressure), yet targets are grounded by demo-competent actions — directly counters mechanism 1 (garbage bins can't win the max while the demo-action bin is a forced candidate) and mechanism 3 (exploration rides on IL proposals, not noise dose). For CQN-AS: force the demo/IL action's coarse-to-fine bin path into the candidate set of both the greedy decode and the bootstrap max.

* SERL / HIL-SERL [Luo et al — SERL: ICRA 2024 (arXiv 2401.16013); HIL-SERL: 'Precise and Dexterous Robotic Manipulation via Human-in-the-Loop Reinforcement Learning', 2024 (arXiv 2410.21845)]
  core_idea: Real-robot instantiation of the RLPD recipe: SAC + symmetric sampling from separate demo and online buffers + LayerNorm critic ensemble + high UTD, with reward supplied by an offline-trained binary success classifier. HIL-SERL adds human SpaceMouse interventions (DAgger-style corrections that enter the buffers as data, not as a loss).
  sparse_reward_evidence: HIL-SERL: 100% success on contact-rich real tasks (Jenga whipping, pan flipping, timing-belt/dashboard/motherboard/IKEA assembly, dual-arm) in 1-2.5 h of real-world training; SERL: near-100% on PCB insertion/cable routing/object relocation in 15-60 min with ~20 demos. No BC term anywhere in the RL objective.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs a binary reward classifier (success detector) trained from a few labeled frames; HIL-SERL needs a human for interventions. No progress/state estimation.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The strongest applied evidence that the no-BC replay-composition recipe scales to precisely the setting the project cares about: sparse binary reward, few demos, contact-rich manipulation, success that grows with online steps via genuine value learning (policies end up exceeding demonstrator skill and speed). Confirms the RLPD ingredient list is sufficient outside benchmarks. Its interventions mechanism is a labeled-data alternative to exploration dose (mechanism 3).

* Q-Transformer conservative regularizer (the other half, not the MC bound) [Chebotar et al — CoRL 2023 (arXiv 2309.10150)]
  core_idea: Discrete per-dimension autoregressive Q-learning where Q-values of action bins NOT in the dataset are explicitly regressed toward the minimal attainable return (0 for 0/1 reward), instead of being left to smoothing/generalization; combined with (separately) an MC-return lower bound on targets. Ablation: replacing the push-to-zero term with a CQL-style softmax penalty performs significantly worse because it over-binds to the data distribution.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Large-scale real-robot manipulation with sparse binary rewards (700+ tasks, demo + autonomous mixed data); primarily offline-with-online-data regime rather than from-scratch online.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The published, discrete-native antidote to mechanism 1: unexecuted sibling bins get an EXPLICIT negative supervision signal rather than optimistic fill-in, so bootstrap-max cannot elevate garbage. Critically, this is orthogonal to the MC lower bound already tested in the project (which clamps executed-action targets and was hypothesized to compress action gaps); Q-Transformer uses both jointly, and the collapse-prevention burden is carried by the unseen-bin push-down, not the MC clamp. For C51, implement as cross-entropy toward the minimum-support atom on non-taken sibling bins (weight is a knob; too-strong reduces to hard conservatism).

* Balanced Replay + Pessimistic Q-Ensemble [Lee, Seo, Lee, Abbeel, Shin — CoRL 2021 (arXiv 2107.00591)]
  core_idea: Offline-to-online fine-tuning with (1) balanced replay: a learned density-ratio network prioritizes offline samples that are near-on-policy w.r.t. the current online distribution (adaptive, state-dependent alternative to a fixed 50/50 ratio); (2) an ensemble of pessimistically pretrained Q-functions to suppress over-optimism on unfamiliar actions at novel states during the early online phase, exactly when bootstrap error from distribution shift would destroy the initialization.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Locomotion + robotic manipulation fine-tuning benchmarks (mostly dense/shaped); mechanism-level relevance rather than sparse-manipulation proof.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs a small density-ratio (likelihood-free classifier) network — a form of distribution estimation, but no progress/success model.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Names and attacks the same failure the project observes at off-demo states: 'state-action distribution shift leads to severe bootstrap error which destroys the good initial policy'. Its two ideas generalize the fixed-ratio recipe: sample demos MORE where they are relevant to current visitation (a state-conditional ratio, richer than the pending anneal-schedule arm), and enter the online phase with pessimism that relaxes as data accrues.

* PQN / LayerNorm+L2 TD stability theory [Gallici et al — 'Simplifying Deep Temporal Difference Learning', ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2407.04811)]
  core_idea: Proves analytically that LayerNorm + L2 regularization make off-policy TD convergent, eliminating the need for target networks and large replay buffers; PQN (parallelized Q-network) validates this with a pure Q-learning agent that is stable at high throughput.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Atari/Craftax etc., not sparse manipulation; contributes the mechanism proof, not the task proof.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The theoretical backbone for why RLPD's LayerNorm ablation is decisive, stated for exactly the critic-only Q-learning family CQN-AS belongs to: divergence from off-policy (demo-heavy) data is a regularization problem, not something that requires a BC anchor. Prescription for the no-BC line: LayerNorm every critic block + weight decay on the critic, then re-test BC removal; the 5k-step collapse is the signature this theory predicts for an under-regularized TD system.

* DR3 (explicit regularization against TD feature co-adaptation) [Kumar, Agarwal, Ma, Courville, Tucker, Levine — ICLR 2022 (arXiv 2112.04716)]
  core_idea: Shows TD bootstrapping on narrow (demo/offline) data induces implicit regularization that co-adapts features of (s,a) and (s',a') pairs, collapsing effective rank and Q quality; adds an explicit penalty on the dot product between consecutive state-action features to cancel it.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Offline RL benchmarks (D4RL, Atari); mechanism-level relevance.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: A candidate root-cause account of the observed 'critic collapses within 5k steps of removing BC': with 60 demos dominating the reward signal, TD chains run repeatedly over the same narrow trajectories, which is DR3's precise collapse condition. The BC hinge may currently be acting as an accidental feature-rank preserver; DR3's penalty (or even monitoring feature rank / consecutive-feature dot products as a collapse probe) replaces that role without constraining action preferences.

* Hybrid RL (Hy-Q) — theory of mixed-buffer Q-learning [Song, Zhou, Sekhari, Bagnell, Krishnamurthy, Sun — ICLR 2023 (arXiv 2210.06718)]
  core_idea: Proves that fitted Q-iteration on a mixture of offline and online data ('hybrid RL') is statistically and computationally efficient WITHOUT pessimism, optimism bonuses, or imitation terms, provided the offline data has coverage of a good policy; online data corrects offline extrapolation and vice versa.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Theory + experiments incl. hard-exploration (Montezuma's Revenge with offline data); not manipulation.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The principled justification that symmetric/mixed sampling alone is a legitimate substitute for a BC anchor in a critic-only method: demos supply coverage of the success manifold; online rollouts supply the off-demo corrections. Frames the project goal ('success scales with online steps via genuine value learning') as exactly the regime hybrid-RL theory covers, and implies the fix for collapse should be sought in coverage/regularization, not in re-adding imitation.

* WSRL (Warm-Start RL) — the replay-retention counterpoint [Zhou et al — 'Efficient Online Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning Need Not Retain Offline Data', ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2412.07762)]
  core_idea: Shows retaining offline/demo data during online fine-tuning is NOT necessary: a short warmup phase of rollouts from the pretrained policy 'recalibrates' the Q-function to the online distribution, after which offline data is discarded entirely; learns faster than retention-based methods (incl. RLPD-style).
  sparse_reward_evidence: Offline-to-online benchmarks (AntMaze sparse, Adroit, kitchen); fine-tuning regime with pretrained init rather than from-scratch demo-driven RL.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Reframes what the demo buffer is for: not a permanent anchor but a device to avoid abrupt TD-target distribution shift. Supports the project's self-imitation observation (successful online rollouts can take over the demos' anchoring role) and motivates schedules where demo ratio decays as online successes accumulate (Wave-3 D2), rather than fixed 50/50 forever — with the warning that the transition must be bridged by on-distribution success data, not by dropping demos cold.

* Cal-QL (Calibrated Q-Learning) [Nakamoto, Mark, Kumar, ... Levine — NeurIPS 2023 (arXiv 2303.05479)]
  core_idea: Conservative (CQL-style) offline pretraining whose learned values are additionally CALIBRATED to be at least the value of a reference (behavior) policy, so the Q-function enters the online phase neither over- nor catastrophically under-estimating; online fine-tuning then improves monotonically without a BC term.
  sparse_reward_evidence: AntMaze (sparse), Adroit, Franka Kitchen offline-to-online; not from-scratch online demo-driven RL.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs reference-policy value estimates (MC return-to-go on demo/offline trajectories) — already available in the project's replay pipeline.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Speaks directly to the project's MC-lower-bound hypothesis: pure pessimism 'clamps the negative half of the value range, compressing action gaps' — Cal-QL's contribution is precisely that uncalibrated conservatism causes an initial unlearning dip and slow recovery, and that the fix is bounding conservatism from below by a reference value (e.g., the demos' empirical return-to-go), not abandoning conservatism. Suggests a calibrated variant of the sibling-bin push-down: suppress unexecuted bins only down to a floor tied to the demo value estimate.

Survey of dense-ification of sparse rewards from demos, mapped onto the CQN-AS failure mechanisms (F1 = sibling-bin optimism/inverted preferences off-demo, F2 = TD chain too slow vs erosion, F3 = exploration dose poisons data mix). Cross-cutting findings: (1) The field splits into policy-preserving potential-based shaping (Ng et al. 1999: F(s,s')=gamma*Phi(s')-Phi(s) leaves the optimal policy set unchanged) vs policy-ALTERING learned rewards (discriminator/ranking/OT), which trade invariance for stronger signal and are all hackable off-distribution. (2) Wiewiora (JAIR 2003) proved PBRS is exactly equivalent to Q-value initialization with Phi — for a critic-only method this means demo-progress shaping can be implemented as a critic prior/initialization instead of a reward term, sidestepping the C51 fixed-support problem (shaped returns shift by Phi(s_0) and would be clipped by the C51 support, silently breaking invariance; state-only potentials, same gamma, terminal Phi=0 are all required for the invariance theorem; state-ACTION potentials — the natural form for demo actions — break invariance unless the look-ahead-advice form of Wiewiora et al. ICML 2003 / Harutyunyan et al. AAAI 2015 is used; with K=16 action chunks the chunk-boundary MDP is still an MDP so Ng's theorem applies with gamma^K and Phi evaluated at chunk boundaries). (3) Dense shaping only adds signal ON EXECUTED transitions — it directly attacks F2 (shortens effective credit-assignment horizon so ~20-34 chunk-level backups suffice) but does NOTHING for F1's unexecuted sibling bins. The only surveyed designs that attack F1 head-on are support-masking (Rank2Reward: progress reward multiplied by an in-distribution discriminator mask, so off-demo states get explicit LOW reward instead of optimistic smoothing) and success-vs-failure discriminators (DrS: their ~300 online episodes of failures become negative training data; dense term bounded to alpha=1/3 per stage band so it can never invert stage ordering — an explicit anti-inversion mechanism). (4) Discriminator reward-sign theory (Kostrikov et al. ICLR 2019, DAC) is literally the optimism story: strictly-positive rewards + bootstrap-max induce survival/looping bias, strictly-negative induce premature termination; AIRL's logD-log(1-D) neutral form and absorbing-state handling are the standard fixes and matter for replay episode-end handling. (5) Two blueprints exist for removing the BC hinge specifically: ROT (adaptively decayed BC weight via soft Q-filtering — trust the critic only where it beats BC — matching the observed 5k-step collapse when the hinge is removed cold) and SQIL (imitation expressed entirely as reward labels +1 demo / 0 agent with 50/50 balanced replay — critic-only compatible, moves imitation pressure from policy-space loss into value space where it can be traded off against the real sparse reward). (6) Universal embedding rewards (VIP/LIV/TCN/TimeRewarder) are cheap ready-made potentials — VIP's delta-of-goal-distance reward is literally PBRS form — but are noisy OOD (LIV authors concede zero-shot rewards fail on unseen domains) and locally non-monotonic under visual aliasing/POMDP (TimeRewarder analysis), so they are safest consumed inside the PBRS wrapper where a bad Phi slows learning but cannot change the asymptotic optimal policy; naive distance-to-goal shaping outside that wrapper provably creates local optima (Trott et al. NeurIPS 2019). Net recommendation ordering for this project: DrS-style success-vs-failure stage discriminator (uses existing failures, bounded anti-inversion, no new supervision beyond the 0/1 reward they already have) and Rank2Reward-style support-masked progress > PBRS with demo-phase potential implemented as critic init/prior (C51-safe) > SQIL/ROT hinge-removal recipes > frozen VIP/LIV potentials (domain gap risk on BiGym pixels).

* Potential-based reward shaping (PBRS) with demo-derived potentials [Ng, Harada & Russell, ICML 1999; Wiewiora, JAIR 2003 (Q-init equivalence); Wiewiora, Cottrell & Elkan, ICML 2003 (look-ahead advice); Brys et al., IJCAI 2015 (Similarity-Based Shaping from demos); Harutyunyan et al., AAAI 2015 (arbitrary rewards as dynamic advice potentials)]
  core_idea: Add F(s,s')=gamma*Phi(s')-Phi(s) to the sparse reward; Ng 1999 proves the optimal policy set is invariant for any state potential Phi. Brys 2015 (SBS) builds Phi from demos via a Gaussian similarity metric over demonstrated state-action pairs; Harutyunyan 2015 converts arbitrary auxiliary rewards (e.g. demo-progress) into dynamic advice potentials learned as an auxiliary value function, extending invariance to state-action advice; Wiewiora 2003 shows PBRS is exactly equivalent to initializing Q with Phi.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Classic gains on sparse Mario/CartPole (Brys 2015: shaping+initialization best on CartPole, initialization-only better on Mario — evidence the same Phi helps differently per domain); RSfD literature shows demo potentials prevent early-training instability. Evidence on hard sparse manipulation is thinner; Trott et al. NeurIPS 2019 (Keeping Your Distance) shows NAIVE distance-to-goal shaping creates local optima that stall sparse tasks — their fix is self-balancing shaped rewards.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs a potential function only: demo phase/timestep index, distance-to-nearest-demo-state under some metric, or a learned auxiliary value. No dynamics model, no stage labels. Quality of Phi affects speed, not the asymptotic optimum.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Best theory-safety per unit effort: shaping preserves the optimal policy so it cannot ADD inversion (unlike learned rewards), and it attacks F2 by densifying TD signal along executed transitions. Three critical caveats for CQN-AS: (a) C51 fixed support — shaped returns are offset by Phi terms; clipping to the support silently breaks invariance; the Wiewiora Q-init equivalence gives the clean alternative: encode Phi as critic initialization/prior instead of reward, no support change needed; (b) demo actions suggest state-ACTION potentials, which break invariance unless look-ahead advice form is used; (c) does nothing for F1 — sibling bins still get no signal. With K=16 chunks, define Phi at chunk boundaries; invariance holds in the chunk-level MDP with gamma^K.

* VIP — Value-Implicit Pre-training [Ma et al., ICLR 2023 (arXiv 2210.00030)]
  core_idea: Casts representation learning from action-free human video (Ego4D) as offline goal-conditioned RL; the learned embedding implicitly parameterizes a goal-conditioned value function as (negative) embedding distance to a goal image. Dense reward for a new task = delta of goal-embedding distance between consecutive frames — structurally a potential-based shaping term with Phi = -dist(embed(o), embed(g)).
  sparse_reward_evidence: Frozen VIP reward enables trajectory optimization and online RL on simulated and real-robot tabletop tasks where no dense reward exists, significantly outperforming prior pretrained representations (R3M etc.) as reward providers. Tasks are mostly short-horizon reaching/pushing/pick-place; no whole-body or long-horizon evidence.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs a goal image per task and a frozen pretrained visual encoder; no actions, no stage labels, no in-domain fine-tuning (zero-shot claimed). Progress is implicit in embedding distance.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Ready-made Phi for PBRS at zero training cost — the delta-form reward is already potential-based, so in theory policy-preserving. Risks for BiGym: domain gap (Ego4D-to-BiGym pixels), symmetric-distance assumption (VIP authors note the metric assumes reversibility; asymmetric/quasimetric structure is future work), and local non-monotonicity under visual aliasing in POMDPs (distinct states with different true progress collapse to one observation, averaging the estimate — TimeRewarder 2025 analysis). Used inside PBRS these produce slowdown, not inversion; used as raw reward they are hackable.

* LIV — Language-Image Value learning [Ma et al., ICML 2023 (arXiv 2306.00958)]
  core_idea: Unified vision-language value/representation objective (dual-RL + mutual-information contrastive connection) pretrained on text-annotated video (EpicKitchens); encodes a universal value function for tasks specified by language OR goal image; supports in-domain fine-tuning with the same objective.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Outperforms prior pretrained representations for imitation and reward specification on simulated + real robot environments. Authors explicitly concede zero-shot rewards do NOT always work on unseen robot domains/tasks due to language grounding and visual domain gaps — fine-tuning on in-domain data is the recommended fix.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs a language or image goal; optionally in-domain video for fine-tuning. No actions or stage labels.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Same PBRS-potential use case as VIP with a language interface; the honest OOD admission is the key datum — BiGym head-cam images are far from EpicKitchens, so zero-shot Phi is likely noisy; fine-tuning LIV on the ~60 demos + online video is cheap and would give an in-domain progress potential. Still only addresses F2, not F1.

* Time-contrastive networks and frame-wise temporal-distance rewards [TCN: Sermanet et al., ICRA 2018; modern successors: Progressor (arXiv 2411.17764, 2024), TimeRewarder (arXiv 2509.26627, 2025)]
  core_idea: TCN: triplet loss pulls temporally-near frames together across viewpoints; reward = negative embedding distance between agent frame and time-aligned demo frame, optimized with RL (PILQR) for real-robot imitation. TimeRewarder: learns pairwise frame temporal distance from passive video as a dense per-step proxy reward; Progressor adds self-supervised online refinement to push back against off-distribution overestimation.
  sparse_reward_evidence: TCN: real-robot pouring/pose imitation from human video. TimeRewarder reports near-complete success on 9/10 Meta-World sparse tasks, outperforming sparse-reward-only RL and prior video-reward baselines with modest environment steps. Single-frame embeddings cannot encode velocity — known TCN limitation — and temporal-distance models violate monotonicity locally under visual aliasing.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs demo videos; TCN additionally needs approximate time alignment between rollout and demo (a strong assumption their fixed-horizon BiGym episodes partially satisfy). Progress = temporal position in demo; no explicit state estimation.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The purest 'progress along the demo' signal: with fixed-ish episode lengths (300-540 steps), demo-frame index is a usable Phi and needs no pretraining corpus. Caveat directly relevant to their post-success-tail memory note: 96% of demo targets clip to the top atom, so late-demo frames are progress-flat — a time-index potential must be computed on the pre-success segment or it injects a flat/misleading tail. Alias-induced non-monotonicity argues for concatenating proprioception into the embedding rather than pixels alone.

* T-REX / D-REX — ranking-based reward extrapolation [T-REX: Brown et al., ICML 2019 (arXiv 1904.06387); D-REX: Brown et al., CoRL 2019 (arXiv 1907.03976); uncertainty-aware follow-up Bayesian REX, ICML 2020]
  core_idea: Learn a state-based reward net from RANKED trajectories via Bradley-Terry; the reward extrapolates beyond the best demonstration. D-REX removes the human-ranking requirement by noise-injecting a BC policy to auto-generate rankings (more noise = worse trajectory).
  sparse_reward_evidence: Atari + MuJoCo: T-REX often exceeds 2x the best demonstration's return and beats GAIL/BCO from suboptimal demos. Not demonstrated on sparse robot manipulation at BiGym difficulty; learned reward is stationary once trained and Brown et al. acknowledge RL optimization can exploit its OOD errors (motivating Bayesian REX's reward-uncertainty posteriors).
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs trajectory rankings — free in this project: online rollouts are auto-ranked by success/failure/steps-to-success, and demo > success > failure gives a 3-tier ranking without human labels; D-REX's noise-injection trick maps to their existing exploration-dose rollouts. Learns a reward net; no explicit progress state.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Attractive because it converts their poisoned data mix (F3) into supervision — failures become negative ranking evidence rather than pure erosion. But a frozen learned reward has exactly the OOD fragility that produced their 9x inverted judge preferences: the reward net's extrapolation off-demo is unconstrained, and bootstrap-max will seek its errors. If used, needs ensemble/uncertainty penalties (Bayesian REX-style) or a support mask. Ranking rewards do NOT preserve the optimal policy — they replace the objective.

* Rank2Reward — progress ranking gated by support discriminator [Yang et al., ICRA 2024 (arXiv 2404.14735)]
  core_idea: From passive demo video only: learn a per-frame utility by Bradley-Terry ranking of temporal frame order (later demo frame = higher utility = progress), then multiply this progress reward by a GAIL-style discriminator estimate of being IN the demo state distribution. Explicitly designed so the policy 'cannot exploit the learned reward function' outside demo support — off-support states get low reward regardless of predicted progress.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Beats GAIL/AIRL/VICE/TCN/ROT/SOIL baselines on several tabletop manipulation tasks in simulation and on a real xArm, learning from raw video with no task reward beyond the learned one.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs demo videos (no actions); learns two nets: frame-order ranker (progress) + support discriminator, both trained online-free from demos plus agent rollouts for the discriminator. No stage labels.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The closest published match to their F1 mechanism: it replaces optimistic smoothing at off-demo states with an explicit multiplicative pessimism mask, which is precisely the anti-inversion pressure their judge experiments call for. In Q-learning terms the mask acts as a learned negative shaping that counters bootstrap-max optimism at unexecuted/OOD states. Caveats: composite reward is non-stationary while the discriminator trains (target-recompute-at-sample-time needed, which their replay already does for other quantities); no policy-invariance guarantee; demo-support masking could fight legitimate off-demo recovery behavior — exactly the tension their fence/recovery-teach arms probe.

* DrS — Dense reward learning from Stages (success-vs-failure discriminators) [Mu et al., ICLR 2024 (arXiv 2404.16779), OpenReview 6CZ50WgfCG]
  core_idea: Reward R(s') = k + alpha*tanh(D_k(s')) with stage index k and alpha=1/3 (any alpha<1/2 keeps stage ordering strictly monotone — a built-in anti-inversion bound). Per-stage discriminators classify SUCCESS vs FAILURE trajectories (positive buffer: trajectories that progressed beyond stage k; negative: stalled at stage k), assigned by highest stage reached, trained off-policy. Crucially NOT agent-vs-demo: a GAIL discriminator converges to 0.5 and loses signal, whereas the success/failure gap 'remains intact and does not shrink'. Demos optional — only a sparse/stage reward is required; demos just seed the success buffer.
  sparse_reward_evidence: ManiSkill: three task families, 1000+ task variants. Semi-sparse rewards alone yield limited success in budget; DrS substantially outperforms semi-sparse and reaches performance comparable to human-engineered dense rewards on Pick-and-Place and Turn Faucet; learned rewards transfer to unseen tasks in the family.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs stage indicator functions (semi-sparse supervision) for the multi-stage version; the one-stage version needs only the 0/1 success signal they already have. Needs online failures — abundant in their 300-episode runs.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Strongest overall fit: (a) it consumes their failure episodes as first-class negative data, converting the F3-poisoned mix into signal; (b) the bounded tanh band is a published mechanical answer to F1-style inversion — the dense term can locally mis-rank but can never overturn the coarse stage/success ordering; (c) BiGym 0/1 reward supports the 1-stage version immediately, and many BiGym tasks have natural 2-3 stage decompositions (reach/grasp/place) if they add contact-based indicators; (d) reward is bounded in [0, K+1/3-ish], compatible with a fixed C51 support after rescale. Non-stationary while discriminators train; no policy-invariance guarantee, but the success-vs-failure objective aligns its fixed point with the true task, unlike style discriminators.

* GAIL-family discriminator shaping and its reward-bias theory [GAIL: Ho & Ermon, NeurIPS 2016; AIRL: Fu et al., ICLR 2018; DAC: Kostrikov et al., ICLR 2019 (Discriminator-Actor-Critic); reward-shape study: arXiv 2104.06687; neutral-reward fix: arXiv 2009.09467]
  core_idea: Train discriminator D between demo and agent transitions; use -log(1-D) (positive) or log(D) (negative) or AIRL's logD-log(1-D) (neutral) as dense reward for RL. DAC proves the positive form biases toward survival/looping (never terminate, farm reward) and the negative form biases toward early termination, because absorbing states implicitly get reward 0; fix = explicit absorbing-state transitions with learned reward plus off-policy training.
  sparse_reward_evidence: GAIL matches experts on MuJoCo locomotion from few demos; DAC extends to off-policy with large sample-efficiency gains. On sparse manipulation, plain GAIL is a weak baseline in DrS/Rank2Reward comparisons (discriminator saturates, reward loses signal as policy approaches demos); GAIL rewards do not incorporate the task's sparse success signal unless added.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Only a discriminator over (s), (s,a) or (s,s'); no progress or stage estimation. Needs continual online agent data (non-stationary reward).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The reward-sign theory is the transferable asset: in their bootstrap-max critic, a strictly-positive dense term is optimism fuel (survival-bias analog of F1), so any adopted dense term should be zero-centered or bounded-negative off-support (AIRL neutral form / Rank2Reward mask / DrS tanh). Absorbing-state handling matters for their replay's episode-end bootstrap. Plain agent-vs-demo GAIL is a poor fit: it saturates precisely when the policy imitates well (their current regime), and its fixed point is imitation, not task success — it cannot make success SCALE with online steps.

* AMP-style additive style reward (discriminator as auxiliary, not sole, reward) [Peng et al., SIGGRAPH / ACM TOG 2021 (arXiv 2104.02180)]
  core_idea: Total reward = w_task * sparse/simple task reward + w_style * discriminator reward over state transitions from unstructured motion data; least-squares discriminator + gradient penalty for stability. Task term defines WHAT, discriminator defines HOW.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Character control (locomotion, obstacle courses, dribbling) with very simple task rewards — style reward carries most of the shaping burden; matches or beats hand-crafted tracking controllers. Widely replicated on legged robots. Known failure: style term dominating and suppressing task completion; weight scheduling and discriminator regularization required. Not a manipulation-native result.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: No progress estimation; discriminator on (s,s') pairs. Needs a corpus of reference behavior (their demos).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The direct 'reward-space BC hinge': moves imitation pressure from a policy-space loss (their current hinge, invisible to the critic) into the value function, where TD can trade imitation against task success and, in principle, let task reward override imitation as online successes accumulate — the scaling property they want. Risk profile mirrors their hinge-removal collapse: if w_style decays before genuine value signal forms, same 5k-step collapse; if it doesn't decay, gains stay imitation-bound. Positive-only style rewards feed bootstrap-max optimism (see DAC), so the bounded/neutral variants are mandatory.

* ROT and SQIL — BC-hinge removal blueprints (imitation via decayed regularization or via reward labels) [ROT: Haldar et al., CoRL 2022 Oral (arXiv 2206.15469); SQIL: Reddy, Dragan & Levine, ICLR 2020 (arXiv 1905.11108)]
  core_idea: ROT: dense reward = optimal-transport distance between agent and demo trajectory embeddings, PLUS a BC regularizer whose weight is adaptively gated by soft Q-filtering — trust BC only where the BC action's Q beats the policy's — so imitation pressure decays automatically as the critic becomes competent. SQIL: no discriminator, no shaping — label all demo transitions reward=1 and all agent transitions reward=0, balanced 50/50 replay, run standard (soft) Q-learning; imitation emerges purely from value propagation.
  sparse_reward_evidence: ROT: 20 sim tasks (DMC, OpenAI Robotics, Meta-World), reaches 90% of expert 7.8x faster than the strongest baseline; real robot: 14 tasks, 90.1% average success from a single demo + 1 hour training; ablations show pure OT reward WITHOUT the BC phase fails early — cold-start collapse analogous to their 5k-step hinge-removal collapse. SQIL: matches/beats GAIL on image-based Car Racing, Lunar Lander, MuJoCo; pure imitation objective, plateaus at demonstrator level.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: ROT: needs a trajectory encoder (trained in-domain) for OT matching; no stage/progress labels. SQIL: needs nothing at all beyond demo/agent provenance bits in replay.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: These are the two published answers to 'how to remove the BC hinge without collapse'. ROT's soft Q-filtered decay is a principled replacement for their hinge: keep BC early, hand control to the critic per-state exactly when Q(a_BC) < Q(a_pi), rather than a global schedule — directly testable in CQN-AS. SQIL is natively critic-only and composes with the real sparse reward (demo transitions get 1, agent transitions get env reward instead of 0 — a standard variant), effectively formalizing the self-imitation-of-successes dynamic they already observe while deleting the policy-space hinge; its known ceiling (imitation fixed point, no extrapolation past demos) means it fixes the collapse but not by itself the scaling goal. Neither fixes F1's sibling-bin optimism; both are compatible with adding a DrS/Rank2Reward-style off-support penalty.
==============================
Offline-to-online (O2O) value calibration literature maps remarkably cleanly onto all three established CQN-AS failure mechanisms, and yields one critical discrimination: the project's negative MC-lower-bound result (Q-Transformer-style target clamping) does NOT falsify calibration approaches, because Cal-QL's mechanism is different — it MASKS the conservative push-down (stop pessimizing a bin once its Q falls to the demo reference value V^ref(s)=MC return-to-go) rather than clamping TD targets, so it caps sibling-bin optimism from above without compressing the negative half of the value range. Cross-cutting findings: (a) "remove the BC hinge -> critic collapses in 5k steps" is a named, studied phenomenon — WSRL (ICLR 2025) shows the sudden Q-divergence at the offline->online seam is a transient recalibration problem caused by distribution mismatch, fixable by a warmup phase (seed buffer with rollouts from the still-constrained policy, recalibrate at high UTD, then drop the constraint), not a permanent property of the critic; (b) the discrete 3x5-bin-per-token structure makes the in-sample family (IQL expectile, In-Sample Softmax) EXACT rather than approximate — support masks over 5 bins per level are enumerable from replay counts, so the bootstrap max can be restricted to executed bins, killing failure mechanism 1 (optimism injection into unexecuted siblings) at the source instead of racing it (failure 2); a BC head can serve as the support mask (inference-time constraint) instead of a gradient hinge (training-time constraint), which is removable without a seam because targets never depended on unexecuted bins; (c) failure 3 (exploration dose taxes rollout success -10~-29pp, poisoning the data mix) is exactly what JSRL's rollin curriculum solves — confine exploration to an annealed tail after a guide-policy rollin, which simultaneously shortens the effective TD chain (failure 2) from 300-540 steps to the explored tail; (d) the erosion-rate-vs-backup-rate race (failure 2) has two documented levers: UTD/update-frequency increase (SO2, RLPD, WSRL warmup) and removing target-network staleness (NTK-regularized target-free critic in the closest experimental match — 50 demos, sparse 0/1 image-based grasping, >90% in 2h); (e) constraint-anchoring has a documented spectrum: fixed anchor to demos (AWAC — no collapse but plateaus, i.e. gains stop scaling with online steps, precisely the project's complaint), self-anchor with annealing (PROTO trust region to previous-iteration policy), feedback-controlled anchor (adaptive BC weight on rollout success), decision-time anchor (PEX frozen-policy expansion, PA-RL candidate reranking — prior art for the project's two-head/rerank arm), and no anchor + architectural bounding (RLPD LayerNorm; note C51's [Vmin,Vmax] already bounds magnitudes, which is why CQN-AS collapse manifests as rank INVERSION not value explosion — so calibration must target ranks/gaps, favoring support-masked decoding and one-sided pushdown over magnitude regularization). A 2025 taxonomy ("The Three Regimes of Offline-to-Online RL", arXiv 2510.01460) frames when to retain the offline objective vs switch. Sources: arXiv 2303.05479, 2006.09359, 2204.02372, 2412.07762, 2302.02948, 2302.00935, 2312.07685, 2309.10150, 2210.13846, 2412.06685, 2305.15669, 2410.14957, 2302.14372, IJCAI 2024 #615, ICLR 2022 IQL (Kostrikov et al.).

* Cal-QL (Calibrated Q-Learning) [Nakamoto, Zhai, Singh, Mark, Ma, Finn, Kumar, Levine — NeurIPS 2023 (arXiv 2303.05479)]
  core_idea: One-line change to CQL: the conservative push-down term on policy/non-data actions is masked by a reference value — pessimism is applied only where Q(s,a) exceeds V^ref(s), the Monte-Carlo return-to-go of the behavior/demo policy (E_pi[max(Q, V^mu)] replaces E_pi[Q]). The learned Q is thus a lower bound on the policy's value AND an upper bound relative to the reference — 'calibrated to a reasonable scale'. This prevents the characteristic dip-and-slow-recovery at online-finetuning onset: naive conservative critics are so underestimated that early policy improvement 'unlearns' the offline policy while Q-values recover scale.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Strongest gains precisely in the sparse regime: AntMaze (sparse 0/1 goal reward), Adroit binary-reward dexterous manipulation from demos + suboptimal data, Franka Kitchen; outperforms SOTA on 9/11 fine-tuning benchmarks.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs per-state reference value V^ref = MC return-to-go of behavior policy. With 0/1 success reward this is gamma^(T_success - t) computed from demo trajectories at replay-write time — no learned estimator. Caveat from project memory: 96% of demo targets clip to the top C51 atom (post-success tail artifact); reference values must be computed from the un-clipped discounted-to-go or they saturate and the mask never activates.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Most direct import. Each CQN-AS level-token is a 5-way discrete action space, so the CQL logsumexp push-down is exact and costs 16 tokens x 3 levels x 5 bins — trivial. Cal-QL's masked version caps unexecuted-sibling-bin optimism at the demo reference value instead of clamping to Vmin, directly attacking failure 1 (inverted preferences from smoothing+bootstrap-max filling) while preserving action gaps — the exact property the project's failed Q-Transformer MC-clamp destroyed. Key discrimination for synthesis: Cal-QL never replaces TD targets with MC; it is a one-sided regularizer mask, so the project's negative MC-bound result does not cover it. For C51, apply the mask on the expected value of the categorical distribution (or as a masked cross-entropy pushing mass downward only for bins whose mean exceeds V^ref).

* IQL online fine-tuning (in-sample expectile backup) [Kostrikov, Nair, Levine — ICLR 2022 (Offline RL with Implicit Q-Learning)]
  core_idea: Never query the critic on actions absent from the dataset: learn V(s) by expectile regression on in-dataset (s,a) Q-values (expectile->1 approximates in-sample max), back up Q against V. Policy extracted by advantage-weighted BC. Fine-tuning continues the same objective on the growing buffer; empirically no seam dip, but improvement is slow — Cal-QL and WSRL both document IQL/AWAC as the 'stable but plateaus' baselines.
  sparse_reward_evidence: SOTA-at-the-time on sparse AntMaze and Adroit in D4RL; the multi-step dynamic programming (vs BC) is what solves AntMaze at all.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None — expectile regression on replay only; no support model, no MC returns.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Replaces the per-level bootstrap max over 5 bins with an expectile over EXECUTED bins only. This removes the erosion channel of failure 1 at the source (unexecuted siblings never enter targets) and changes the failure-2 rate race to trivially winnable (erosion injection rate ~0). Cost: known improvement ceiling — with expectile fixed, the critic tracks the data distribution's good quantile, so success scales with online steps only insofar as the buffer improves; consider annealing expectile upward online. Critic-only compatible: no actor needed since decode is argmax over bins — but the argmax at DECODE time must also be support-aware or off-demo inversion still hijacks rollouts.

* In-Sample Softmax / In-Sample Actor-Critic [Xiao, Wang, Pan, White, White — ICLR 2023 oral (arXiv 2302.14372)]
  core_idea: Diagnoses the exact failure: with insufficient action coverage, the bootstrap max selects unseen actions and bootstrapping from their inaccurate values causes overestimation and divergence. Fix: replace max with an in-sample softmax computed only over actions with behavior support (weighted by behavior density mu(a|s)); as temperature -> 0 it converges to the in-sample max; policy iteration with it provably converges.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Evaluated on D4RL including sparse AntMaze; consistently matches or beats offline SOTA. (Offline-focused; the O2O transfer of the idea is via IQL-family behavior.)
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs behavior support/density over actions. In the discrete 5-bin case this is exactly a BC head over bins (already trained for the hinge) or replay visit counts — used as a MASK in the target, not as a loss.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The discrete-native mechanism the continuous papers approximate. For 5^K structured actions: compute the per-level target max only over bins the behavior model puts mass on (threshold mu(b|s) > eps, or count >= n). This repurposes the existing BC hinge machinery from a gradient constraint into a target-support mask — removable without a seam because the critic never learned to depend on unexecuted bins. Combines naturally with the project's 'restricted decode' resurrection probe (support-masked argmax at rollout), which is the decode-time half of the same idea.

* AWAC (Advantage-Weighted Actor-Critic) [Nair, Gupta, Dalal, Levine — arXiv 2006.09359, 2020]
  core_idea: Off-policy TD critic on demos+online replay; actor update is advantage-weighted maximum likelihood on dataset actions (implicit KL constraint to the data distribution, no explicit BC term, no density model). Designed exactly for the demos-then-online regime; avoids offline over-conservatism but the constraint stays anchored to the (mixed) data distribution forever.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Adroit dexterous manipulation from human demos (binary-ish sparse), real robot tasks; but Cal-QL's comparisons show AWAC plateaus during extended online training.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Critic-only analog: bootstrap with an advantage-weighted softmax over bins actually present in replay instead of max. Main value to the synthesis is as a documented cautionary datapoint: a fixed anchor to the data distribution prevents collapse but caps scaling with online steps — the precise property the project rejects ('gains ride on self-imitation'). Any AWAC-flavored fix must be paired with an annealing or calibration mechanism to meet the scaling goal.

* JSRL (Jump-Start RL) [Uchendu et al. — ICML 2023 (arXiv 2204.02372)]
  core_idea: Two policies: a guide (any pre-existing/BC/offline policy) rolls in for the first h steps of each episode, then the exploration policy takes over; h is annealed downward as the exploration policy's success sustains, forming a backward curriculum of start states. Theory: improves sample complexity of non-optimistic exploration from exponential to polynomial in horizon. Meta-algorithm, compatible with any RL learner; no value-function machinery touched.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Simulated robotic manipulation (vision-based grasping etc.) in the small-data regime; strongest wins where reward is sparse and horizon long — exactly the exponential-to-polynomial case.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Only a scalar curriculum controller on h (success-rate thresholds); no state novelty/progress estimator.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Attacks failure 3 head-on: instead of dosing exploration noise uniformly across the episode (measured -10~-29pp rollout success, poisoning the data mix), confine exploration to an annealed tail after a guide rollin — rollout success stays high (healthy data mix, self-imitation channel intact) while on-policy negatives concentrate at the frontier where the critic needs them. Simultaneously attacks failure 2: TD backups only need to propagate across the explored tail, not 300-540 env steps (~20-34 chunk backups). Guide = current BC-hinged CQN-AS snapshot; zero critic modifications; fully policy-agnostic. Cheapest orthogonal arm to run alongside any critic fix.

* WSRL (Warm-Start RL: fine-tuning without retained offline data) [Zhou, Peng, Li, Levine, Kumar — ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2412.07762)]
  core_idea: Empirical finding: continuing to train on offline data during fine-tuning matters mostly to prevent a SUDDEN Q-divergence at fine-tuning onset caused by offline/online distribution mismatch, which otherwise triggers unlearning of the pretrained policy. WSRL therefore runs a warmup phase — a small number of rollouts from the frozen pretrained policy seed the online buffer and 'recalibrate' the Q-function to the online distribution at high update-to-data ratio — then does pure online RL with NO offline data and no constraint, learning faster than all retain-data baselines.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Evaluated on D4RL AntMaze (sparse), Adroit, and Franka Kitchen fine-tuning suites against Cal-QL/IQL/RLPD-class baselines.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None — just a staged schedule (warmup episode count, UTD setting).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Reframes the project's core observation: 'removing the BC hinge collapses the critic within 5k steps' is the documented seam-divergence transient, not proof the hinge is permanently load-bearing. Prescription: never remove the hinge cold — (1) freeze hinge, (2) collect warmup rollouts from the hinged policy (on-policy successes, matching online state distribution), (3) recalibrate the critic on that warmup data at elevated UTD, (4) drop the hinge. Testable prediction: post-warmup, the no-hinge critic should not collapse, and success should scale with online steps — the project's stated goal. Directly critic-only compatible; no architectural change.

* RLPD (online RL with offline data, no pretraining) [Ball, Smith, Kostrikov, Levine — ICML 2023 (arXiv 2302.02948)]
  core_idea: Skip offline pretraining and constraints entirely: symmetric 50/50 sampling between demo buffer and online buffer every batch, LayerNorm in the critic to prevent catastrophic value over-extrapolation on out-of-distribution actions (bounds the critic's extrapolation in feature space), ensemble of critics + high UTD. 2.5x improvement over prior demo-driven online RL, SOTA on sparse-reward benchmarks.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Sparse-reward Adroit, sparse AntMaze, and vision-based robot tasks; explicitly a demos+sparse-reward-online recipe.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Two imports. (1) LayerNorm-as-calibration: C51 already bounds Q to [Vmin,Vmax], which is WHY CQN-AS collapse manifests as rank inversion rather than value explosion — but LayerNorm on the shared torso still curbs feature-space extrapolation that fills unexecuted sibling bins with confident garbage; cheap ablation. (2) The 50/50 symmetric demo sampling is the minimal no-BC anchor — the project already oversamples demos, but RLPD's evidence says the demo STREAM in the batch (not a demo LOSS) plus high UTD may be sufficient anchoring; high UTD also attacks the failure-2 backup-rate race directly.

* PEX (Policy Expansion) [Zhang, Xu, Yu — ICLR 2023 (arXiv 2302.00935)]
  core_idea: Freeze the offline-learned policy as one member of a policy set; learn a new online policy as an 'expansion'; at rollout, both propose actions and one is selected adaptively via Boltzmann over their Q-values under the current critic. Ablation shows freezing the offline member is load-bearing — unfreezing causes clear drops. Unlearning of offline competence is structurally impossible.
  sparse_reward_evidence: D4RL including sparse AntMaze fine-tuning; outperforms direct offline-to-online baselines.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Citable prior art for the project's existing two-head + decision-time rerank arm (R6+R4): convert BC from a training-time gradient hinge into a decision-time proposal — frozen hinged snapshot proposes an action chunk, unconstrained critic/decode proposes another, critic scores both. Critic trains free of BC while demo competence is preserved structurally. Key caveat mapped to established failure 1: the selector trusts the critic's RANKING at off-demo states, exactly where inversion lives (judge prefers hijacked garbage 9x) — so the selection must use pessimistic/ensemble/support-masked scoring, not the raw optimistic Q, or the hijacked head wins the vote.

* SO2 (perturbed value updates + high update frequency) [Zhang, Liu, Li, Niu, Yang, Liu, Ouyang — AAAI 2024 (arXiv 2312.07685)]
  core_idea: Diagnoses O2O failure as a Q-value ESTIMATION problem: after offline pretraining, Q estimates are biased with sharp spurious peaks and inaccurate action RANKING, which misleads early online updates (CQL and TD3+BC both fail online for this reason). Fix: (1) perturbed value updates — smooth targets by perturbing the target action, flattening spurious sharp peaks so the policy can't exploit them early; (2) increased Q-update frequency to burn down inherited estimation bias faster. Up to +83.1% over SOTA.
  sparse_reward_evidence: MuJoCo and Adroit (Adroit sparse-ish dexterous tasks); primary evidence is dense-reward locomotion — weakest sparse evidence in this list.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Its diagnosis — inaccurate RANK of Q, sharp spurious peaks — is literally the project's measured inversion signature. Discrete analog of perturbed value updates: exploit the ordinal structure of the 5 bins per level and smooth targets across ADJACENT bins (the hijacked-garbage peaks are non-adjacent to demo bins by construction of the coarse-to-fine tree). Update-frequency finding independently supports raising UTD to win the failure-2 rate race (~20-34 backups/episode is the measured deficit).

* Q-Transformer (contrast/negative-control entry) [Chebotar et al. — CoRL 2023 (arXiv 2309.10150)]
  core_idea: Per-dimension autoregressive discretization of actions (each dimension a Bellman step); conservative regularizer pushes UNSEEN action bins toward the minimal value; plus MC-return lower bound on TD targets (max(target, MC)). Scales offline TD learning to large multi-task robot datasets with sparse binary rewards.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Real-robot multi-task manipulation with sparse binary success rewards, large offline datasets — the founding evidence that discretized-bin conservative Q-learning works at scale on sparse reward.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: MC returns from trajectories (trivial for 0/1 reward).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Already tested by the project (MC lower bound: no gain; hypothesis = clamps the negative half of the value range, compressing action gaps). Critical for the synthesis as the discrimination anchor: Q-Transformer's sibling-bin treatment is an UNMASKED push-to-Vmin plus target clamping; Cal-QL (masked one-sided pushdown, regularizer not target) and In-Sample Softmax (support-masked bootstrap) are the calibrated refinements of the same intuition that the project's negative result does NOT rule out. Its per-dimension autoregressive Bellman decomposition is also the structural sibling of CQN-AS's per-token/per-level decomposition, so its regularizer slots in at identical granularity.

* Adaptive BC regularization (feedback-controlled hinge weight) [Zhao, Boney, Ilin, Kannala, Pajarinen — ESANN 2022 (arXiv 2210.13846; earlier NeurIPS 2021 offline-RL workshop version)]
  core_idea: Keep a TD3+BC-style cloning term during online fine-tuning but make its weight a feedback controller: adaptively weighted by the agent's online return and training stability — decays while performance sustains, re-tightens on degradation. Combined with a randomized Q-ensemble to allow many updates per step. SOTA O2O on D4RL at the time.
  sparse_reward_evidence: D4RL benchmark (includes sparse AntMaze in the suite); modest but direct.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: Needs only the rollout success-rate signal already logged by the project.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: The lowest-cost arm in this whole survey: keep the exact CQN-AS hinge, replace the fixed lambda with a controller on rollout success (decay on sustained success, auto-re-tighten on collapse onset). Converts the binary 'hinge required vs collapse' finding into a measurable annealing frontier, and its failure mode is graceful (controller re-tightens instead of run dying). Pairs naturally with the WSRL staged-removal protocol — the controller IS the staging.

* PA-RL (Policy-Agnostic RL: critic-driven action optimization + distillation) [Mark, Gao, et al. — ICLR 2025 (arXiv 2412.06685)]
  core_idea: Universal policy-improvement step decoupled from policy class: sample multiple candidate actions from the base policy, run global optimization (rerank candidates by Q) and local optimization (gradient steps on actions to ascend Q), then distill the optimized actions back into the base policy by supervised learning. Works with diffusion/transformer/autoregressive-token policies; used WITH Cal-QL as the critic for online fine-tuning; fine-tuned OpenVLA (7B) on a real robot from 40% to 70% success in 40 minutes.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Real-robot fine-tuning with success-based reward (OpenVLA 40->70% in 40 min); simulation suites vs offline RL and fine-tuning baselines with up to 2x sample-efficiency gains.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None beyond the Cal-QL reference values if that critic is used.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Validates the candidate-restricted-argmax pattern at scale: never take a free argmax over 5^K structured actions; decode the critic only over candidate chunks proposed by a demo-anchored generator (BC head samples, demo-retrieval neighbors, or the frozen hinged snapshot). Hijacked off-support bins can then never be selected regardless of their inflated Q — failure 1 becomes decode-time-inert while the critic heals. Also the strongest evidence that a Cal-QL-calibrated critic + reranking is the current best-practice recipe for demo-driven online improvement.

* PROTO (iterative trust-region policy regularization) [Li, Hu, Xu, Liu, Zhan, Zhang — arXiv 2305.15669, 2023]
  core_idea: Augment the standard RL objective with an ITERATIVELY EVOLVING KL regularizer to the previous-iteration policy (trust region to self, not to demos), with strength annealed over fine-tuning. Early: constraint approximately anchors to the offline policy (stability); late: relaxed for optimal final performance. Few-line change; bridges any offline pretraining to any off-policy fine-tuner.
  sparse_reward_evidence: D4RL fine-tuning suites including sparse AntMaze; stable initial fine-tuning plus strong final performance reported across diverse method pairings.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Replaces anchor-to-demos with anchor-to-self: for a critic-only method, constrain the KL between successive per-level softmax policies over the 5 bins (a policy-churn limiter). This bounds the RATE at which hijacked bins can enter rollouts — a direct throttle on the failure-2 erosion-injection rate — while permitting unlimited cumulative drift beyond the demos, which is exactly the 'success scales with online steps' requirement that a fixed BC hinge violates. Trivially implementable as a penalty between current and EMA/target critic softmaxes.

* O2O RL for image-based grasping with scarce demonstrations (closest regime match) [Chan, Leung, Bergstra — arXiv 2410.14957; CoRL 2024 Workshop on Mastering Robot Manipulation in a World of Abundant Data]
  core_idea: Real-life image-based vacuum grasping: 50 human demos + offline-to-online RL reaches >90% success in under 2 hours of robot interaction where BC suffers distribution shift. Technical core: replace the target network in off-policy actor-critic with a neural-tangent-kernel-inspired regularization of the critic, removing target-staleness while keeping updates stable.
  sparse_reward_evidence: The entire setting is sparse binary grasp success with ~50 demos — the closest published match to the project's regime (60 demos, 0/1 BiGym reward, ~100k online steps).
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None.
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Existence proof for the project's goal statement: demo-driven, sparse-0/1, scarce-demo O2O RL that genuinely improves with online interaction without riding on BC. The target-network-free NTK regularization is aimed at the same bottleneck as the project's failure 2 — target-network staleness multiplies the effective TD-chain latency (backups per episode), and removing it accelerates value propagation; worth testing given the measured ~20-34 chunk-level backups per 300-540-step episode.

* ENOTO (ensemble pessimism-to-optimism bridge) [IJCAI 2024, pp. 5563-5571 (arXiv 2306.06871)]
  core_idea: Use an enlarged Q-ensemble as the bridge between offline and online phases: pessimistic (min/LCB) ensemble aggregation during and immediately after offline training, then progressively loosen pessimism and add ensemble-based (disagreement-driven) exploration online. Improves stability, efficiency, and final performance over O2O baselines on locomotion and navigation without degradation at the seam.
  sparse_reward_evidence: Navigation tasks in its suite (AntMaze-style sparse); primary evidence locomotion — moderate sparse evidence.
  needs_progress_or_state_estimation: None (ensemble disagreement is self-contained).
  relevance_to_critic_only_demo_rl: Ensemble disagreement is a per-bin 'this value was never trained by TD' detector — precisely the missing flag behind failure 1 (smoothing-filled sibling bins are indistinguishable from learned values in a single critic). Aggregate per-bin C51 heads with an LCB (mean minus beta x std across ensemble members): unexecuted siblings carry high disagreement, get discounted, and preferences un-invert without any BC term; anneal beta down as bins gain visitation. Complementary to support masks (soft, uncertainty-weighted rather than hard count thresholds), at the cost of ensemble compute.
