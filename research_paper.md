### 1.1 Critic-only / value-based RL——target 与 loss 质量

#### Stop Regressing: Training Value Functions via Classification for Scalable Deep RL (HL-Gauss) — Farebrother et al., 2024 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03950>
- **方法**：Keeps a scalar value estimate but trains it by classification: the scalar TD/MC target T is treated as the mean of a Gaussian with std sigma, and each of m bins receives the Gaussian CDF mass falling inside it (histogram loss); the critic's m-way softmax is trained with cross-entropy against this smoothed target, and the scalar Q is decoded as the expectation. Recommended sigma/bin-width = 0.75 (~6 bins receive mass). This differs from Two-Hot (mass on exactly the 2 straddling bins, no smoothing) and from C51 (which models the return DISTRIBUTION and projects the distributional Bellman target; HL-Gauss first computes the scalar target, then smears it). Ablations: HL-Gauss beats MSE, Two-Hot, and even C51 across Atari, chess distillation (+70%), robotic manipulation (+67%); optimal sigma is independent of bin count (it exploits ordinal structure, not just label smoothing); a categorical head with MSE gives nothing — the cross-entropy loss itself is the driver; CE 'retains higher plasticity under non-stationary targets' and degrades more gracefully with noisy rewards.
- **为何有效**：Cross-entropy gives scale-free, bounded-gradient learning with an ordinal soft label, which is robust to target noise and non-stationarity and yields more expressive representations (verified by linear probing) — the pathologies of bootstrapped RL specifically. Because the scalar target is computed BEFORE binning, there is no distributional projection step and no requirement that the full return distribution fit the support, only the scalar expectation.
- **可借鉴**: Directly upgrades our critic loss and simplifies the working no-BC recipe (finding 4). Replace the C51 projection with HL-Gauss on our already-scalar targets: executed bin gets HL-Gauss(max(MC, Bellman)); every counterfactual sibling bin gets HL-Gauss(task-minimum-return floor) — the hand-built 'floor distribution' becomes a principled one-liner. This removes the support-saturation trap (failure mode 3: a scalar target near Vmax just smears over the top ~6 bins instead of the whole distribution clipping) and the plasticity/noise findings target exactly the online phase where targets are non-stationary as the policy leaves the demo manifold (context for failure mode 2). Lowest-risk, highest-confidence transplant in this category.

#### Munchausen Reinforcement Learning — Vieillard et al., 2020 (NeurIPS) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14430>
- **方法**：One-line change to the DQN target: q_target = r_t + alpha*tau*ln pi(a_t|s_t) + gamma * E_{a'~pi}[q(s_{t+1},a') - tau*ln pi(a'|s_{t+1})], where pi = softmax(q_target_net/tau); i.e. add a scaled log-probability of the EXECUTED action to the reward, and use a soft (entropy-regularized) next-state backup. The log-policy term is clipped to [l0, 0] (defaults alpha=0.9, tau=0.03, l0=-1) for numerical safety. No actor exists — pi is derived from the Q-net itself. Theory: this performs implicit KL regularization between successive policies, and as tau→0 it converges to Advantage Learning; the action gap is provably multiplied by (1+alpha)/(1-alpha), and error accumulation averages ((1-gamma)^-1 bound) instead of compounding ((1-gamma)^-2). M-DQN beats C51 on Atari without distributional RL; M-IQN beats Rainbow.
- **为何有效**：The log-policy bonus rewards the agent for taking actions its own recent policy considered likely, which is equivalent to KL-penalizing policy churn between iterations — targets stop chasing a moving argmax, and the (1+alpha)/(1-alpha) gap amplification makes the greedy choice robust to approximation error. Crucially the regularizer is derived from the critic's own values, so it stabilizes without injecting any external action-frequency prior.
- **可借鉴**: The most principled replacement for what the DQfD BC-margin loss is actually doing for us. Factored implementation: at each of the ~240 (timestep, dim, level) decisions, define a 5-way softmax over sibling-bin Q's, add alpha*tau*log pi(chosen bin) (clipped per-factor to [l0,0]) to that factor's target, and use the soft backup at s'. This targets failure mode 2 head-on — it is an operator-level margin amplifier ((1+alpha)/(1-alpha)) on exactly the top1-top2 gaps we measured collapsing 0.356→0.066 without BC — while avoiding failure mode 1, because the bonus regularizes toward the critic's own previous policy rather than toward demo-manifold frequency, leaving return ordering intact. On demo transitions it even behaves like a soft self-consistent imitation term with none of the invented 0.89 Q-gap.

#### Increasing the Action Gap: New Operators for Reinforcement Learning — Bellemare et al., 2016 (AAAI) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04860>
- **方法**：Introduces gap-increasing Bellman operators. Advantage Learning (AL): T_AL Q(x,a) = TQ(x,a) - alpha*(V(x) - Q(x,a)) with V(x)=max_b Q(x,b), alpha in [0,1) — the target of any non-greedy executed action is pushed DOWN by alpha times its advantage deficit, while the greedy action's target is unchanged. Persistent AL: T_PAL Q = max{T_AL Q, r + gamma*E[Q(x',a)]} (don't devalue an action below the value of persisting with it). Theorem 1 gives sufficient conditions for any operator to be optimality-preserving while gap-increasing: TQ - alpha*(V-Q) <= T'Q <= TQ. On Atari: AL statistically beats DQN on 37/60 games and loses on 1; measured action gaps visibly widen and value estimates move closer to ground truth (less overestimation bias).
- **为何有效**：The greedy policy only depends on the ORDERING of Q values at each state, and a small top1-top2 gap means any approximation noise flips the argmax. Devaluing suboptimal actions proportionally to their advantage deficit widens exactly that gap without changing which action is optimal, buying robustness of the induced greedy policy to estimation error — and as a side effect reduces bias since suboptimal actions absorb less bootstrapped overestimation.
- **可借鉴**: Our failure mode 2 IS the action-gap problem: composite argmax over ~240 factored 5-way decisions is exponentially sensitive to per-factor margins, and removing BC lets them collapse. Transplant: apply AL per factored decision — when the executed bin at a (timestep, dim, level) slot is not that slot's argmax, subtract alpha*(max-sibling Q - executed-bin Q) from its scalar target before the C51 projection / HL-Gauss binning (with C51 this is a shift of the target support location; with HL-Gauss it's a scalar subtraction, trivially). This replaces the margin-propping function of the DQfD large-margin loss with a mechanism grounded in the critic's own value ordering rather than demo membership, so it widens decision margins (mode 2) without corrupting return ordering (mode 1). Start with small alpha (0.1-0.3); pairs naturally with the Munchausen soft version.

#### Self-Imitation Learning — Oh et al., 2018 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.05635>
- **方法**：Stores past episodes as (s, a, R) with R the observed discounted return, in a replay buffer PRIORITIZED by (R - V(s))_+. Adds two one-sided losses on sampled transitions: L_policy = -log pi(a|s) * (R - V(s))_+ and L_value = 0.5*((R - V(s))_+)^2 — i.e. update only when the realized return EXCEEDED the current value estimate, never push values down toward bad returns. M=4-10 SIL updates per A2C/PPO step. Theoretically shown to be lower-bound soft Q-learning: the updates move Q/V toward a lower bound of the optimal value implied by observed good trajectories. Improves 6/7 hard-exploration Atari games and helps most under delayed/sparse reward; fails when no positive return is ever found, and can lock in suboptimal behavior if exploitation starts too early.
- **为何有效**：Under sparse reward, a successful trajectory is proof that at least return R is achievable from those states; a one-sided loss exploits that certificate without the variance of full MC regression or the pessimism of pure bootstrapping. Prioritizing by exceedance (R - V)_+ concentrates updates exactly where the critic most underestimates achievable return, so good experience propagates fast instead of being averaged away.
- **可借鉴**: This is the theoretical frame for lab finding 4 — our max(MC, Bellman) executed-bin target and positive-return-only floor are precisely 'learn a lower bound of Q* from good experience'. Two concrete additions: (a) prioritized sampling by (R - Q(s,a))_+ over the online buffer, so gradient budget concentrates on successful online trajectories the critic currently undervalues — the exact states where we measured margin decay (mode 2); (b) make the one-sidedness explicit and per-transition in the online phase: apply the MC/dense-floor machinery whenever the realized return exceeds current Q, rather than gating on whole-trajectory positive return. Also a warning label: SIL's known failure (premature exploitation lock-in) is a risk our frozen expert buffer partially mitigates and worth probing.

#### Learning to Play in a Day: Faster Deep RL by Optimality Tightening — He et al., 2017 (ICLR) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01606>
- **方法**：Exploits trajectory structure to bound Q* from BOTH sides. Lower bounds from the future: L_{j,k} = sum_{i=0..k} gamma^i r_{j+i} + gamma^{k+1} max_a Q(s_{j+k+1}, a) for k=1..K, take L_max = max_k. Upper bounds from the PAST: rearranging Q*(s_{j-k-1}, a_{j-k-1}) >= (rewards) + gamma^{k+1} Q*(s_j,a_j) gives U_{j,k} = gamma^{-(k+1)} Q(s_{j-k-1}, a_{j-k-1}) - sum gamma^{i-k-1} r_{j-k-1+i}; take U_min = min_k. The DQN loss gains quadratic penalties lambda*((L_max - Q)_+)^2 + lambda*((Q - U_min)_+)^2 with lambda=4. With 10M frames it matches/beats 200M-frame DQN on 30/49 Atari games (mean normalized 346% vs 241%).
- **为何有效**：One-step Bellman propagates reward information one transition per update; k-step bounds teleport information K steps in both directions along real trajectories without off-policy correction (bounds on Q* hold regardless of the behavior policy). The upper bound is the rarely-used half: it caps how much value a state-action can claim given what its predecessors' values and the intervening rewards permit, actively suppressing value inflation instead of only fighting underestimation.
- **可借鉴**: Two transplants. (a) Strengthen finding 4's lower bound: replace single-MC with max over k-step lower bounds L_{j,k} (our max(MC, Bellman) is the K=infinity vs K=1 special case; intermediate k propagates the 24-28-step sparse success signal faster under our nstep=1). (b) The upper-bound penalty is a new tool against failure mode 1's signature: the BC-era critic INVENTS a 0.89 Q-gap where truth is 0.009 — pure value inflation on demo bins. A ((Q - U_min)_+)^2 penalty (implemented on the scalar expectation of the categorical head, or by clipping the scalar target into [L_max, U_min] before HL-Gauss binning) uses demo-trajectory structure itself to cap demo-bin Q at levels consistent with neighboring values, directly shrinking invented gaps. Being bounds on Q*, they remain valid on our highly off-policy demos (finding 5).

#### A Distributional Perspective on Reinforcement Learning (C51) — Bellemare et al., 2017 (ICML) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06887>
- **方法**：Models the return distribution as a categorical over 51 fixed atoms on support [Vmin,Vmax] (Atari: [-10,10], chosen 'from preliminary experiments'). The distributional Bellman target shifts/scales each atom by r + gamma*z, CLIPS it to the support bounds, then projects mass onto the two neighboring fixed atoms proportionally to distance; loss is cross-entropy between the projected target distribution and the predicted softmax.
- **为何有效**：Cross-entropy on a categorical target is a better-conditioned learning signal than scalar MSE, and modeling the full return distribution preserves multimodality that averaging destroys.
- **可借鉴**: We already run C51; the borrow is the failure-mode analysis. The boundary-clipping pathology is exactly our failure mode 3 (every positive BiGym target clipped to the top atom until q_reward_scale=0.07). Concrete additions: (a) derive Vmax from measured max discounted return-to-go (~24 scaled) with headroom instead of ad-hoc scale tuning, and (b) log boundary-atom mass as a standing saturation probe — mass piling on the top atom is an early-warning signal that temporal value ordering is being destroyed again (e.g. if reward shaping or n-step changes shift target scale).

#### Robust Action Gap Increasing with Clipped Advantage Learning — Zhang, Gan & Tan, 2022 (arXiv:2203.11677) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11677>
- **方法**：Identifies the failure mode of vanilla AL: when the approximate critic's greedy action is WRONG, blindly increasing the gap devalues the truly-optimal action and the error accumulates in the performance bound (their Theorem 1 shows an alpha-weighted sum of mismatch terms). Fix: apply the AL penalty selectively — T_clipAL Q(s,a) = TQ(s,a) - alpha*(V(s)-Q(s,a)) * I[(Q(s,a)-Q_l)/(V(s)-Q_l) >= c], i.e.
- **为何有效**：Gap-increasing is only safe when the critic's ranking is trustworthy; clipping restricts the devaluation to near-top actions, where mis-devaluing a truly-optimal action costs little (it stays competitive), and stops the operator from grinding down mid/low-ranked actions the critic may be mis-ranking.
- **可借鉴**: A safety guard for the AL/Munchausen transplant, tuned to our specific regime: lab finding 1 says our critic's ranking is unreliable (coin-flip sign accuracy), which is exactly the condition under which vanilla AL accumulates error. Concretely: apply the per-factor AL penalty only to sibling bins whose Q is within the top fraction c of that 5-way slot's Q-range. Addresses the interaction of failure modes 1 and 2 — widen margins where the ordering is likely right, do no harm where it is likely wrong. Refinement of the AL borrow rather than a standalone idea.

#### Distributional Reinforcement Learning with Quantile Regression (QR-DQN) — Dabney et al., 2018 (AAAI) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10044>
- **方法**：Transposes C51: instead of fixed atom LOCATIONS with learned probabilities, it fixes N uniform quantile probabilities and LEARNS the atom locations (the quantile values of the return distribution). Trained with the quantile-regression Huber loss against distributional Bellman targets, which minimizes 1-Wasserstein distance without any projection step and — critically — without any [Vmin, Vmax] support hyperparameter: the learned quantiles move wherever the returns are.
- **为何有效**：The support adapts automatically to the return scale of the task and the training phase, so there is no boundary atom for extremal returns to pile up on and no projection information loss; quantile regression is a proper scoring approach for the Wasserstein geometry that matches how distributional Bellman targets shift and scale.
- **可借鉴**: Changes the borrow story for failure mode 3: replacing the C51 head with N learned quantiles per bin (argmax over quantile means) would eliminate support saturation STRUCTURALLY rather than via the tuned q_reward_scale=0.07 — future reward-scale drift (n-step changes, new tasks) could not silently re-clip targets. The counterweight is the HL-Gauss finding that the cross-entropy loss itself drives much of the categorical benefit (plasticity, noise robustness), which QR's Huber regression gives up. Verdict: keep C51/HL-Gauss as primary, hold QR-DQN as the fallback if support management keeps biting across tasks.

#### Start Classifying: Categorical Critics for LLM Reinforcement Learning — Zhou et al., 2026 (arXiv:2608.02181) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02181>
- **方法**：Replaces PPO's scalar MSE value head with a categorical predictor over 101 bins trained by cross-entropy against HL-Gauss-smoothed return targets (each bin's target probability = Gaussian CDF mass in the bin); the scalar value for GAE is decoded as the softmax expectation. Evaluated on sparse/binary-reward LLM tasks (math reasoning, tool use, search) with Qwen backbones: consistent gains over PPO/DAPO (+2.86 avg@256, +9.58 pass@256 on Qwen2.5-Math-7B).
- **为何有效**：Confirms in a fresh, sparse-binary-reward domain that the HL-Gauss cross-entropy mechanism (ordinal soft labels, scale-free gradients) is what drives value-fidelity gains, and shows the benefit is measurable as CALIBRATION — the critic's stated values track realized returns — not just as end-task score.
- **可借鉴**: Secondary evidence for the HL-Gauss transplant under sparse reward, plus one directly liftable idea: adopt Brier score / expected calibration error of the critic's success prediction (does decoded Q track realized discounted return-to-go?) as standing probes in our value-fidelity suite. That gives a scalar tracking metric for failure mode 1 (a calibrated critic cannot simultaneously invent a 0.89 gap over a true 0.009 one) that is cheaper than the sibling-bin return-ordering probes and can be logged every eval.


### 1.2 Value-based RL 在连续/因子化动作空间（含 CQN 家族）

#### Reinforcement Learning with Action Chunking (Q-chunking / QC) — Li, Park & Levine, 2025 (arXiv 2507.07969) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07969>
- **方法**：Trains a SCALAR chunk critic Q(s_t, a_{t:t+h}) with the backup Q(s_t, chunk) ← Σ discounted rewards over h steps + gamma^h·Q(s_{t+h}, next chunk): because the critic conditions on the entire executed chunk, this is an h-step backup with NO off-policy bias (the paper is explicit that naive n-step returns with a single-action critic are biased, but a sequence-conditioned critic makes them exact). A flow-matching behavior model f_xi is trained on the data; the QC variant acts by Best-of-N — sample N chunks from the flow prior, execute argmax Q — which implicitly enforces a KL(pi‖behavior) ≤ log N − (N−1)/N constraint with no policy-gradient step; QC-FQL instead distills a noise-conditioned policy under a 2-Wasserstein constraint to the prior. Protocol: offline pretrain on demos, then online fine-tuning with offline+online data mixed. Beats FQL/RLPD on sparse OGBench (cube-triple/quadruple) and Robomimic.
- **为何有效**：Its gains decompose into exactly three pieces. (a) Unbiased h-step backups propagate sparse reward h× faster than 1-step TD — the dominant fix for slow credit assignment. (b) The behavior prior restricts both exploration and the critic's effective argmax to temporally coherent, on-manifold chunks, so exploration actually completes multi-step maneuvers and the critic is only ever asked to RANK a handful of plausible candidates — a task it can do even when its global value surface is unreliable. (c) The scalar joint critic over the whole chunk removes factored decisions entirely, so there is no per-factor margin to be exponentially sensitive to.
- **可借鉴**: Already ported; the analysis prescribes which pieces carry the load and how to finish the transplant inside CQN-AS. First, our nstep=1 (finding 5) forfeits QC's main gain even though we already pay for a sequence critic — the chunk-conditioned h-step backup (target = h-step reward sum + gamma^h · chunk-critic at t+h) is bias-free for us for the same reason and directly accelerates sparse-reward propagation. Second, Best-of-N over prior-sampled chunks with a joint scalar readout is the principled replacement for composite factored argmax (finding 2): with N≈32 the critic needs only ordinal correctness among on-manifold candidates, which sidesteps finding 1's coin-flip sibling ordering off-manifold, and the log N − (N−1)/N bound gives a tunable, annealable trust region to replace the BC margin's hard anchor. Flag: the flow prior is actor-adjacent but argmax-Q still decides, so it sits inside the allowed 'proposal/prior' carve-out.
- *另见类别*：off-policy targets, multi-step returns, and pessimism

#### Continuous Control with Coarse-to-fine Reinforcement Learning (CQN) — Seo, Uruç & James, 2024 (CoRL) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07787>
- **方法**：Per action dimension, the action interval is discretized into B=5 bins over L=3 levels (L=4, B=3 for real robots); at each level the argmax bin's interval becomes the next level's support and the executed action is the final centroid. Per-level, per-dim Q heads share linear layers (one-hot level index input) and condition on the previous level's full action vector; the critic is a C51 distributional dueling network (51 atoms). Losses: per-head distributional TD with Polyak target network, plus a DQfD-style large-margin BC loss L^{l,n} = max_{a'}[Q(h_t,a') + f_margin(demo_bin, a')] − Q(h_t, demo_bin) applied at every level and dimension on demo samples, weighted equally with the RL loss in this paper. Demos live in a separate buffer sampled 50/50 with online data, and successful online episodes are relabeled into the demo buffer (self-imitation). Exploration is only Gaussian noise sigma=0.01 on the executed continuous action — an ablation shows sigma=0.1 collapses fine-grained success from 77.5% to 50.8% — and rollout actions are selected with the TARGET network, not the online one; optimizer is AdamW with weight decay 0.1, UTD=1.
- **为何有效**：Factoring the argmax over dims and levels replaces a B^L-per-dim joint maximization with L cheap 5-way decisions, keeping value-based RL tractable in continuous spaces, while C51+dueling stabilizes sparse-reward targets. The margin loss plus 50/50 demo sampling makes the demo bin the argmax everywhere demos exist, which is what actually drives the sparse-reward success — the RL term mostly breaks ties near the manifold.
- **可借鉴**: This is the stock spec against which fork deviations should be audited. Two stock details matter for our failure modes: (a) rollout action selection uses the target network — if our fork acts with the online net, that is an undocumented deviation that plausibly worsens the online-state margin decay in finding 2 (target-net argmax changes more slowly, so composite argmax flips less per update); (b) the noise-scale ablation (0.01 vs 0.1) is direct stock evidence for finding 2's exponential margin sensitivity — the original authors already knew fine-level decisions are fragile and kept perturbations tiny.

#### Coarse-to-fine Q-Network with Action Sequence for Data-Efficient Reinforcement Learning (CQN-AS) — Seo & Abbeel, 2024, rev. 2025 (arXiv 2411.12155) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12155>
- **方法**：Extends CQN to a critic over an action SEQUENCE a_{t:t+K} (K=16 on BiGym, 4 on RLBench/HumanoidBench): for each chunk step k and level l, features [h_t, a^{l−1}_{t+k−1}, one-hot step embedding e_k] go through a shared MLP then a GRU, and a shared projection maps each GRU state to per-bin C51 logits (51 atoms, support [−2,2], L=3, B=5). The TD loss sums over ALL (k,l) heads: each head regresses to n-step reward (N=1 on BiGym/RLBench) plus gamma·max over its own bins of the target network's Q at the next state, with the coarser-level greedy sequence pi_K^{l−1} as conditioning — i.e., every one of the ~K·L·dims factored heads gets an independent Bellman backup. Demo handling is CQN's (separate demo buffer, 50/50 sampling, success relabeling) plus the same Hester-style large-margin BC loss; crucially the paper's loss scales on BiGym/RLBench are RL 0.1 vs BC 1.0 (margin value m not stated in the paper). Exploration is Gaussian noise N(0, 0.01) only; execution uses a temporal ensemble, the exp(−0.01·i)-weighted average of overlapping chunk predictions. lr 5e-5, tau 0.02, batch 256.
- **为何有效**：Predicting Q for a whole chunk lets the sparse ~24-step post-success reward burst be captured by heads whose horizon overlaps it, and the paper's own motivation is that return-to-go prediction from action sequences has lower validation loss — the sequence gives the critic near-supervised signal in demo-dense regions. The 10:1 BC:RL weighting means the published system is, by design, a margin-shaped demo-manifold scorer with small RL corrections, which is why it is stable where naive RL fails.
- **可借鉴**: Spec reference rather than transplant, with one decisive fact: stock down-weights the RL loss to 0.1 while keeping BC at 1.0, so our measured finding 1 (Q encodes demo-frequency, not return ordering) is a designed-in property of the original, not a fork bug — which strengthens the case that any no-BC line must REPLACE the pessimism/anchoring the margin provides (as our finding-4 dense floor does) rather than just re-weight losses. Also verify our fork keeps stock's success-episode relabeling into the demo buffer and the temporal ensemble; both matter for reproducing the 64.6% official number.

#### Solving Continuous Control via Q-learning (DecQN) — Seyde et al., 2022 (ICLR 2023) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.12566>
- **方法**：Casts continuous control as cooperative multi-agent Q-learning over action dimensions: a single trunk (500-1024 units) outputs per-dimension 'utility' heads Q^j(s, a^j), and the JOINT value is defined as their mean, Q(s,a) = (1/M)·Σ_j Q^j(s, a^j). One shared TD loss trains all heads jointly: target = 3-step discounted reward + gamma^3 · (1/M)·Σ_j max over bins of target-net Q^j(s', ·), with double Q-learning, prioritized replay (alpha=0.6), and Huber loss; no actor, no dueling, non-distributional by default (C51 variant in appendix). Actions are bang-bang (2 bins) or bang-off-bang (3 bins) per dimension; exploration is plain epsilon-greedy with epsilon=0.1. Matches D4PG/DMPO on DMC from state and is competitive with DrQ-v2 from pixels, including 38-dim Dog, with one hyperparameter set.
- **为何有效**：Two mechanisms defuse factored-argmax fragility. First, extreme coarseness: with 2-3 bins the per-dimension decision boundary is huge, so per-factor value gaps are naturally large and epsilon=0.1 dithering is survivable — the opposite regime from our 240 fine decisions with 0.066 margins. Second, coupling: because the joint Q is the MEAN of utilities and all heads share one TD loss on that mean, gradients coordinate dimensions through the shared state representation (their matrix-game analysis shows decoupled critics coordinate via the state distribution the agent itself shapes), instead of each head independently chasing its own bootstrap.
- **可借鉴**: Replace CQN-AS's ~240 independent per-(dim,level,step) Bellman backups with a DecQN-style coupled objective: define a joint scalar Q_joint = mean over factor heads of the executed-bin utilities and train ONE TD loss whose target is n-step reward + gamma^n · mean-over-factors of per-factor maxes (keep C51 by projecting the shared scalar target). This directly targets finding 2 — margins decay per-head because each head's independent target drifts on off-manifold states, while a shared-mean target ties all factor scales to one return estimate — and their n-step=3 + PER + double-Q stack is a concrete upgrade path for finding 5's nstep=1/single-critic config. Secondary borrow: bin-level epsilon-greedy restricted to the COARSE level only, exploiting that coarse decisions have DecQN-scale robustness.

#### Offline Reinforcement Learning with Implicit Q-Learning (IQL) — Kostrikov, Nair & Levine, 2021 (ICLR 2022) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06169>
- **方法**：Three components, none of which ever evaluates Q at an action absent from the data. (1) A state-value net V(s) is trained by EXPECTILE regression (tau≈0.7; 0.9 on harder tasks) to Q(s,a) over DATASET actions only — the asymmetric squared loss weights positive errors by tau, so V approaches an upper envelope 'best in-support action value' without an explicit max. (2) The Q update is a SARSA-like backup r + gamma·V(s') — the bootstrap never queries the Q-function at any policy-chosen or counterfactual action. (3) A policy is extracted afterwards by advantage-weighted regression, exp(beta·(Q−V))-weighted BC. Together this performs multi-step dynamic programming (unlike one-step methods) while keeping every Q query in-support; SOTA on D4RL at publication, especially AntMaze sparse-reward stitching.
- **为何有效**：OOD overestimation is prevented by construction — the max is replaced by an expectile statistic estimated only at seen actions — rather than by a penalty that must fight the TD objective. So there is no term (like a BC margin or CQL floor) actively writing fictitious value gaps into the critic: in-support actions retain their true relative return ordering, which is exactly the property our margin-dominated critic lost.
- **可借鉴**: An alternative third leg for the no-BC line, replacing both the margin loss and the counterfactual-sibling floor of finding 4: per factor head, train V^{l,k,n}(h) by expectile regression on the Q of the EXECUTED bin only (executed bins are the in-support set in bin space), and bootstrap every head with r + gamma·V(h') so the target never contains a max over sibling bins the data never chose. This attacks finding 1 at its root — no loss term ever asserts anything about unexecuted siblings, so the critic cannot invent the median 0.89 fake gaps — and attacks finding 2 differently from BC: off-manifold bins are simply never dragged up by bootstrapping, rather than pushed down by margins. Risk to flag: with argmax acting still over all bins, unexecuted-bin values are purely generalization-determined, so this likely needs QC-style candidate restriction at act time; expectile tau also gives a tunable pessimism knob for the positive-vs-negative-return asymmetry in our finding-4 recipe.

#### Learning from Suboptimal Data in Continuous Control via Auto-Regressive Soft Q-Network (ARSQ) — Liu et al., 2025 (ICML, PMLR v267) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00288>
- **方法**：A direct CQN successor for learning from SUBOPTIMAL demos: keeps coarse-to-fine discretization (L levels, B bins) but replaces independent per-dimension heads with an AUTOREGRESSIVE factorization — action bins are chosen sequentially, coarse-to-fine across levels and dimension-by-dimension within a level, each choice conditioned on all previously selected dimensions. Two nets: a soft value function V_soft(s) and dimensional soft-advantage heads A^d (shared backbone), with log-sum-exp normalization enforcing their Theorem 4.3 that the SUM of dimensional soft advantages equals the joint soft advantage — so the sequential greedy/soft choice is consistent with maximizing the true joint soft Q. Training combines a soft Q-learning (max-entropy) TD loss with double networks against overestimation, plus a CQN-style large-margin BC loss on demo actions. Beats CQN ~2.2× on D4RL with bottom-30%-quality data (1.62× average) and beats CQN/DrQ-v2+/ACT on RLBench.
- **为何有效**：Their motivating failure is literally our finding 1 mechanism: with per-dimension independence, a critic trained on mixed-quality data votes for the most FREQUENT per-dimension bins, which recombine into a dominant suboptimal mode; conditioning each dimension's choice on the previous ones lets the critic represent 'this combination is good' even when its marginals are rare. The soft (entropy-regularized) backup additionally keeps non-argmax bins at finite, calibrated value gaps — log-sum-exp targets never drive sibling gaps to the degenerate all-or-nothing shape a margin loss carves.
- **可借鉴**: Two separable transplants. (1) Autoregressive within-level dimension conditioning for the CQN-AS head (CQN-AS already conditions level l on the level-(l−1) action vector, but dims WITHIN a level are independent): feed previously-decided dims of the current level into the GRU features h_{t,k}^l — this attacks the frequency-vs-return confusion of finding 1 at the representational level, and is the published, validated fix for exactly the sibling-recombination pathology our probes measured. (2) Swap the hard max in each head's C51 target for a soft (log-sum-exp with temperature) max: soft targets keep gradients flowing to near-argmax siblings and empirically maintain graded sibling gaps, a candidate mitigation for finding 2's margin-decay-to-zero under BC annealing without reintroducing the margin loss. Caveat: ARSQ still keeps the margin BC loss, so it is evidence for the architecture, not for BC-free training.

#### Action Branching Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning (BDQ) — Tavakoli, Pardo & Kormushev, 2018 (AAAI) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08946>
- **方法**：A shared trunk feeds one state-value head V(s) and N per-dimension advantage branches A_d(s, a_d); per-branch Q is aggregated as Q_d = V(s) + (A_d − mean over that branch's sub-actions of A_d), i.e., dueling with per-branch mean-subtracted advantages (naive sum and max-subtraction tested and worse). For targets they compare per-branch independent maxes, a global max across branches, and a single GLOBAL target that averages over branches — y = r + gamma · (1/N)·Σ_d Q̄_d(s', argmax Q_d(s', ·)) — and the averaged global target shared by all branches wins.
- **为何有效**：Per-branch mean-subtraction pins each branch's advantages to a common V(s) scale, and the single branch-averaged target forces every branch to bootstrap toward the same scalar future value — both remove the degrees of freedom by which independent branches drift apart in scale.
- **可借鉴**: Three low-risk retrofits for the CQN-AS head farm: (1) share ONE bootstrapped scalar target — the mean over all (dim,level,step) heads' greedy target values — instead of per-head independent targets (same coupling idea as DecQN, in target space only, so it composes with keeping per-head C51 losses); (2) per-head mean-subtracted advantage normalization inside the dueling heads so sibling-bin gaps are anchored to a shared V, directly shrinking the invented 0.89 Q-gaps of finding 1; (3) rescale the encoder/GRU trunk gradient by 1/(num_heads+1) — with ~240 heads our trunk sees an enormous summed gradient that stock DQN-style implementations never faced, a plausible unmeasured contributor to finding 2's off-manifold margin decay.

#### QT-Opt: Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning for Vision-Based Robotic Manipulation — Kalashnikov et al., 2018 (CoRL) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.10293>
- **方法**：A single scalar Q(s,a) network (1.2M params, RGB input) with NO actor: both acting and the Bellman target's max are computed by the cross-entropy method — sample action candidates from a Gaussian, evaluate Q, refit the Gaussian to the elite fraction, iterate (2 iterations, 64 samples in the paper), take the best. Targets use a clipped-double-Q-style minimum over two lagged target networks evaluated at the CEM-optimized next action, computed asynchronously by a distributed 'Bellman updater'.
- **为何有效**：Because the max in the Bellman target and in acting is a stochastic search over a scalar critic, there is no factored decomposition at all — action dimensions are jointly optimized every time, so inter-dimension dependencies cost nothing and there is no per-factor margin to collapse.
- **可借鉴**: The transplant is the sampled-optimization decision rule (allowed under the constraint: proposals plus argmax-Q deciding): at act/eval time, treat the coarse-to-fine factored argmax output as the CEM initialization, sample perturbed action sequences around it (per-level bin flips or Gaussian in continuous space), score them with a joint scalar readout (sum of chosen-bin Q over factors, or a QC-style scalar chunk head), and execute the best. This converts finding 2's 240 independent knife-edge decisions into one joint ranking over ~64 candidates, where a 0.066 per-factor margin no longer compounds. Also borrow min-over-two target heads as the no-BC optimism control — cheaper than our CQL-flavored floor on states where the floor doesn't apply (non-positive-return trajectories, finding 4).

#### Growing Q-Networks: Solving Continuous Control Tasks with Adaptive Control Resolution — Seyde et al., 2024 (arXiv 2404.04253) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04253>
- **方法**：A DecQN-style decoupled critic (per-dimension utilities, mean decomposition, scaling to 38-dim actions) whose per-dimension discrete action set GROWS during training: start with bang-bang (2 extreme values), then progressively add finer intermediate values as training advances, with the Q-head sized for the final resolution and unavailable finer actions masked out early on. The critic-only training stack is inherited from DecQN (shared TD on the decomposed value).
- **为何有效**：Coarse action sets are the exploration-and-optimization sweet spot for value-based control — few, well-separated options mean large per-decision value gaps, cheap maxes, and state-space coverage from saturated actions — while fine resolution is only needed at convergence for smoothness.
- **可借鉴**: A level curriculum for CQN-AS's existing hierarchy, targeting finding 2: during online (especially no-BC) training, act and explore with coarse levels only (L=1, then 1-2), executing coarse-bin centroids, and unlock level 3 only once coarse top1-top2 margins on online states stabilize above a threshold — the fine levels are precisely where 0.066-scale margins make the 240-factor composite argmax flip, and GQN shows coarse-only control is competent enough to keep collecting success in the meantime. Implementation is nearly free since the levels already exist; it is a schedule plus a margin-monitor gate (we already measure those margins), and composes with the finding-4 dense-floor recipe rather than replacing it.


### 1.3 Demo-driven RL——带显式模仿正则（如何加权/门控/退火）

#### Deep Q-learning from Demonstrations (DQfD) — Hester et al., 2018 (AAAI) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03732>
- **方法**：Total loss J(Q) = J_DQ + λ1·J_n + λ2·J_E + λ3·J_L2 with λ1=1.0 (n-step TD, n=10), λ2=1.0 (supervised margin), λ3=1e-5 (L2). The margin loss is J_E = max_a[Q(s,a) + l(a_E,a)] − Q(s,a_E) with l = 0.8 for a ≠ a_E and 0 otherwise — applied ONLY to demonstration transitions, never to self-generated data (λ2=0 there), and never annealed. 750k gradient steps of demo-only pre-training precede environment interaction. Demo data is permanently retained in a prioritized replay buffer with priority bonuses ε_a=0.001 for agent transitions and ε_d=1.0 for demos, so demos are oversampled by TD-error priority plus a constant demo bonus. Ablations show both the margin loss and the n-step TD loss are individually critical.
- **为何有效**：The margin term is an action classifier on demo states — it prevents the untrained argmax from wandering off-demo during pre-training — but it was never asked to produce calibrated values: the n-step (n=10) TD loss simultaneously grounds Q at demo states in actual observed returns, propagating real reward signal 10 steps per update, and prioritized replay keeps demo gradient pressure alive without inflating Q-gaps further. The results are carried by the ensemble of n-step + prioritized replay + permanent demo data; the margin alone would produce exactly the manifold-membership Q-function we measured.
- **可借鉴**: Two transplants. (1) Diagnostic alignment: CQN-AS copied the margin (ours: λ=1.0, margin=0.1) but NOT the machinery that kept DQfD's values honest — we run nstep=1 (failure mode 5) so demo-state Q has almost no return grounding to compete with the margin's fake gaps (mode 1). Adopt n≈5-10 step returns (with q_reward_scale=0.07 so targets stay inside C51 support, mode 3) as the primary demo-value grounding, computed within the action-chunk so it composes with the sequence critic. (2) Replace part of the margin's job with DQfD's priority machinery: permanent demo buffer + ε_d-style priority bonus, so demo states keep receiving gradient (maintaining decision margins, mode 2) without any term that carves Q-gaps. Also verify our margin is restricted to demo transitions as DQfD's was — applying it more broadly would be an uncontrolled divergence.
- *另见类别*：off-policy targets, multi-step returns, and pessimism

#### Leveraging Demonstrations for Deep Reinforcement Learning on Robotics Problems with Sparse Rewards (DDPGfD) — Vecerik et al., 2017 (arXiv) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.08817>
- **方法**：DDPG with demos loaded into the replay buffer before training and kept forever; NO behavior cloning or margin loss anywhere — demos enter purely through prioritized sampling. Priority p_i = δ_i² + λ3·|∇_a Q(s_i,a_i)|² + ε + ε_D, where ε_D is a constant bonus added only to demo transitions, so the demo:agent sampling ratio is tuned automatically by TD-error-driven prioritization. Critic loss mixes 1-step and n-step return targets; L2 regularization on both actor and critic; 40 gradient updates per environment step for sample efficiency on sparse-reward insertion tasks.
- **为何有效**：Demo adherence emerges from gradient frequency rather than from a loss that manufactures preferences: demo transitions with high TD error get sampled until their values are consistent with observed returns, then their priority naturally decays — a self-annealing demo influence with no schedule. Because no term ever pushes Q(demo action) above siblings except real return evidence, the critic's ordering stays a return ordering.
- **可借鉴**: The cleanest 'delete the margin' template for our no-BC line (finding 4): keep the frozen expert buffer but move from fixed mixing to TD-error prioritized sampling with an ε_D demo bonus, add the n-step mixed target, and raise UTD above 1 (mode 5: we run UTD=1, they used 40 — even UTD 4-8 would multiply demo-state gradient pressure that currently only the margin provides, which is why removing it collapses margins, mode 2). Addresses mode 1 by construction: no mechanism exists to carve fake gaps.

#### A Minimalist Approach to Offline Reinforcement Learning (TD3+BC) — Fujimoto & Gu, 2021 (NeurIPS) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06860>
- **方法**：TD3 with exactly two changes: (1) actor loss becomes π = argmax E[λ·Q(s,π(s)) − (π(s)−a)²], an L2 BC penalty toward dataset actions applied ONLY to the actor — the critic keeps the unmodified TD3 loss; (2) per-dimension state normalization. The BC weight is scale-normalized: λ = α / (batch mean of |Q(s,a)|) with α=2.5 fixed everywhere, so the imitation term's strength is defined relative to current Q magnitude rather than in absolute units. Ablations: performance robust for α∈[2,3]; α=1 over-imitates, α=4 under-constrains; normalization gives modest but consistent gains.
- **为何有效**：Two causal ingredients. First, the BC gradient never touches the critic — Q is trained purely on Bellman targets, so it remains a (biased but return-grounded) value estimate; the imitation pressure lives in the policy improvement step where it can bias action choice without corrupting the value ordering used to judge actions. Second, normalizing λ by mean |Q| keeps the RL:imitation gradient ratio constant as value scale drifts during training, preventing either term from silently dominating.
- **可借鉴**: CQN-AS has no actor, so our margin lands directly on Q — TD3+BC's separation principle says that is the root design error behind mode 1. Concrete transplant: move imitation to a separate demo-bin classification head (cross-entropy on demo transitions) sharing the encoder/trunk but NOT the Q/C51 head. This preserves the representation-shaping and demo-state feature pressure that plausibly maintains decision margins (mode 2) while making it architecturally impossible to carve fake Q-gaps (mode 1). Secondary borrow: if any margin term survives, normalize its weight by batch-mean |top1−top2 Q-gap| (the TD3+BC λ trick transposed to gaps) — our fixed margin=0.1 against a 0.89 induced gap shows the current term is wildly out of scale with true value differences (0.009).

#### Watch and Match: Supercharging Imitation with Regularized Optimal Transport (ROT) — Haldar et al., 2022 (CoRL) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15469>
- **方法**：Online imitation: OT-based trajectory-matching reward r^OT(o_t) = −Σ C_{t,t'}·μ*_{t,t'} (Sinkhorn alignment of behavior vs expert trajectories in a target-network feature space) trains a DDPG-style agent, plus an adaptive BC term on the actor. The actor loss is π = argmax[(1−λ(π))·E[Q(s,a)] − α·λ(π)·E||a^e − π(s^e)||²] with fixed α=0.03. The gate is a soft Q-filter: λ(π) = E_{s~buffer}[1{Q(s, π^BC(s)) > Q(s, π(s))}] — the fraction of sampled states where the pretrained BC policy's action looks better than the current policy's under the CURRENT critic. Clipped double-Q reduces the risk of the gate being corrupted by overestimation. Ablations: the soft Q-filter matches or beats hand-tuned BC-decay schedules with much lower variance, and both fixed weight and no-BC variants are worse.
- **为何有效**：The gate implements 'imitate exactly while the policy is worse than the demonstrator, then let go' — as the critic learns that the policy's own actions match or beat BC actions, λ falls toward 0 automatically, so annealing is an emergent consequence of competence rather than a schedule. Because the comparison is between two concrete candidate actions under the same Q (a ranking query, not a calibration query), it tolerates substantial absolute Q error.
- **可借鉴**: The most directly transplantable annealing mechanism for our margin. Per-transition gate: apply the margin (or any demo-pressure term) at demo state s only when the demo's actual MC return-to-go exceeds the critic's Q(s, greedy chunk) — using ground-truth demo returns (already computed for finding 4's MC lower bound) as the 'BC side' of the comparison instead of a possibly-poisoned Q(s, a_demo), which sidesteps mode 1's fake gaps corrupting the gate itself. Globally, the batch-mean of this indicator IS the annealing schedule: it starts near 1 and decays as the critic's greedy chunk catches up to demo performance, directly replacing the naive λ-anneal that collapsed decision margins (mode 2) with a closed-loop one that only removes demo pressure where the critic has demonstrably absorbed it.

#### AWAC: Accelerating Online Reinforcement Learning with Offline Datasets — Nair et al., 2020 (arXiv) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.09359>
- **方法**：Actor-critic for offline pretraining + online fine-tuning. Critic: standard Bellman TD with target network — no pessimism, no BC term. Actor: θ ← argmax E_{(s,a)~buffer}[log π_θ(a|s) · exp(A^π(s,a)/λ)], the closed-form solution of KL-constrained policy improvement projected by forward KL — a weighted maximum-likelihood update where each dataset action is weighted by the exponentiated advantage under the current critic (λ=0.3 for manipulation, 1.0 for locomotion). The KL constraint to the data distribution is enforced IMPLICITLY — no behavior-policy density model is ever fit. Their analysis: methods with explicit behavior models (e.g., BEAR) become over-conservative during fine-tuning because the behavior model can't track the shifting online data mixture; BC-then-on-policy-RL wastes the prior data.
- **为何有效**：Imitation pressure is proportional to measured advantage: demo actions the critic already rates as good get strongly cloned, mediocre or absorbed demo actions get weight exp(A/λ) ≈ 1 or less, so the demo term self-fades per state-action as the critic improves — no fake preferences are ever injected into Q, and no explicit density model exists to go stale when the data distribution shifts online. The critic stays a pure return estimator throughout.
- **可借鉴**: Advantage-weighted margin: keep our margin loss but scale each demo transition's contribution by exp(A_demo/λ) where A_demo = (demo MC return-to-go, reward-scaled per mode 3) − (critic's Q at that state, e.g., mean or greedy value). Where the demo action is genuinely better than what the critic would do, the margin fires; where the true gap is ~0.009 (mode 1's measured reality), the weight collapses to ~1·small and no 0.89 fake gap is carved. Uses ground-truth demo returns rather than possibly-poisoned Q(s,a_demo), and provides continuous per-transition annealing that avoids the cliff of global λ removal (mode 2). Cheaper than the ROT gate (no greedy chunk rollout needed if the value baseline is the C51 mean).

#### Imitation Bootstrapped Reinforcement Learning (IBRL) — Hu et al., 2023 (arXiv; RSS 2024) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02198>
- **方法**：Train a BC policy μ on demos, then FREEZE it. During online TD3-style RL with NO imitation loss: (1) acting — sample a_IL ~ μ(s) and a_RL = π(s)+ε, execute a = argmax over the two of min_{i∈K} Q_i(s,a) (min over a random 2-subset of the critic ensemble, E=2 for pixels / 5 for state); (2) bootstrapping — TD target y = r + γ·max_{a'∈{a_IL, a_RL}} min_i Q'_i(s',a'), i.e., the frozen BC proposal also competes inside the target. Demos initialize the replay buffer; oversampling is optional and stops helping on all but the hardest task. Actor dropout p=0.5 and a ViT Q-network with early action injection are large-effect implementation details. Beats RLPD+ by up to 6.4x sample efficiency on Robomimic; ablation shows removing the bootstrap proposal hurts, more so on harder tasks.
- **为何有效**：Imitation is moved entirely from the loss to decision time: the critic only ever answers cheap ranking queries ('which of these 2 concrete actions is better?') instead of needing calibrated values over the whole action space, and the frozen BC policy guarantees a competent candidate is always on the ballot both for exploration and for the Bellman max — so target values are propagated along demo-quality behavior even while the RL actor is bad. Because no loss term pushes Q toward demo actions, Q remains a return estimator, and the BC influence automatically becomes a no-op once the RL policy's actions win the argmax.
- **可借鉴**: The flagship transplant, and it generalizes finding 4's 'candidate backup from replay argmax' from retrieval to a learned proposer. Add a small BC head (cross-entropy on demo bins, per level per dim — trained offline, then frozen; actor-like but decision power stays with argmax-Q, per the allowed borderline). At acting time, construct 2 candidate chunks — the coarse-to-fine greedy chunk and the BC head's chunk — score both with the sequence critic, execute the winner; in the TD target, bootstrap with max over the same two candidates' Q. This attacks mode 2 at its root: the composite argmax over ~240 factored bin decisions no longer needs every per-factor margin to be right off-manifold, because a demo-manifold candidate is always available and the critic need only rank 2 full chunks. Meanwhile the margin loss can be deleted outright (mode 1). Pair with a 2-critic min ensemble (mode 5) as IBRL does to keep the proposal comparison from being won by overestimation noise.
- *另见类别*：demo-driven RL with NO explicit BC loss

#### Adaptive Behavior Cloning Regularization for Stable Offline-to-Online Reinforcement Learning — Zhao et al., 2022 (arXiv) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13846>
- **方法**：TD3+BC extended for online fine-tuning: the BC weight α_online is adjusted every episode by a proportional-derivative controller on returns: Δα = K_P·(R_avg − R_target) + K_D·max(0, R_avg − R_current), with R_avg a moving-average normalized return, R_target ≈ expert level (1.05 normalized), α clipped to [0, 0.4]. The P-term decreases α as average performance approaches target; the D-term INCREASES α when the current episode drops below the moving average — an explicit stability-recovery reflex. Ablations: fixed α=0.4 is stable but slow; α=0 learns fast on some tasks but collapses on others; the adaptive controller is stable everywhere while nearly matching the fast variant's efficiency.
- **为何有效**：It converts BC-weight annealing from an open-loop schedule (which must be tuned per task and fails when decayed too early — exactly our measured collapse) into a closed-loop controller on the quantity we actually care about, task return: demo pressure is released only as fast as performance is retained, and is automatically restored the moment performance dips, bounding the damage of any premature release to roughly one controller period.
- **可借鉴**: Discovered addition; the smallest-diff fix to our mode-2 finding that 'annealing/removing BC naively collapses'. Wrap our margin weight λ (currently fixed 1.0) in this PD controller driven by the online success-rate moving average (sparse-reward success fraction as R; R_target = official baseline 64.6% or demo-level success): decay λ while success holds, snap it back up on regression. This makes the BC→no-BC transition of finding 4 a controlled descent rather than a cliff, and composes with any per-state gating (ROT/AWAC-style) — the controller sets the global budget, the gate allocates it across states. Note it does not fix mode 1 by itself (the margin still carves fake gaps while active); it is the safe exit ramp, not the destination.

#### Policy Decorator: Model-Agnostic Online Refinement for Large Policy Model — Yuan et al., 2024 (arXiv; ICLR 2025) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13630>
- **方法**：A large IL base policy (Behavior Transformer, Diffusion Policy) is frozen; a small residual Gaussian policy is trained with SAC on sparse reward, executing a = π_base(s) + π_res(s), with the critic evaluating the summed action. Two controlled-exploration mechanisms make it work: (1) bounded residual — tanh-squashed residual scaled to (−α, α) with α ≈ 0.03-0.3 of action scale, recommended near the action deltas seen in demos; (2) progressive exploration — the residual is applied with probability ε that ramps linearly 0→1 over H steps (30k-4M), so early behavior is almost pure IL.
- **为何有效**：The frozen IL policy defines a trust region in action space: the RL problem is reduced to searching a small box around a competent action, so random exploration is safe (never leaves the near-demo funnel where sparse reward is reachable) and the critic only needs to rank small perturbations rather than value the full action space.
- **可借鉴**: Discovered addition; the 'bounded deviation from a demo prior' idea maps cleanly onto our factored bin argmax. Using the frozen BC bin head (from the IBRL borrow) as base, restrict the online argmax at each level/dim to bins within radius r of the BC-proposed bin (r=1 early: 2-3 candidate bins instead of 5 per factor), and grow r on a progressive schedule or via the Zhao controller. This contains mode 2's exponential sensitivity — with ~240 factored decisions, cutting per-factor candidates and anchoring them near the demo manifold means small margin errors cannot compound into off-manifold chunks — while requiring no margin loss (mode 1). Marked medium because IBRL's 2-candidate argmax likely achieves the same protection more simply; this variant is the fallback if full-chunk candidate scoring is too coarse and per-factor trust regions are needed.


### 1.4 Demo-driven RL——无显式 BC loss（核心类别）

#### SQIL: Imitation Learning via Reinforcement Learning with Sparse Rewards — Reddy et al., 2019 (ICLR 2020) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11108>
- **方法**：Imitation is expressed entirely through value targets: demo transitions are hardwired to reward +1, agent-collected transitions to reward 0, and a soft Q-network minimizes the squared soft Bellman error over both buffers with balanced 50/50 sampling — theta minimizes delta^2(D_demo, r=1) + lambda_samp * delta^2(D_samp, r=0), where delta^2 uses the soft (log-sum-exp) backup. Base learner is soft Q-learning (discrete) or SAC (continuous). No reward model, no BC gradient. Theoretically equivalent to BC regularized by a sparsity prior on the implied reward plus a dynamics-consistency (Bellman) penalty. Known failure: reward decay as the policy matches the expert; the balanced buffer guarantees an effective minimum reward of 1/(1+lambda_samp).
- **为何有效**：Because demo states carry +1 and everything else 0, the learned soft value function forms a gradient field pointing back toward the demo manifold from any out-of-distribution state — the agent is steered by Q-shape rather than action matching. Since the only loss is a Bellman loss, imitation pressure and value semantics can never disagree, unlike a margin loss stapled onto TD.
- **可借鉴**: SQIL is the closest published ancestor of our sealed dense-floor recipe (finding 4): our 'executed demo bin gets max(MC, Bellman), counterfactual siblings get a floor distribution' is a calibrated, distributional refinement of SQIL's +1/0 dichotomy. Two concrete refinements to take: (a) replace the DQfD margin loss entirely with target-level demo preference — a small fixed reward bonus on demo transitions (scaled through q_reward_scale=0.07 so it cannot re-trigger C51 saturation, failure 3) — so demo preference enters through C51 targets and Q retains return ordering (fixes failure 1's manifold-frequency-not-return pathology at the root); (b) their reward-decay analysis warns that our online floor applied only to positive-return trajectories will dilute as successes become routine — the 1/(1+lambda_samp) guarantee is the argument for a permanent, never-annealed frozen demo fraction.

#### Precise and Dexterous Robotic Manipulation via Human-in-the-Loop Reinforcement Learning (HIL-SERL) — Luo et al., 2024 (arXiv 2410.21845) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21845>
- **方法**：RLPD core on real robots: two buffers (20-30 demos offline; online RL data), symmetric 50/50 sampling per training step, SAC-style updates with no BC loss anywhere, pretrained ResNet-10 features plus proprioception, actions as 6D impedance-controller twists. Sparse reward comes from a binary image success classifier (>95% accuracy). Human operators intervene mid-rollout; intervention segments are stored in BOTH the demo buffer and the RL buffer, while autonomous policy transitions go only to the RL buffer. The discrete gripper is handled as a separate MDP solved with DQN (pure argmax over discrete actions) alongside the continuous critic. Results: ~100% success on 13 precision tasks (assembly, dynamic, dual-arm) within 1-2.5 hours of real-world training, 1.8x faster cycle times than IL baselines.
- **为何有效**：Symmetric sampling keeps rewarded transitions in every batch under extreme sparsity, and the intervention-to-demo-buffer rule makes the 'demo' distribution non-stationary in exactly the right way: corrections are collected at the current policy's failure states, so the privileged buffer tracks where the policy actually needs value signal instead of going stale.
- **可借鉴**: Two transplants. (1) The intervention trick has a no-human analog addressing failure 5 (demos highly off-policy): promote successful ONLINE trajectories into the privileged expert buffer (growing it, keeping it 50% of each batch, applying the positive-return dense floor of finding 4 to it), so the demo half of the batch continuously re-centers on states the current policy visits. This is a principled upgrade of our recipe's frozen expert buffer from static to curated-growing. (2) Their gripper-as-DQN result is existence proof that pure argmax-over-discrete-values control works on real sparse-reward manipulation at scale — supporting the critic-only CQN-AS premise itself.

#### Reverse Forward Curriculum Learning (RFCL) — Tao et al., 2024 (ICLR) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03379>
- **方法**：Demo influence enters ONLY through the initial-state distribution. Reverse stage: per-demonstration reset points — the sim is reset to states along each demo, starting near success, and each demo's reset point advances backward (reverse_step_size 4-8 states) as the agent succeeds from it, yielding a policy strong on a narrow initial distribution. Forward stage: a curriculum then expands over the task's full randomized initial state distribution, prioritizing initial states at the frontier of solvability. Base learner is SAC with a 10-critic Q-ensemble; the codebase contains no BC loss anywhere. Solves 1-10-demo sparse-reward tasks including previously unsolved ManiSkill2 PegInsertionSide and PlugCharger, and outperforms RLPD, Cal-QL, JSRL, and DAPG — largest margins on high-precision tasks with high initial-state randomization.
- **为何有效**：State resets convert one demo into a dense ladder of solvable subproblems where sparse reward is always a few steps from the start, collapsing exponential exploration; per-demo curricula plus the forward stage fix Backplay's known failure under randomized initial states because coverage comes from many demo trajectories and a deliberately expanded start distribution rather than one path.
- **可借鉴**: The strongest fully loss-free alternative for the no-BC line: BiGym is MuJoCo-based, so implement physics state save/restore, reset a fraction of online episodes to per-demo checkpoints with success-gated backward stepping, then a forward curriculum over BiGym's randomized inits. Because no imitation gradient exists, failure 1 (BC-margin destroying return semantics) is structurally impossible, and demos stop being off-policy replay data (failure 5) — they become on-policy start states. It beat RLPD and Cal-QL exactly on the high-precision, high-randomization regime BiGym occupies. Cost is engineering (state serialization in BiGym), not algorithmic risk; composable with the dense-floor recipe.

#### MoDem: Accelerating Visual Model-Based Reinforcement Learning with Demonstrations — Hansen et al., 2022 (ICLR 2023) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05698>
- **方法**：TD-MPC base (encoder h, latent dynamics d, reward R, Q, policy pi). Phase 1: BC-pretrain encoder+policy with -log pi(a|h(s)) on demos.
- **为何有效**：The BC gradient exists only in Phase 1 and only for the proposal policy/encoder; the heavy lifting at the online transition is done by the BC-free mechanisms — seeding puts the critic's first gradients on the policy's own state distribution (same shock-absorber logic WSRL later isolated), and oversampling keeps rare rewarded demo transitions in the batch while annealing off as online successes accumulate.
- **可借鉴**: The BC-free parts transplant to critic-only CQN-AS: (a) a seeding phase — after the offline 10k dense-reward-Q phase (finding 4), roll out the greedy coarse-to-fine policy with bin-level noise to pre-fill the online buffer before any online gradient step (converges with the WSRL warmup borrow; addresses failure 2's sudden-shift collapse); (b) replace fixed 50/50 demo sampling with kappa annealed 75 -> 25% over early online training, which their ablations show beats naive constant mixing when online data is still scarce. The 'components are useless alone, strong jointly' ablation is also a warning for our A/B methodology: test stabilizer bundles, not single knobs.

#### Jump-Start Reinforcement Learning (JSRL) — Uchendu et al., 2022 (ICML 2023) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02372>
- **方法**：Two policies: a guide (from demos/offline RL/any prior) and an exploration policy. Each episode, the guide rolls in for the first h steps, then the exploration policy takes over and is the only one trained; a curriculum shrinks h as the learner's evaluated performance improves, so the learner conquers the task back-to-front.
- **为何有效**：Roll-ins place the learner's TD updates on near-success states first, where the sparse reward is immediately reachable, and always bootstrapping into values it has already learned; the shrinking horizon is a soft version of reverse-curriculum resets that needs no simulator state restore.
- **可借鉴**: The cheap, no-plumbing sibling of RFCL: replay recorded demo action prefixes for the first h steps of online BiGym episodes, let the coarse-to-fine argmax finish, and anneal h with success rate. Attacks failure 2 from the state side — the critic meets off-manifold states gradually and near success, where margins can be learned from actual returns instead of propped up by a BC margin. Medium rather than high because BiGym's randomized initial states make open-loop demo-prefix replay drift from the recorded trajectory, limiting roll-in fidelity (the exact weakness RFCL's state resets remove).

#### Learning Montezuma's Revenge from a Single Demonstration — Salimans & Chen, 2018 (arXiv/OpenAI) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03381>
- **方法**：Every episode resets the environment to a state along one human demonstration, starting near the end of the game and moving the reset point earlier as the agent's success rate rises; training is plain PPO with zero imitation gradient. Their analysis: standard RL run-time scales exponentially in the number of states between rewards; demo-anchored reverse resets reduce the burden to roughly quadratic because the agent only ever has to bridge the short gap from the current reset point to states it already masters.
- **为何有效**：It decouples credit assignment from exploration: the agent always starts within a short horizon of already-valued states, so sparse reward propagates backward one curriculum stage at a time, and no mechanism ever asks the value function to encode 'demo-ness' — values encode return by construction.
- **可借鉴**: Same transplant family as RFCL (which supersedes it for randomized-init manipulation): MuJoCo state restore to late-demo states for a fraction of online episodes, advancing earlier on success. For CQN-AS specifically it shortens the horizon over which the ~24-step success tail (finding 3's reward structure) must propagate through nstep=1 backups, and converts demos from off-policy replay (failure 5) into on-policy start states. Listed medium because RFCL is the modern, benchmark-proven version of this idea for our setting; this paper is the clean mechanism statement.

#### Backplay: 'Man muss immer umkehren' — Resnick et al., 2018 (arXiv) 【low】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06919>
- **方法**：Concurrent with Salimans & Chen: starting states are sampled from a sliding window over a single demonstration, beginning near the end; windows advance backward on a fixed epoch schedule (e.g. [0,4) steps-from-goal at epoch 0 to the full start by epoch 1750).
- **为何有效**：Same reverse-curriculum mechanism as Salimans & Chen; the distinctive evidence is that imitation-free demo usage can EXCEED the demonstrator (no BC ceiling) and works from suboptimal demos, because the agent inherits only the demo's states, never its action choices.
- **可借鉴**: Little to transplant beyond what Salimans & Chen and RFCL already give; its value here is two negative/positive results that shape our design: (a) the no-BC-ceiling result is the argument for the whole no-BC line (a BC-margin critic can never rank actions above demo behavior — failure 1 restated); (b) its randomized-init failure mode is exactly BiGym's setting and mandates RFCL-style per-demo curricula (we have ~60 demos) rather than single-demo Backplay. Context paper.


### 1.5 Critic 稳定性 / 归一化 / 容量工艺

#### Efficient Online Reinforcement Learning with Offline Data (RLPD) — Ball et al., 2023 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02948>
- **方法**：Shows off-policy SAC can exploit offline/demo data with no offline pretraining phase and no BC or explicit conservatism, via three changes: (1) symmetric sampling — every batch is 50% from the online replay buffer and 50% from the offline/demo buffer; (2) LayerNorm in the critic MLP, which bounds the critic's output growth (||Q|| effectively bounded by weight norms) so value extrapolation on out-of-distribution actions cannot diverge; (3) an ensemble of E=10 critics with subset-min over Z in {1,2} for TD targets (REDQ-style), at UTD=20 (10 for pixels). Results: 2.5x prior best on sparse-reward Adroit, first method to solve all six D4RL AntMaze tasks. Ablations: without LayerNorm, complete failure ('no progress on any task') on expert-demo sparse Adroit; without true ensembles (dropout or weight decay substitutes), sparse-reward performance degrades badly; CDQ min needs per-domain tuning.
- **为何有效**：Mixing off-policy demo data makes the critic query actions/states far from the online distribution; unbounded MLPs extrapolate arbitrarily large values there, and bootstrapped maxima harvest exactly those errors into runaway targets. LayerNorm caps extrapolated magnitudes (implicit, geometry-based conservatism that does not penalize exploration like CQL), the ensemble-min removes residual overestimation statistically, and symmetric sampling keeps demo gradient signal constant instead of decaying as online data accumulates.
- **可借鉴**: The closest published neighbor to our no-BC recipe (finding 4) — their offline buffer + no-pretrain + implicit conservatism parallels our frozen expert replay + value-floor construction. Three transplants: (a) enforce 50/50 demo/online batch composition (a principled version of our frozen expert buffer sampling); (b) add LayerNorm to every dense layer of the critic trunk and C51 heads — their LayerNorm ablation on expert-only sparse Adroit is a published confirmation of our failure-2 mechanism (unbounded extrapolation off the demo manifold destroying action selection), and it is the cheapest single intervention in this whole category; (c) small critic ensemble with subset-min as statistical pessimism on OOD bins, partially replacing the hand-tuned counterfactual floor. Directly addresses failures 2, 4-refinement, and 5.
- *另见类别*：demo-driven RL with NO explicit BC loss

#### Randomized Ensembled Double Q-Learning: Learning Fast Without a Model (REDQ) — Chen et al., 2021 (ICLR) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05982>
- **方法**：Maintains an ensemble of N=10 Q-networks and runs UTD ratio G=20. Each gradient step, a fresh random subset M of size 2 is drawn and the shared TD target is y = r + gamma*(min over the 2 sampled target critics of Q_i(s', a~') - alpha*log pi(a~'|s')); all 10 critics regress to this y. Their bias analysis shows this keeps average Q-estimation bias small, slightly negative, and near-constant, and — crucially — keeps the STD of the bias across states low, whereas SAC at G=20 develops large, highly non-uniform bias. Theorem 1 shows E[target] depends on M but not N, so M controls the bias level (M=2 avoids maxmin over-pessimism) while N independently shrinks target variance.
- **为何有效**：At high UTD the single-critic bootstrap amplifies its own overestimation noise into divergence; min-over-a-random-small-subset injects just enough pessimism to cancel the max-induced positive bias while the large ensemble averages away variance. Near-uniform bias matters more than near-zero bias: a uniformly shifted Q preserves action ordering (argmax), while non-uniform bias corrupts it.
- **可借鉴**: Replace the single CQN-AS critic with N=5-10 C51 heads sharing the CNN encoder and sequence torso (cheap — heads are small), and compute C51 targets per factor using the head with the minimum expected value among a random subset of 2. This gives adaptive, uncertainty-driven pessimism on off-manifold bins — a statistical replacement/complement for our hand-crafted task-minimum floor distribution on counterfactual siblings (finding 4) — and directly counters the per-factor overestimation whose accumulation destroys composite argmax margins (failure 2). Their 'near-uniform bias preserves argmax ordering' analysis is exactly the property our 240-factored-decision chunk needs. It also licenses raising UTD from 1 toward 4-10 to extract more from 60 demos (failure 5) without divergence.

#### Bigger, Better, Faster: Human-level Atari with human-level efficiency (BBF) — Schwarzer et al., 2023 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19452>
- **方法**：A value-only distributional (Rainbow/C51-lineage) agent for Atari-100k built from: replay ratio 8; shrink-and-perturb resets every 40k gradient steps (conv layers interpolated 50% toward a random init, later/head layers fully reset); n-step annealed exponentially from 10 to 3 over the first 10k gradient steps after each reset (~25% of the inter-reset budget); discount annealed 0.97 to 0.997 on the same schedule; AdamW weight decay 0.1; the SPR self-predictive auxiliary loss; a 4x-width 15-layer Impala ResNet; and an EMA target network used for both training and acting. Ablations: removing SPR or weight decay hurts most, then softer resets, then removing the receding n-step horizon and discount annealing.
- **为何有效**：Each reset wipes accumulated overfitting to early data (restoring plasticity at high replay ratio), and the post-reset schedule re-learns cheaply: large n immediately after a reset propagates reward with low bootstrap dependence while the freshly reset value function is untrustworthy, then annealing to small n reduces variance once bootstrap targets become reliable; weight decay and SPR keep the enlarged network from memorizing the tiny dataset.
- **可借鉴**: The most directly transplantable full recipe here, because BBF is architecturally a cousin of CQN-AS (distributional value-only agent with argmax action selection). Concretely: (a) replace nstep=1 (failure 5) with n annealed from ~10 to ~1-3 — with the 24-28-step sparse reward tail, high-n targets are a principled version of our max(MC, Bellman) lower-bound hack from finding 4 and propagate success signal to early chunk decisions immediately; (b) shrink-and-perturb the critic (conv 50% interpolation, C51 heads full reset) at the offline-to-online transition to break the 60-demo overfit; (c) AdamW weight decay ~0.1. Each piece addresses a measured mode: n-step annealing (failure 5 + the MC-bound need in finding 4), resets (demo primacy bias), weight decay (60-demo memorization that produces failure 1's manifold-frequency Q).

#### The Primacy Bias in Deep Reinforcement Learning — Nikishin et al., 2022 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07802>
- **方法**：Identifies primacy bias: deep RL agents overfit their earliest experience and this damage persists even as good data accumulates. The diagnostic 'heavy priming' experiment — many gradient updates on a small early buffer — permanently cripples subsequent learning even with unlimited fresh data. The intervention: periodically reset agent network parameters while PRESERVING the replay buffer (full networks for SAC on DMC; final layers/heads for SPR on Atari), then continue training on the retained data. Improves SAC and SPR consistently, with larger gains at higher replay ratios; post-reset performance recovers almost immediately because the buffer retains the knowledge.
- **为何有效**：Networks trained too long on a narrow early distribution lose plasticity and lock in features/values fit to that distribution; a reset discards the corrupted function while the replay buffer preserves the information, so relearning from the full mixed dataset produces a better-conditioned solution than continued fine-tuning ever reaches.
- **可借鉴**: Our regime is a literal heavy-priming instantiation: 10k offline updates on 60 demos before online data exists — the paper predicts exactly our observation that 'the critic overfits 60 demos long before online data matters,' and failure 1 (Q = demo-manifold frequency) is the fingerprint of that overfit. Transplant: at the offline-to-online switch, and optionally every ~100-250k online steps, reset the per-level/per-dim C51 bin heads (keep encoder and sequence torso, or shrink-and-perturb them per BBF) plus optimizer state, retaining both the frozen expert buffer and the online buffer (compatible with finding 4's recipe). The frozen expert buffer guarantees demo information survives the reset, so this decouples 'demo knowledge' from 'demo overfit'.

#### Hyperspherical Normalization for Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning (SimbaV2) — Lee et al., 2025 (ICML spotlight) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15280>
- **方法**：Successor to SimBa with two mechanisms: (1) hyperspherical normalization — weight matrices and intermediate features are constrained/projected to the unit hypersphere throughout training, so neither weight norms nor feature norms can grow (a hard geometric constraint, vs LayerNorm's per-feature statistics which still permit weight-norm inflation); (2) distributional value estimation combined with reward scaling by running return statistics, keeping TD target magnitudes and hence gradient scales stable across tasks with different reward scales. Built on SAC; scales cleanly with both model size and UTD without needing resets; SOTA on 57 continuous-control tasks across 4 domains (DMC, MyoSuite, HumanoidBench, etc.).
- **为何有效**：Under nonstationary RL targets, weight-norm growth is the common pathway to both plasticity loss (effective learning rate decays as norms grow) and value extrapolation blowups; projecting to the hypersphere removes that degree of freedom entirely, keeping the effective learning rate constant and features bounded for the whole run — which is why it obviates the periodic resets other recipes need. Return-scaled rewards keep the distributional critic's support well-utilized regardless of task reward magnitude.
- **可借鉴**: Two direct hits. (a) Their return-statistics-based reward scaling is the principled generalization of our hand-tuned q_reward_scale=0.07 (failure 3, C51 support saturation): normalize rewards by a running std of discounted returns so the [-2,2] atom support stays calibrated automatically per task, instead of re-tuning a scalar per reward scheme. (b) Hyperspherical weight/feature projection on the critic trunk is a resets-free counter to the demo-overfit dynamics: the 10k-update offline phase cannot inflate weight norms and burn the plasticity budget before online learning starts, attacking the same mechanism as Nikishin resets but without discarding the learned function — and bounded features attack failure 2's off-manifold margin decay at the source.

#### CrossQ: Batch Normalization in Deep Reinforcement Learning for Greater Sample Efficiency and Simplicity — Bhatt et al., 2024 (ICLR) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05605>
- **方法**：Removes target networks from SAC entirely and instead inserts BatchNorm into the critic's MLP layers. The load-bearing trick is batch construction: the current pair (s,a) and the bootstrap pair (s',a') are concatenated into a single batch for one forward pass, so BatchNorm statistics are computed over the mixture of both distributions; the Bellman target r + gamma*(Q(s',a') - alpha*log pi) is then evaluated by the live network under those shared statistics.
- **为何有效**：A target network is a slow, implicit regularizer that also delays value propagation; CrossQ shows the real requirement is not staleness but distributional consistency between the live and bootstrap evaluations.
- **可借鉴**: Replace the tau=0.02 EMA target critic in CQN-AS: compute C51 targets with the live critic by jointly forwarding (o_t, executed chunk) and (o_{t+1}, per-factor argmax chunk) through a BatchNorm-equipped trunk. The mixture-statistics property is the interesting part for failure mode 2: with 50/50 demo/online batches, normalization statistics are forced to span both the demo manifold and online-visited states, which should resist the feature drift underlying the 0.356-to-0.066 margin decay. However this is the riskiest transplant in the category — BatchNorm under factored 240-way argmax targets and heavily off-policy demo mixtures (failure 5) is untested, and batch-statistics coupling to sampling ratios is a real hazard. Try after the LayerNorm/ensemble borrows, not before.

#### SimBa: Simplicity Bias for Scaling Up Parameters in Deep Reinforcement Learning — Lee et al., 2024 (ICLR 2025 spotlight) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09754>
- **方法**：A drop-in network architecture, not a new loss: (1) RSNorm — observation normalization by running mean/std; (2) a linear embedding followed by pre-LayerNorm residual feedforward blocks (LN -> MLP -> residual add), giving a direct linear path from input to output; (3) a final LayerNorm before the value/policy heads. Plugged into SAC (and other off-policy/on-policy/unsupervised methods) with losses unchanged, it makes parameter scaling monotonically helpful rather than harmful: SAC+SimBa matches or beats SOTA (including TD-MPC2-class methods) on DMC, MyoSuite, HumanoidBench.
- **为何有效**：The residual-plus-LayerNorm structure biases the network toward simple (low-frequency) functions early, so added capacity is spent on generalizable structure instead of memorizing early replay data; LayerNorm bounds feature magnitudes so value estimates cannot blow up on inputs outside the training distribution; input normalization keeps gradient scale stable under nonstationary state distributions.
- **可借鉴**: Rebuild the CQN-AS critic trunk (the sequence torso and per-level per-dim MLP heads feeding the C51 dueling logits) as a SimBa stack: RSNorm on proprioceptive inputs, pre-LN residual blocks, final LN before the bin/atom heads — then scale width, which our single 60-demo-overfit critic (failure 5 regime) has headroom for. The bounded-feature property attacks the suspected root of failure mode 2 (unbounded feature extrapolation off the demo manifold shrinking relative per-factor margins). This is general craft rather than a targeted fix, hence medium; it is also the substrate the REDQ/RLPD borrows should be built on.

#### Bigger, Regularized, Optimistic: scaling for compute and sample-efficient continuous control (BRO) — Nauman et al., 2024 (NeurIPS spotlight) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16158>
- **方法**：SAC-based recipe with a scaled BroNet critic: dense layer + LayerNorm, then residual blocks with LayerNorm after every dense layer (post-LN, like LLM FFN sublayers), ~5M params (~7x standard SAC); replay ratio 10 (BRO-Fast: 2); full-parameter resets at steps 15k, 50k, then every 250k up to 1M; a 100-quantile distributional critic; and optimistic exploration via a dual pessimistic/optimistic actor with a KL constraint (target KL 0.05), where the optimistic Q adds beta * |Q1 - Q2| disagreement. Notably they REMOVE clipped double-Q (using ensemble mean instead, ~20% gain) and found n-step returns did not help.
- **为何有效**：Strong regularization (LayerNorm everywhere + resets + weight decay) removes the failure modes that previously made big critics diverge in RL, so capacity finally converts into better value fits; once the critic is well-regularized, global min-based pessimism becomes an unnecessary handicap and mild optimism plus disagreement-based exploration recovers the lost signal.
- **可借鉴**: Two components survive removing the actor: (a) BroNet-style LayerNorm-after-every-dense-layer in the CQN-AS critic trunk plus a width increase; (b) the early-reset schedule (15k/50k) transplanted to our offline-to-online boundary — reset critic parameters at the end of the 10k demo-only phase and once shortly after online interaction begins, directly targeting the 60-demo overfit (primacy-bias) regime. Their anti-borrow finding matters too: global min-pessimism (CDQ) hurt them, supporting our finding-4 design where pessimism is applied only to counterfactual sibling bins rather than uniformly. The optimism/dual-actor half does not transplant (actor-based), hence medium overall.

#### Dropout Q-Functions for Doubly Efficient Reinforcement Learning (DroQ) — Hiraoka et al., 2022 (ICLR) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02034>
- **方法**：Replaces REDQ's 10-critic ensemble with just M=2 Q-functions, each equipped with dropout (rate ~0.01, tuned per environment in [0.0001, 0.1]) after weight layers and LayerNorm applied after the dropout. Target is y = r + gamma*(min over the 2 dropout critics of Q(s',a') - alpha*log pi), with dropout active so each target draw is a stochastic ensemble sample; UTD G=20 as in REDQ.
- **为何有效**：Dropout provides implicit ensembling — each stochastic forward pass is a different thinned network — so the min over two dropout critics approximates the pessimism/variance-reduction of an explicit 10-model ensemble at near-single-model cost; LayerNorm keeps the injected stochasticity from destabilizing optimization.
- **可借鉴**: The compute-frugal fallback for the REDQ borrow: since the CQN-AS sequence critic is large, duplicate only the C51 head stack twice with dropout 0.01 + LayerNorm and take per-factor min targets, enabling UTD > 1 (failure 5) with ~10% overhead instead of 5-10x. Important caveat that caps relevance: RLPD explicitly found dropout-based uncertainty 'does not generalize to sparse reward' settings and true ensembles were required — and sparse-reward BiGym with off-policy demos is exactly that regime. Treat DroQ as the cheap ablation arm, REDQ-style heads as the default.

#### Data-Efficient Reinforcement Learning with Self-Predictive Representations (SPR) — Schwarzer et al., 2021 (ICLR) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05929>
- **方法**：Adds an auxiliary objective to Rainbow: an action-conditioned latent transition model rolls the online encoder's representation forward K steps (K=5); targets are the EMA (momentum) copy of the encoder applied to the actually observed future frames; both sides pass through projection heads and the loss is negative cosine similarity between predicted and target projections at each of the K steps, summed and added to the RL loss. Different augmentations are applied to online and target views, so the loss simultaneously enforces temporal predictiveness and augmentation invariance.
- **为何有效**：Sparse TD errors provide almost no gradient signal to a pixel encoder early in training; forcing the representation to be predictable under known action sequences makes it encode controllable dynamics rather than visual nuisance, and the EMA target prevents collapse.
- **可借鉴**: CQN-AS already consumes action chunks, so this is nearly free structurally: add a small transition head that, from the fused 3-cam 84x84 encoder latent at o_t and the K=4-16 executed action chunk, predicts the EMA-encoder latents at t+1..t+K with cosine loss, weighted alongside the C51/TD loss. The point: today the only dense gradient shaping our encoder is the BC-margin loss, which is why Q encodes demo-manifold frequency (failure 1); SPR provides a dense, demo-independent signal so representations of online-visited off-manifold states stay structured — plausibly the upstream cause of failure 2's margin decay. BBF's ablation showing SPR is its single most important component makes this the strongest 'medium': the mechanism targets a hypothesized cause, not yet one we've measured directly.

#### Normalization and effective learning rates in reinforcement learning (NaP) — Lyle et al., 2024 (NeurIPS) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01800>
- **方法**：Analyzes a side effect of normalization layers: because LayerNorm makes the function scale-invariant to preceding weights, gradient magnitude scales as 1/||w||, so as weight norms grow during training the EFFECTIVE learning rate silently decays — an implicit, uncontrolled schedule that in RL decays too fast relative to the nonstationarity of the targets. Proposed fix, Normalize-and-Project (NaP): keep normalization layers everywhere, and periodically (or continually) project weight matrices back to a fixed norm — a loss-preserving operation by scale invariance — making the effective learning rate explicit and constant.
- **为何有效**：Plasticity loss under nonstationary targets is substantially an optimization artifact — the network's effective step size has quietly shrunk by the time the data distribution shifts, so it cannot chase new targets; restoring weight norm restores step size without destroying the learned function, unlike a reset.
- **可借鉴**: A rider on the LayerNorm borrows rather than a standalone: if we add LayerNorm to the CQN-AS critic (per RLPD/SimBa), also project critic weight norms after each update (or every N updates) to a fixed value. The specific concern it addresses: our 10k-update offline demo phase will inflate weight norms while overfitting 60 demos, so by the time online data arrives the effective learning rate is a fraction of the nominal 5e-5 — a clean mechanistic account of why online experience 'fails to matter' (the demo-overfit regime) that complements primacy-bias resets and is subsumed by SimbaV2's hypersphere constraint if that borrow is taken instead. Craft-level, mechanism verified in continual-learning settings rather than our exact regime.


### 1.6 Off-policy target、多步回报与悲观主义

#### Revisiting Peng's Q(lambda) for Modern Reinforcement Learning — Kozuno, Tang, Rowland, Munos, Kapturowski, Dabney, Valko, Abel, 2021 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00107>
- **方法**：Peng's Q(lambda) is a lambda-weighted average of UNCORRECTED n-step returns: intermediate steps use behavior-policy rewards with no importance ratios and no trace cutting, bootstrapped at the end with the greedy/target Q (operator (1-lambda) sum_n lambda^{n-1} (T^mu)^{n-1} T^pi Q). With a fixed behavior policy its fixed point is biased: it converges to the Q-function of the best mixture policy of the form lambda*mu + (1-lambda)*pi, i.e. pulled toward Q^mu. The authors prove it still converges to the OPTIMAL policy provided the behavior policy slowly tracks the greedy policy (mu_k = alpha*pi_k + (1-alpha)*mu_{k-1}, alpha >= 1-lambda), in the style of conservative policy iteration. Empirically it consistently outperforms Retrace and tree-backup, most strongly on sparse-reward continuous-control tasks.
- **为何有效**：The bias toward Q^mu is the price for never cutting traces, so reward information propagates the full return length every update — decisive under sparse reward where 1-step bootstrapping must relay the success signal through dozens of updates via a possibly-corrupt critic. When the behavior data is good (expert demos, or a replay that tracks the improving policy), the 'bias' is toward an expert value function, which is benign or actively helpful.
- **可借鉴**: Replace the nstep=1 C51 TD target on demo transitions (and positive-return online trajectories) with an uncorrected lambda-weighted multi-step return (or fixed n=5-10), i.e. shift the C51 support by the scaled n-step reward sum and bootstrap the tail with the target net. For DEMO data mu is the expert, so Peng's bias toward Q^mu is exactly the value grounding our critic lacks (finding 1: Q encodes manifold membership, not return). It also generalizes our existing MC-lower-bound trick: MC is the n=infinity member of this family, and lambda interpolates the whole geometric spectrum instead of the current max(MC, 1-step-Bellman) two-point choice. Directly addresses the nstep=1 status quo and finding 5.

#### Conservative Q-Learning for Offline Reinforcement Learning (CQL) — Kumar, Zhou, Tucker, Levine, 2020 (NeurIPS) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04779>
- **方法**：CQL augments the Bellman error with alpha * (logsumexp_a Q(s,a) - E_{a~dataset} Q(s,a)): push down a soft-maximum of Q over ALL actions while pushing up Q at dataset actions. CQL(H) is the entropy-regularized variant whose inner distribution has closed form mu(a|s) proportional to exp(Q(s,a)), so the push-down gradient concentrates on the currently highest-valued (most dangerously overestimated) actions. Theorem 3.2: the expected Q under the learned policy lower-bounds the true policy value, preventing OOD overestimation from corrupting the bootstrap. alpha is tuned by Lagrangian dual descent against a target conservatism gap (fixed constant in discrete domains).
- **为何有效**：Offline value estimates fail because the bootstrap max queries OOD actions whose errors are never corrected by data; CQL suppresses exactly those values, and the logsumexp form self-targets: whichever OOD action currently has the highest Q receives the largest push-down, while dataset actions are anchored by data. The Lagrange mechanism keeps the conservatism adaptive rather than fixed.
- **可借鉴**: Our counterfactual-sibling-bin floor IS CQL taken to infinite strength — every non-executed sibling gets pinned to a fixed task-minimum distribution, which manufactures the fake 0.89 Q-gap and coin-flip sibling ordering of finding 1. Soft version: per (timestep, dim, level), add alpha * (logsumexp over the 5 bins of mean-Q - Q(executed bin)) to the C51 loss instead of (or blended with) the hard floor distribution. This buys (a) push-down proportional to actual overestimation, so siblings the online policy genuinely visits can rise and true return ordering among siblings can survive (improving our sign-accuracy probes), and (b) a Lagrange alpha targeting a desired top1-top2 margin — turning finding 2's measured margin decay (0.356 to 0.066) into a directly controlled quantity.

#### EMaQ: Expected-Max Q-Learning Operator for Simple Yet Effective Offline and Online RL — Ghasemipour, Schuurmans, Gu, 2021 (ICML) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.11091>
- **方法**：EMaQ strips BCQ to one clean operator: the bootstrap target is E[max_{i=1..N} Q(s', a_i)] with a_1..a_N sampled from an AUTOREGRESSIVE generative model of the behavior policy; no perturbation network, no explicit penalty. N is the single conservatism knob: N=1 evaluates the behavior policy (pure BC-value), N to infinity recovers max over the behavior support (standard Q-learning restricted to support). The authors derive sub-optimality bounds in terms of N and the behavior model quality, giving a complexity measure for offline problems, and match/beat D4RL SOTA while staying SAC-competitive online.
- **为何有效**：The expected-max over N behavior samples never queries an action outside behavior support (no extrapolation), and the expectation over sample sets softens the max: it is a smooth, tunable interpolation between evaluation and improvement, so pessimism comes structurally from the operator rather than from a penalty whose scale must be tuned against the reward scale.
- **可借鉴**: The most structurally natural fix for finding 2's exponential margin sensitivity: coarse-to-fine bin selection IS an autoregressive generative process (each level conditions on the chosen coarser bin), so a lightweight autoregressive bin prior over (timestep, dim, level) trained on demos + successful replay directly plays EMaQ's proposal role. Replace the bootstrap's full factored argmax over ~240 decisions with 'sample N complete chunks from the prior, take E[max] of their Q' — the target then never depends on 240 fragile independent per-factor margins or on any OOD sibling bin, and N anneals pessimism online without our hard floor. This is a proposal mechanism with Q still deciding (flagged: it adds a generative net, but argmax/max-Q remains the decision rule).

#### DR3: Value-Based Deep RL Requires Explicit Regularization — Kumar, Agarwal, Ma, Courville, Tucker, Levine, 2022 (ICLR) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04716>
- **方法**：DR3 shows the implicit regularization of SGD, benign in supervised learning, is actively harmful in offline TD: bootstrapping drives FEATURE CO-ADAPTATION, where the penultimate-layer features of (s,a) and its bootstrap successor (s',a') become aligned/aliased, their dot product growing through training. On narrow data this yields feature rank collapse, aliased representations across the Bellman chain, and Q divergence/unlearning late in training. The fix is an explicit counter-regularizer: add beta * phi(s,a) . phi(s',a') (dot product of consecutive state-action features) to the TD loss, penalizing the alignment the implicit regularizer creates. Improves stability and performance on Atari, D4RL, and image-based robotic manipulation.
- **为何有效**：TD's targets are generated by the same network being trained, so gradient descent can cheat by making successor features point in the same direction as current features — shrinking TD error without learning value structure. On 60 demos there is almost no data diversity to oppose this, so the representation collapses onto whatever cheap solution fits the dominant loss; an explicit dot-product penalty removes the cheat.
- **可借鉴**: A representational hypothesis and fix for finding 1 that no target-side change addresses: with lambda=1.0 the margin loss is the dominant, easiest-to-satisfy objective, and co-adapted/rank-collapsed features would explain why sibling bins share aliased representations that encode manifold-membership but cannot support return ordering (0.45-0.57 probe accuracy). Transplant: add beta * phi(s_t) . phi(s_{t+1}) on the sequence-critic torso's per-timestep features (before the dueling/level heads), plus track feature rank (srank) of demo-state features as a probe — cheap to try, orthogonal to every target-side fix in this category, and specifically indicated by our 60-demo narrow-data regime.

#### Efficient Online Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning Need Not Retain Offline Data (WSRL) — Zhou, Peng, Li, Levine, Kumar, 2024 (ICLR 2025) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07762>
- **方法**：WSRL diagnoses why offline-to-online fine-tuning stalls: retaining offline data in the online buffer both slows learning and imports offline pessimism, while dropping it naively causes a sudden Q-divergence — the distribution mismatch between offline data and online rollouts makes early online TD updates unlearn the pretrained values. The fix is a WARMUP phase: before any fine-tuning gradients, roll out the frozen pretrained policy for a small number of episodes to seed the online buffer with on-distribution transitions; these 'recalibrate' the Q-function to the online distribution. Then run standard high-UTD online RL on online data only, retaining no offline data, learning faster and reaching higher performance than retention-based methods.
- **为何有效**：The first online batches determine whether the pretrained value function survives contact with its own induced distribution: if they come from a mismatched mixture (stale offline data + tiny online sample), TD error concentrates on the mismatch and overwrites the pretrained structure. Seeding the buffer with the pretrained policy's OWN rollouts makes the first targets self-consistent, so fine-tuning refines instead of resets.
- **可借鉴**: Directly targets finding 2's measured mechanism (top1-top2 margin decaying 0.356 to 0.066 on states the ONLINE policy visits — that is WSRL's distribution-mismatch unlearning, observed at bin-margin granularity). Transplant into the no-BC recipe at the offline-to-online boundary: after the 10k demo-only phase, collect a warmup batch of episodes with the frozen greedy argmax policy (no exploration noise, no gradient updates), push them into the online buffer, and only then resume training — optionally re-examining whether the frozen expert replay buffer is still needed at all afterward, per WSRL's no-retention result. Near-zero implementation cost and composable with Cal-QL-style floor calibration.
- *另见类别*：demo-driven RL with NO explicit BC loss

#### Making Efficient Use of Demonstrations to Solve Hard Exploration Problems (R2D3) — Paine, Gulcehre, et al., 2019 (ICLR 2020) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01387>
- **方法**：R2D3 is R2D2 (recurrent LSTM Q-network, 80-step sequences with 40-step burn-in, dueling heads, prioritized replay) with two replay buffers: each batch element is drawn from the expert-demo buffer with probability rho (the 'demo ratio') and from agent experience with probability 1-rho, using n-step double Q-learning with n=5. The stochastic per-element sampling lets rho be far smaller than 1/batch-size.
- **为何有效**：Demos are needed only as a seed of successful-outcome transitions the agent cannot find by exploration; once online success data exists, over-sampling demos amplifies their off-policyness (stale action distribution, narrow states) and overfits the critic to expert idiosyncrasies.
- **可借鉴**: Tuning guidance for our frozen expert replay buffer in the no-BC recipe (findings 4 and 5): sweep the demo:online sampling ratio DOWN aggressively — CQN-AS-style implementations commonly sample demos at 25-50%, and R2D3's evidence says the optimum can be two orders of magnitude lower once online successes accumulate; consider annealing rho with the online success count. Also a second external vote (with DQfD, Rainbow) that a sequence/recurrent critic on demo-driven sparse-reward tasks should use uncorrected n-step of about 5, not 1.
- *另见类别*：demo-driven RL WITH explicit imitation regularization

#### Safe and Efficient Off-Policy Reinforcement Learning (Retrace(lambda)) — Munos, Stepleton, Harutyunyan, Bellemare, 2016 (NIPS) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.02647>
- **方法**：Retrace(lambda) is a return-based off-policy operator: R Q(x,a) = Q(x,a) + E_mu[sum_t gamma^t (c_1...c_t)(r_t + gamma E_pi Q(x_{t+1},.) - Q(x_t,a_t))] with truncated importance-weight traces c_s = lambda * min(1, pi(a_s|x_s)/mu(a_s|x_s)). The truncation at 1 kills the variance explosion of raw importance-sampling products, while (unlike tree-backup's c_s = lambda*pi(a_s|x_s)) it keeps full traces when behavior is near the target policy.
- **为何有效**：Multi-step returns propagate reward information many steps per update instead of one bootstrap hop, but naive off-policy use either explodes in variance (IS) or converges to the wrong fixed point.
- **可借鉴**: Mostly a cautionary calibration for our nstep>1 move, addressing finding 5 (demos highly off-policy, nstep=1 status quo): with our deterministic argmax target policy, pi(a|s) is a 0/1 indicator, so Retrace's c_s cuts the trace at the FIRST demo step where the current critic's argmax disagrees with the demo action — i.e. safe correction deletes multi-step exactly on the demo transitions we most need it for (early training, off-manifold critic). Conclusion: do NOT transplant Retrace for demo transitions; use uncorrected n-step there (see Peng's Q(lambda)/DQfD) and reserve trace-cutting, if at all, for stale online replay where the argmax has drifted.

#### Rainbow: Combining Improvements in Deep Reinforcement Learning — Hessel et al., 2018 (AAAI) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02298>
- **方法**：Rainbow combines six DQN improvements — double Q, prioritized replay, dueling, multi-step returns, C51 distributional learning, noisy nets — with n=3 uncorrected multi-step returns. The multi-step distributional target shifts the C51 support by the truncated n-step reward sum, then L2-projects the shifted-and-shrunk (gamma^n) support onto the fixed atoms before the KL loss.
- **为何有效**：n-step returns shorten the bootstrap chain by a factor of n, which both accelerates reward propagation and reduces the number of times target-network error is compounded; combined with a distributional critic this matters doubly because the projected target distribution is only as good as the bootstrap it is built from.
- **可借鉴**: The exact mechanical recipe for taking our C51 critic off nstep=1: reward-sum support shift + gamma^n shrink + projection, unchanged dueling/atoms. One CQN-AS-specific check it forces (finding 3): with q_reward_scale=0.07 and support [-2,2], an n-step sum of up to n consecutive scaled 1.0 rewards must stay clear of the top atom — for n=3-5 headroom is fine (0.21-0.35 vs 2.0 minus bootstrap term), but validate against the ~24-28-step success tail so the fix for saturation is not silently undone. Also implies our currently-absent prioritized replay is the other highest-leverage standard component.

#### Off-Policy Deep Reinforcement Learning without Exploration (BCQ) — Fujimoto, Meger, Precup, 2019 (ICML) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.02900>
- **方法**：BCQ names and attacks EXTRAPOLATION ERROR: on fixed batches, the bootstrap max evaluates actions absent from the data, whose fabricated values then propagate through TD. Continuous BCQ trains a conditional VAE on the batch to generate candidate actions near the data, a small perturbation network to locally adjust them, and restricts both the policy and the bootstrap max to these data-supported candidates, forcing the agent 'close to on-policy with respect to a subset of the given data'.
- **为何有效**：TD error on in-support actions is corrected by data; on out-of-support actions it is never corrected and is systematically inflated by the max.
- **可借鉴**: Our 'candidate backup from replay argmax' is instance-based discrete BCQ; what the generative version buys is STATE-CONDITIONED support generalization: train a small per-(timestep, dim, level) bin classifier pi_b on demos + successful replay, and mask the bootstrap argmax to bins with pi_b >= tau * max (tau anneals toward 0 online). This addresses bootstrap corruption from OOD sibling bins (findings 1-2) at states never literally seen in replay, where our replay-argmax candidates do not exist, and it composes with, rather than replaces, the pessimism floor. Note pi_b is a proposal/masking prior only — argmax-Q still decides, so it stays inside the critic-only constraint.


### 1.7 2025–2026 前沿（action-chunking RL、demo 进价值空间）

#### Long-Horizon Q-Learning: Accurate Value Learning via n-Step Inequalities (LQL) — Abraham, Shi & Finn, 2026 (arXiv, May 2026) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05812>
- **方法**：Augments standard 1-step TD with hinge-squared penalties enforcing two families of optimality inequalities over an L-step trajectory window. Lower bound: Q(s_k,a_k) must be at least the realized discounted partial return G_{k:l} plus gamma^(l-k) * Q_target(s_l, a*_l) for offsets l >= k+2 (any realized action sequence lower-bounds what the optimal policy achieves). Upper bound: the greedy value Q(s_i,a*_i) at an earlier state must be at least G_{i:k} + gamma^(k-i) * Q(s_k,a_k) (switching to optimal behavior earlier cannot underperform the observed continuation). Penalties are one-sided squared hinges delta_LB = [G + gamma^n Q_target - Q]_+^2 and the symmetric UB version, computed from network outputs already produced for the TD error — no extra networks or forward passes, ~4.7% per-update slowdown. Combined loss is TD + lambda_UB*L_UB + lambda_LB*L_LB with untuned lambda=1. On sparse-reward humanoidmaze-giant: 75.7% success vs 0% for 1-step TD, and n-step TD degrades as n grows (off-policy bias) while LQL does not.
- **为何有效**：The LB inequality injects Monte-Carlo-grade long-horizon signal without n-step's off-policy bias, because it is a one-sided constraint: realized returns only push Q up when Q underestimates, never drag it down to the behavior policy's value. The UB inequality propagates value backward along the trajectory even when bootstrapped chains would take many updates. Together they act as a principled backstop against compounding TD error on long-horizon sparse-reward tasks with heterogeneous (off-policy) data — exactly the demo+online mixture regime.
- **可借鉴**: Direct, principled replacement for our ad-hoc max(MC, Bellman) target in the no-BC dense reward-Q recipe (finding 4): apply the hinge LB penalty to demo and positive-return replay sequences and the hinge UB penalty along all stored trajectories, on top of the existing C51 TD loss (as hinges on the per-bin Q expectation, or on projected distributional targets). CQN-AS's sequence critic already emits per-timestep Q over the K-step chunk, so in-chunk (k,l) pairs come nearly free from one forward pass. Addresses findings 1-2 (long-horizon return signal without any BC/margin term poisoning bin ordering), finding 4 (formalizes the MC lower bound with a one-sided loss instead of a hard max), and finding 5 (demos highly off-policy: the one-sided constraint is exactly the safe way to use them at nstep>1).

#### Accelerating Q-learning through Efficient Value-Sharing across Actions (Mean-Expansion Layer) — Nagarajan, Daley, White & Machado, 2026 (ICML 2026 Spotlight) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29806>
- **方法**：A parameter-free final layer for Q-networks: given the penultimate residual vector z (one entry per action), it computes q = (I + (k/n)J) z, i.e. it extracts the mean across actions, scales it by (k+1) (typically k = n = number of actions), and adds it back to each entry. The network thus learns a low-norm residual encoding only RELATIVE action differences, while the shared state-value baseline is implicitly amplified rather than fit per-action. A small penalty on the mean magnitude constrains value scale. Because the mean couples all outputs, each TD error is distributed across all sibling actions instead of updating one action independently. Applied to DQN and to distributional IQN (as final output layer); improves aggregate performance on 57 Atari games, increases relative action gaps in the vast majority of environments, and dramatically reduces value overestimation; IB-IQN reaches IQN's peak performance in ~67% of the training time.
- **为何有效**：Most of a Q-head's capacity normally goes to fitting a large baseline value common to all actions; near-identical sibling outputs then have tiny, noise-dominated differences — small action gaps. Factoring the baseline out architecturally means the learned residual IS the action gap, so gradient signal concentrates on the decision-relevant quantity; distributing TD errors across siblings also tempers outlier increases in the max action-value, cutting bootstrapping overestimation.
- **可借鉴**: Insert a mean-expansion layer on every factored 5-bin head of the CQN-AS critic (per action-dim, per level, per chunk timestep) so each 5-way decision learns a shared sibling baseline plus low-norm relative offsets. This is the most direct architectural attack on finding 2: the measured top1-top2 margin decay (0.356 to 0.066) off the demo manifold is precisely the small-action-gap failure this layer counteracts, and with ~240 factored argmax decisions per chunk the exponential sensitivity to per-factor gaps makes gap enlargement disproportionately valuable. Caveat to flag: our critic is C51, not scalar — apply the layer per-atom-logit or on the implied Q-means (the paper only validated scalar DQN and quantile IQN, not categorical C51).

#### SEAR: Sample Efficient Action Chunking Reinforcement Learning — Nagy, Celik, Gospodinov, Seligmann, Liao, Kaushik & Neumann, 2026 (arXiv, March 2026) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01891>
- **方法**：A chunked off-policy RL method built around a causal-transformer critic mapping (state, action chunk of length N) to N Q-values, one per chunk PREFIX. From a single sampled segment it computes N multi-horizon TD targets: for each prefix length k, G^(k) = sum of k discounted rewards + gamma^k * Q^(N)(s_{t+k}, next full chunk), and the critic loss averages squared errors over all k. During training only random-length prefixes are executed before replanning (receding horizon), improving state coverage; at eval it executes k < N actions then replans. The actor is a SAC-style max-entropy policy over chunks (reverse-KL to exponentiated Q). Explicitly compares against CQN-AS: >20% IQM success improvement on Metaworld's 20 hardest tasks, chunk size 10 optimal, also beats SimbaV2.
- **为何有效**：A monolithic Q over a whole chunk is hard to fit and wastes the reward decomposition inside the chunk; per-prefix targets turn one transition into N unbiased backups of different horizons, giving dense learning signal and letting long-horizon prefixes propagate sparse rewards fast while short prefixes stay accurate near contact. Random-length replanning breaks the open-loop commitment that makes chunked policies fragile, and diversifies visited states.
- **可借鉴**: Transplant the critic-side machinery (the actor is severable — flag: SEAR itself is actor-critic): (a) train the CQN-AS sequence critic with per-prefix multi-horizon TD targets instead of our current nstep=1 backup — each replay chunk yields K backups at horizons 1..K, accelerating sparse-reward propagation (findings 3-5) without off-policy n-step bias in the demo buffer becoming a correction problem (rewards inside the chunk were actually executed); (b) adopt random-length prefix execution online so the replay distribution covers replan states, directly attacking finding 2 (margin collapse on online-policy states comes partly from never training on the states the open-loop chunk creates). This is the strongest published head-to-head evidence that CQN-AS's target structure, not its critic-only premise, is the bottleneck.

#### Fill in the Blanks: Accelerating Q-Learning with a Handful of Demonstrations in Sparse Reward Settings (SODA) — Basiri Azad & Boedecker, 2025 (arXiv, October 2025) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24432>
- **方法**：Uses a handful of successful demos to precompute Monte Carlo value estimates — for sparse terminal reward, V(s_t) = gamma^(T-t) along each demo — and installs them as warm-start Q targets for the demonstrated state-action pairs, Q(s_t,a_t) <- V(s_t), before standard TD learning refines them online. Non-demonstrated actions keep their default initialization (no margin pushing them down, no imitation term).
- **为何有效**：In sparse-reward tasks the bottleneck is that positive value information exists only at the end of rare successful trajectories; writing demo MC returns directly into Q gives the bootstrapping chain correct anchors from step one, collapsing the exploration burden, while leaving counterfactual actions untouched avoids fabricating preference structure the data cannot support.
- **可借鉴**: Primarily independent validation of our finding-4 recipe from a different group: demo-MC-as-value-target + distributional head + separate frozen demo buffer is almost exactly our offline dense reward-Q phase, arrived at from the same diagnosis of DQfD margins. Incremental transplants: their gamma^(T-t) closed-form target is a cleaner init than our max(MC, Bellman) for the demo-executed bins (composable with q_reward_scale=0.07 from finding 3), and their 'warm-start then let TD overwrite' schedule is a tested alternative to keeping the floor loss active online. Medium because it confirms direction more than it adds machinery — and its benchmarks are far simpler than BiGym.

#### Robot Critics that Sweat the Small Stuff — Sudhakar, Liang, Rammohan, Tokmakov, Zemel & Vondrick, 2026 (arXiv, June 2026, Columbia + TRI) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21572>
- **方法**：Shows off-the-shelf VLM critics discriminate coarse task states but fail at fine-grained progress (55-72% success/failure detection, near chance), then fine-tunes VLM critics with PAIRWISE progress supervision: cross-entropy on binary comparisons 'which frame reflects greater progress', using success-success consecutive-frame pairs (forward progress) and matched-initial-condition success-failure pairs (policy-specific failure modes). This yields >90% discrimination accuracy.
- **为何有效**：Absolute scalar value regression is poorly conditioned for fine discrimination — the supervision never forces the model to rank near-identical alternatives.
- **可借鉴**: The VLM/video-model stack is not transplantable to our budget, but the loss design maps directly onto finding 1: our critic's sibling-bin return-ORDERING is coin-flip (0.45-0.57) while it hallucinates 0.89 Q-gaps — the signature of a critic never trained on ordering. Add an auxiliary pairwise ranking loss to the CQN-AS critic: sample pairs of sibling bins (or candidate chunks) whose executed outcomes exist in replay with different MC returns, and apply a comparison cross-entropy on which has higher return, alongside the C51 TD loss. Their matched-pair recipe translates to same-state pairs from replay/relabeled rollouts. This trains exactly the quantity the 240-way composite argmax consumes (relative order), complementing the mean-expansion layer's architectural gap enlargement.

#### Adaptive Action Chunking via Multi-Chunk Q Value Estimation (ACH) — Shin, Chae, Kim, Park & Sung, 2026 (arXiv, May 2026) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10044>
- **方法**：An offline-to-online RL successor to Q-chunking (cites CQN-AS) addressing the fixed-chunk-length limitation: a Transformer critic simultaneously estimates action-values for ALL candidate chunk lengths in a single forward pass, and the agent selects the most effective chunk length per state, dynamically, during both training and inference. Evaluated on 34 tasks; consistently outperforms fixed-length Q-chunking baselines with improved generalization and learning efficiency.
- **为何有效**：The optimal commitment horizon varies by state: long chunks give temporally coherent exploration and better credit assignment in free space, short chunks give reactivity near contact.
- **可借鉴**: CQN-AS already outputs per-timestep Q over the K-step chunk, so multi-length value heads are nearly free: add per-prefix-length aggregate value estimates and choose the executed prefix length per state at inference (execute k <= K then replan). This shrinks the number of open-loop factored argmax decisions actually executed, directly reducing exposure to finding 2's exponential sensitivity (fewer simultaneous per-factor margins have to be right), and gives reactive short chunks near contact where BiGym failures concentrate. Overlaps with SEAR's receding horizon — treat ACH as the per-state length-SELECTION mechanism on top of SEAR-style prefix targets.

#### Adaptive Q-Chunking for Offline-to-Online Reinforcement Learning (AQC) — Gireesh, Ju & Wang, 2026 (arXiv, May 2026) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05544>
- **方法**：Companion problem to ACH, with the key technical contribution being HOW to compare values across chunk sizes: naive comparison of Q-values across horizons collapses to the shortest chunk because of discount-scale mismatch (gamma^k differs per horizon), and degrades in low-value states. AQC trains critics for multiple chunk sizes and selects per state by comparing each chunk size's ADVANTAGE relative to a per-horizon baseline, normalized by the discount factor — unbiased (non-discriminative) when no temporal-scale signal exists, discriminative when one horizon genuinely plans better.
- **为何有效**：Raw Q magnitudes confound 'this horizon is better' with 'this horizon's values live on a different discount scale and this state is low-value', so argmax-over-horizons systematically picks short chunks.
- **可借鉴**: The narrow but load-bearing piece for any adaptive-length CQN-AS variant (and for our existing diagnostics): per-horizon baselined, discount-normalized advantage comparison. If we adopt ACH/SEAR-style variable prefix execution, use AQC's normalized advantage — not raw per-timestep Q — to pick the executed prefix length; the same correction applies whenever we compare Q across our critic's K per-timestep heads (e.g., in value-fidelity probes like scripts/analyze_cqn_value_fidelity.py), where discount-scale mismatch could masquerade as temporal value structure. Indirectly serves finding 2 via the same shorter-effective-open-loop argument as ACH; no relevance to findings 1/3/4 directly.
- *另见类别*：off-policy targets, multi-step returns, and pessimism


### 1.8 补检（gap critic 指定）

#### Cal-QL: Calibrated Offline RL Pre-Training for Efficient Online Fine-Tuning — Nakamoto et al., 2023 (NeurIPS) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05479>
- **方法**：A one-line modification to CQL for the offline-pretrain → online-finetune setting. Standard CQL minimizes alpha·(E_{a~pi}[Q(s,a)] − E_{a~data}[Q(s,a)]) + TD error; Cal-QL replaces the pushdown term with E_{a~pi}[max(Q(s,a), V^mu(s))], where V^mu is the value of a reference (behavior) policy — i.e., the conservative pushdown on non-dataset actions is MASKED whenever Q has already fallen below what the behavior policy actually achieves. V^mu is estimated with no extra network on sparse-reward terminal tasks: it is simply the Monte Carlo return-to-go from the dataset (a regressed MC network only for infinite-horizon locomotion). The result is a Q-function that is provably both a lower bound on the learned policy's value and an upper bound on the reference policy's value ('calibrated'). Outperforms prior methods on 9/11 fine-tuning benchmarks including sparse-reward Adroit and visual pick-and-place.
- **为何有效**：They measure exactly why naive conservative pretraining dips when going online: CQL learns Q-values far below ground truth, so when online data introduces new state-actions at a realistic scale, actions worse than the offline policy spuriously rank higher and the policy unlearns its initialization before recovering. Flooring the pushdown at the behavior policy's MC return keeps the Q scale anchored to real returns, so the offline ordering survives contact with online data and no recovery dip occurs.
- **可借鉴**: Directly answers the recipe's most ad-hoc choice — 'dense floor only on positive-return trajectories online' (finding 4) — with a principled rule: keep the counterfactual-sibling floor everywhere, but clamp it so a sibling bin is never pushed BELOW the state's demo/behavior MC return-to-go, i.e., replace the fixed task-minimum floor distribution with floor(s) = max(task-minimum, V^behavior(s)) projected onto C51 atoms, where V^behavior is the MC return-to-go we already compute for the max(MC, Bellman) target. This targets failure mode 2 (collapse at the offline→online seam: our margin decay 0.356→0.066 is their measured unlearning dip in factored-argmax form) and failure mode 1 (a calibrated floor keeps sibling Q-gaps commensurate with true return gaps instead of inventing 0.89 gaps where truth is 0.009). Cost is one line in the sibling-floor target construction; no new networks.
- *另见类别*：demo-driven RL with NO explicit BC loss

#### Q-Transformer: Scalable Offline Reinforcement Learning via Autoregressive Q-Functions — Chebotar et al., 2023 (CoRL) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10150>
- **方法**：Critic-only Q-learning on real-robot manipulation: each of 8 action dims is discretized into 256 bins and treated as its own 'timestep' — a Transformer (RT-1-style FiLM EfficientNet) emits per-bin Q-values, sigmoid-normalized to [0,1], trained with MSE TD loss (EMA target net, gamma=0.98, sparse terminal 0/1 reward). The Bellman update is autoregressive: for dim i<d_A the target is max over the NEXT dim's bins of Q(s, a^{1:i}, a^{i+1}) with discount 1.0 within the timestep; only the last dim bootstraps R + gamma·max_{a_{t+1}^1} Q(next state). A conservative regularizer adds alpha/2·E_{unseen bins}[(Q−0)^2] — every bin not taken in the dataset is regressed toward the minimal attainable cumulative reward (0), with alpha=1.0 untuned and never annealed; they explicitly reject softmax-based CQL. The TD target is additionally floored by the Monte Carlo return-to-go: target = max(MC_{t:T}, Bellman target). An n-step variant skips the intermediate per-dim maximizations and targets the last dim of the next timestep directly, reaching equal performance with 4x fewer gradient steps. Trained fully offline on 38k demos + 20k FAILED autonomous episodes; a tabular analysis shows the regularizer acts as a weighted Bellman backup whose weight is ~0 for out-of-distribution actions.
- **为何有效**：The quadratic floor anchors every counterfactual bin at the true worst-case return instead of pushing it to −inf or entangling it with dataset-action values the way softmax CQL does — so dataset actions win the argmax by exactly their advantage over 'do nothing useful', and Q retains return semantics rather than density semantics. Their ablations are causal evidence: no conservatism → collapse via overestimation; softmax CQL → success rate collapses to roughly the demo fraction of the data (~8%), i.e., the critic degenerates into a behavior-density model — precisely our measured BC-margin pathology. The MC floor injects ground-truth return signal at episode ends before bootstrapping has propagated, and the sigmoid [0,1] normalization guarantees returns fit the value support.
- **可借鉴**: This is the published validation of finding-4's sealed recipe (counterfactual-sibling floor ≈ their regularizer; max(MC, Bellman) ≈ their MC-floored target) plus three tested answers the recipe lacks. (a) Keep the floor ALWAYS ON at fixed weight on ALL data — including failed online episodes — rather than only on positive-return trajectories; their 20k failed episodes improved the policy 70% over IL under an always-on floor, directly attacking failure mode 2 (off-manifold margin decay: siblings stay pinned at floor on states the online policy visits, so the top1−top2 gap cannot decay to 0.066). (b) Their softmax-CQL ablation says: never re-introduce a margin/softmax-shaped penalty — it recreates failure mode 1 (Q encodes demo density, not return). (c) Replace nstep=1 with their dimension-skipping n-step target — safe under the floor because the floor bounds intermediate-maximization overestimation — to speed sparse-reward propagation given our off-policy demos (finding 5). (d) Their within-timestep autoregressive conditioning (later dims see chosen earlier bins) is the principled fix for our ~240-way independent factored argmax whose joint-max guarantee CQN-AS currently lacks; CQN-AS already conditions across levels, extending it across dims of a timestep is the direct transplant. (e) Their sigmoid [0,1] Q with terminal 0/1 reward is the non-distributional analog of the q_reward_scale=0.07 fix for C51 saturation (finding 3): pick the scale so max return sits strictly inside the support.

#### Q-Learning in Enormous Action Spaces via Amortized Approximate Maximization (AQL) — Van de Wiele et al., 2020 (DeepMind, arXiv 2001.08116) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08116>
- **方法**：Replaces exact/factored argmax in Q-learning with a stochastic search over a small candidate set: at each state, sample N=100 joint actions from a learned autoregressive proposal mu(a|s) = prod_d mu_d(a_d | s, a_<d) (softmax per discretized sub-action, or fixed-variance Gaussian) plus M=400 cheap uniform actions, then argmax the action-in Q-network over the union. The proposal is trained by supervised amortization: NLL of the best action found by the search, minus an entropy bonus lambda*H(mu) to prevent collapse (L = -log mu(a*(s)|s) - lambda*H). The TD target uses the same approximate maximizer at s': L(Q) = (r + gamma*Q_target(s', a*(s')) - Q(s,a))^2. No policy gradient anywhere; the proposal is pure supervised learning and argmax-Q always decides. On 39 DM Control tasks with 5^D discretization it beat D3PG and QT-Opt (CEM with K=100, 10 elites, 3 iterations), especially on 21-D humanoid; ablations show the learned proposal clearly beats uniform sampling and CEM in high dimensions, and discretized AQL beats continuous-Gaussian AQL.
- **为何有效**：A composed per-dimension argmax (or CEM's unconditioned sampler) must get every factor right independently, and errors compound; AQL instead makes ONE comparison among ~500 fully-specified joint actions, so the decision margin is the joint Q-gap between whole candidates rather than a product of per-factor margins. Amortization makes the search improve over time: the proposal caches where good actions were previously found, concentrating the tiny sample budget on the high-value manifold, which is why it beats fixed search procedures (uniform, CEM) as dimensionality grows.
- **可借鉴**: Directly attacks Finding 2's mechanism. Keep the coarse-to-fine discretization (AQL's own ablation says discretized bins > continuous proposals) but change DECISION TIME and the TD-target max: instead of composing ~240 independent per-level/per-dim/per-timestep bin argmaxes, sample N whole action-sequence candidates (full K-step chunks, each a complete bin assignment decoded coarse-to-fine) from an autoregressive proposal trained by NLL-of-best-found-candidate + entropy, mix in replay actions and the demo action as free candidates, and pick the chunk with highest joint sequence-Q. The next-state bootstrap becomes max over the same candidate set — which subsumes and formalizes the recipe's ad-hoc 'candidate backup from replay argmax' (the executed replay action is just one guaranteed candidate). This converts the exponentially-fragile 240-factor margin problem (0.356->0.066 collapse off-manifold) into a single joint comparison. BORDERLINE-ACTOR FLAG: the proposal is a policy-shaped network, but it is trained by supervised amortization (not policy gradient) and argmax-Q always decides — squarely inside the carve-out. Caveat from Finding 1: candidate ranking only helps if Q carries return ordering, so this must ride on the no-BC return-trained critic (recipe 4); with the BC-margin critic it would just re-select demo-frequent chunks.

#### Learning in Complex Action Spaces Without Policy Gradients (QMLE) — Tavakoli et al., 2025 (TMLR; arXiv 2410.06317) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06317>
- **方法**：Modern successor to AQL that distills three principles letting value-based methods match policy-gradient methods in complex action spaces: (1) Monte Carlo approximation of the max via sampled candidates, (2) amortized maximization via MLE — an ensemble of k argmax-predictor networks f_theta(.|s) trained by log-likelihood on the best argmax approximation found so far, (3) action-in architectures (Q takes (s,a) as input for generalization across the joint space). Candidates are drawn from a mixture (their Eq. 21): proportion rho_0 uniform, rho_1..rho_k from the ensemble predictors, PLUS the previously cached best argmax for that transition, which is stored in the replay buffer and updated whenever a better candidate is found — so the approximate max improves monotonically per transition. Separate budgets m_greedy (acting) and m_target (TD target: r + gamma*Q_target(s', argmax over m_target candidates)). Reports superior performance to QT-Opt and AQL, matches/beats DDPG on 14/16 DM Control tasks up to 38 action dimensions, competitive with DMPO except in the highest dimensions.
- **为何有效**：The replay-cached argmax converts a one-shot stochastic search into an anytime search amortized over revisits: undersampling in any single update is dampened because past search effort is never thrown away, so TD targets stop being systematically underestimated by a lucky-sample-dependent max. The predictor ensemble plus uniform mixing keeps candidate diversity, avoiding the proposal-collapse failure of a single amortized policy.
- **可借鉴**: This is the literature-formalized version of the recipe's 'candidate backup from replay argmax' (recipe 4), with two concrete upgrades to transplant: (a) store per-transition the best action-sequence candidate found so far (initialize with the executed chunk / demo chunk) and update it whenever any candidate scores higher under the current critic — use it both as a guaranteed TD-target candidate and as a decision-time candidate, making the approximate max monotone instead of ad-hoc; (b) sample remaining candidates from a small ENSEMBLE of proposal heads plus a uniform-over-bins component, addressing Finding 2 (joint scoring instead of 240 composed factors) while hedging against proposal collapse onto the demo manifold. Secondary borrow: the action-in principle suggests adding an action-in re-scoring head (Q(s, full chunk) as input) alongside the existing action-out per-bin heads — the action-out factored head is exactly what makes CQN-AS unable to score joint candidates coherently. Same Finding 1 caveat as AQL: needs the return-trained (no-BC) critic to rank candidates meaningfully.

#### IDQL: Implicit Q-Learning as an Actor-Critic Method with Diffusion Policies — Hansen-Estruch et al., 2023 (arXiv 2304.10573) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10573>
- **方法**：Critic side: Q is trained ONLY on dataset actions with L_Q = (r + gamma*V_psi(s') - Q(s,a))^2, where V is trained by expectile regression of Q on in-sample (s,a) — the critic never evaluates an action the dataset does not contain, so no argmax over learned Q appears in any training target. Theory side: each asymmetric V-loss (expectile tau, quantile tau, exponential alpha) is shown to induce an implicit actor = behavior policy reweighted by a specific w(s,a) (e.g. expectiles give w = |tau - 1(Q(s,a) < V(s))|). Acting side: fit an expressive diffusion model to the behavior data by pure imitation, sample N candidates per state (N=32-128), and select — stochastically by normalized w during training, hard argmax_a_i Q(s,a_i) at eval. Beats IQL/CQL on D4RL (1213.8 total vs 1070.4/1002.1), with large gains on sparse-reward antmaze (474.6 vs 378.0) and low hyperparameter sensitivity.
- **为何有效**：It cleanly decouples the two jobs our single BC-margin critic conflates: a density/manifold model (the diffusion BC proposal) says WHAT actions are plausible, and a return-only critic says WHICH is best. Because the critic is trained purely in-sample, its values are never queried where it has no data, so the off-manifold value pathology never enters the targets — yet best-of-N over the proposal still extracts a policy better than the behavior data, since the proposal covers the data manifold and Q only has to rank on-manifold candidates, where it is reliable.
- **可借鉴**: Two transplants. (a) Target replacement addressing Findings 1+2 jointly: replace the next-state coarse-to-fine argmax bootstrap (which queries Q at off-manifold composed argmax chunks whose margins collapse, 0.356->0.066) with an expectile-V bootstrap r + gamma*V_tau(s') trained in-sample only — this removes the very OOD queries the DQfD margin loss exists to immunize, i.e., it substitutes for the BC-margin crutch instead of fighting it (C51 caveat: expectile regression on a categorical distribution is nontrivial; start with a scalar V head next to the C51 heads, keeping q_reward_scale=0.07 per Finding 3). (b) Role decomposition exploiting Finding 1 as a feature: our measurement says the BC-margin critic IS a demo-manifold membership model — exactly IDQL's proposal role. Keep it (or a BC chunk model) purely as the N-candidate proposal over action sequences, and let a separately trained no-BC return-critic (recipe 4) do the argmax over those N chunks. BORDERLINE-ACTOR FLAG: the diffusion/BC net is a proposal-prior only and argmax-Q decides at eval — inside the carve-out, but it is a generative policy network in all but name.

#### Learning values across many orders of magnitude (Pop-Art) — van Hasselt et al., 2016 (NeurIPS) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07714>
- **方法**：The value head regresses targets in normalized space: Y_tilde = (Y - mu)/sigma, where mu and sigma are EMA statistics of the targets themselves, tracked separately from the network (mu_t = (1-beta)mu_{t-1} + beta*Y_t; second moment nu_t updated the same way; sigma^2 = nu - mu^2). Every step, BEFORE the gradient update, the last linear layer is rescaled exactly so all unnormalized outputs are preserved under the new stats: W <- (sigma_old/sigma_new)W, b <- (sigma_old*b + mu_old - mu_new)/sigma_new (the 'POP' step); then SGD runs on the normalized TD error (the 'ART' step). The rescale-first ordering means a rare huge target is absorbed into the stats before it can produce an exploding gradient. Proposition 2 proves the normalized target is bounded by +/- sqrt((1-beta)/beta) for any target distribution (beta=1e-4 gives (-100,100)) — bounded errors with zero prior knowledge of scale. On 57 Atari games, Double DQN on raw unclipped rewards + Pop-Art matched clipped Double DQN (median +0.4%, mean +34%) and confined gradient norms to under ~2 orders of magnitude vs ~6 for unclipped-unnormalized; the paper also proves Pop-Art SGD is exactly equivalent to shrinking lower-layer learning rates by sigma^-2.
- **为何有效**：Lower-layer SGD updates scale quadratically with target magnitude while the last layer already handles scale, so unnormalized large targets over-drive the representation for no reason; normalizing the regression restores a well-conditioned effective step size at every scale. The POP output-preservation step is the crucial half: adapting stats without it silently rewrites the network's predictions on all other inputs, destroying past learning exactly when a scale shift happens. Together they remove the per-domain scale hyperparameter while keeping learning stationary from the optimizer's view.
- **可借鉴**: Directly replaces our static q_reward_scale=0.07 patch for failure mode 3 (C51 top-atom saturation). Concretely: compute the scalar target y = max(MC, r + gamma*Q_hat) as now, normalize it with Pop-Art EMA stats (mu, sigma) BEFORE the categorical projection onto the fixed [-2,2] support, so the support always covers the running target distribution even as online returns grow or across tasks. One adaptation is required because our head is categorical, not scalar-linear: when (mu, sigma) change, the POP step becomes 'affinely shift/rescale the target network's atom locations and re-project onto the canonical atoms with the C51 projection operator' (output preservation up to projection error), and scalar quantities (MC lower bound, the no-BC floor level, logged Q) are denormalized as Q = sigma * sum(p_i z_i) + mu. Two CQN-AS-specific bonuses: (1) the normalization is a state-independent monotone affine map shared by all sibling bins, so the ~240-factor composite argmax and the top1-top2 margins of failure mode 2 are provably invariant to normalization drift — it can rescale margins but never reorder bins; (2) the DQfD margin 0.1 and the task-minimum floor distribution become defined in normalized units and auto-adapt per task, removing two more hand-set scale constants. Sparse-reward caution the paper's binary-regression 'rare spike' experiment motivates: run scale-only Pop-Art (mu frozen at 0 so the zero-return anchor and floor semantics are preserved) with a lower bound on sigma, or the max(1,S) denominator from DreamerV3, so early near-zero-return phases don't amplify noise.

#### Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models (DreamerV3) — Hafner et al., 2023 (arXiv; Nature 2025) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104>
- **方法**：Two separable scale mechanisms, verified from the paper. Critic side: the critic and reward heads output softmax distributions over K=255 atoms placed EQUALLY IN SYMLOG SPACE over [-20, 20] (symlog(x) = sign(x)ln(|x|+1)), i.e. exponentially spaced in return space covering roughly +/-5e8; scalar targets are symlog-transformed, two-hot encoded onto the two adjacent atoms, and trained with categorical cross-entropy; the scalar value is decoded as symexp of the expected atom. Actor side: lambda-returns are scaled by 1/max(1, S) where S is an EMA (decay 0.99) of the batch range between the 5th and 95th return percentiles, with stop-gradient on the return. The critic is additionally regularized toward the outputs of an EMA copy of its own weights (decay 0.98). One hyperparameter set works across 150+ tasks from Atari to sparse-reward Minecraft.
- **为何有效**：Symlog-spaced atoms make the FIXED support scale-invariant by construction: it spans many orders of magnitude while concentrating resolution near zero, exactly where sparse-reward return distributions live, so no nonstationary normalization state exists to drift or to invalidate old predictions. Cross-entropy on two-hot targets gives gradients whose magnitude is independent of target scale (unlike squared error). The max(1,S) percentile denominator fixes precisely the sparse-reward failure of std-based normalization: when most returns are near zero, std is tiny and dividing by it amplifies noise, whereas max(1,S) only ever shrinks large returns and leaves small ones untouched — the authors measure percentile scaling beating std-of-return and std-of-advantage normalization on sparse-reward tasks.
- **可借鉴**: Two independent transplants, both aimed at failure mode 3 and its brittleness. (a) Support-side (lowest risk, recommended first): keep C51's fixed support but respace the 51 atoms uniformly in symlog space and symlog the scalar target before projection (equivalently switch to two-hot in symlog space). This deletes q_reward_scale entirely with a STATIC change — no EMA state, nothing that drifts as the online policy improves — while representing our discounted RTG up to ~24 natively with fine resolution in [0,1] where most targets sit (our current 0.07 scale on [-2,2] is an effective +/-28.6 range, which symlog atoms cover). One honest caveat: decoded Q = symexp(E[symlog-return]) is a monotone transform of expected symlog-return, a mild ordering bias vs true expected return that DreamerV3's breadth of results suggests is benign. (b) Scale-side: wherever a runtime scalar normalization survives (Pop-Art stats if adopted, exploration bonus scale, floor level), use the percentile-range EMA with max(1,S) instead of variance — this is the sparse-reward-safe form for BiGym's mostly-zero returns. The critic-EMA regularizer is a third, smaller borrow relevant to failure mode 2: it damps the drift of Q on off-demo-manifold states between target updates, which is the state-coverage regime where our margins collapse.

#### Learning the Supports for Categorical Critic in Reinforcement Learning (DySEL) — Chang, Osa & Harada, 2026 (arXiv) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01880>
- **方法**：Makes the categorical critic's support endpoints [nu_min, nu_max] learned quantities instead of fixed hyperparameters. Targets are represented with HL-Gauss (the scalar Bellman target becomes a Gaussian-smoothed probability mass over the bins, sigma proportional to bin width) and the critic is trained by cross-entropy. The endpoints are trained by a min-max constrained objective: minimize cross-entropy plus a width penalty alpha*max(|nu_min|,|nu_max|) (pressure to shrink the support for finer bin resolution), subject to a coverage constraint that the target's truncated-Gaussian mass captured inside the support stays above 1-epsilon, enforced by an adversarially maximized Lagrange multiplier — so the support expands whenever targets would be truncated at the edges. Evaluated as TD3+DySEL on 11 DeepMind Control tasks (10 seeds, 3M steps): competitive with fixed-support [-100,100] HL-Gauss overall, with substantial gains on humanoid tasks where value magnitudes are hardest to preset. The paper explicitly notes Pop-Art's mean/scale statistics do not naturally extend to preserving distributional shape in categorical critics.
- **为何有效**：It optimizes the resolution-vs-coverage tradeoff that a fixed support hand-sets: too-narrow support truncates targets (our exact failure mode 3 — mass piling on the edge atom destroys value ordering), too-wide support wastes atoms and coarsens per-bin resolution. Edge truncation is made a first-class, differentiable constraint violation, so the support grows before saturation corrupts learning and shrinks when returns concentrate. Caveat on evidence strength: dense-reward DMC with TD3, preprint, and only 'competitive' outside humanoid — the mechanism fits our failure mode better than the experiments prove it.
- **可借鉴**: The categorical-native competitor to Pop-Art for the same slot in CQN-AS: keep the 51-atom head but make z_min, z_max trainable with (i) a width penalty and (ii) a coverage constraint driven by the fraction of projected target mass landing on the edge atoms — note that our measured '96% of demo targets clip to the top atom' statistic (failure mode 3 pre-fix) is literally DySEL's coverage-violation signal, so the diagnostic we already log becomes the training signal. Natural companion change: swap C51's point projection for HL-Gauss smoothing, which independent work (Farebrother et al. 2024) finds better-behaved for value cross-entropy. Two integration cautions: our no-BC floor distribution deliberately places mass AT the support minimum, so the floor must be defined relative to the learned nu_min (e.g. the bottom atom) or it would fight the coverage constraint; and target-network distributions must be re-projected when endpoints move (same machinery as the Pop-Art categorical POP step). Compared to symlog atoms this keeps Q in raw return units (no symexp ordering bias) at the cost of nonstationary endpoints.

#### Overcoming Exploration in Reinforcement Learning with Demonstrations — Nair et al., 2018 (ICRA) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.10089>
- **方法**：DDPG + HER with a second replay buffer R_D holding demo transitions; every update draws N_D=128 demo samples alongside N=1024 agent samples. An auxiliary BC loss L_BC = sum_i ||pi(s_i) - a_i||^2 over the demo samples is added to the actor update as a separate gradient term (lambda_1=1e-3 on the policy gradient J, lambda_2=1/N_D on L_BC). The Q-filter multiplies each demo sample's BC term by the indicator 1[Q(s_i, a_i) > Q(s_i, pi(s_i))]: the imitation gradient exists only where the critic judges the demonstrator's action better than the policy's own — verified per sample, every batch, using the current critic. Separately, some training episodes reset from a state sampled uniformly from a random demo trajectory (with the demo's final state as goal), disabled at test time. Ablations: no Q-filter slows sparse 3-block stacking; no BC loss is ~2x slower and fails >=4 blocks; no resets fails 5-6 block stacking.
- **为何有效**：BC supplies decision signal exactly where the critic is still ignorant, and the indicator makes imitation self-annealing per state: as soon as the critic prefers its own action, the BC gradient hard-zeros on that sample, so imitation cannot cap performance at demonstrator level or keep pushing on values the critic already ranks correctly. Resets-from-demo-states convert a sparse long-horizon problem into many short-horizon ones by starting rollouts near the reward.
- **可借鉴**: Gate the DQfD large-margin loss per factored decision (each of the ~240 timestep x dim x level choices): apply the margin push only when the critic's current argmax bin != the demo bin — the critic-only translation of Nair's Q(s,a_demo) > Q(s,pi(s)), since here pi(s) IS the argmax over sibling bins — with hard-zero gradient the moment the demo bin wins the ordering (no continued push to a 0.1 buffer). This targets Finding 1: on correctly-ranked factors the loss can no longer keep inflating the sibling gap (measured 0.89 vs true 0.009), leaving those bins' expected-Q to the C51 TD/MC target so return ordering can survive; and Finding 2: on online-visited off-manifold states where argmax disagrees with the demo, the gate stays open, preserving exactly the per-factor top1-top2 margins that collapsed 0.356->0.066 under naive BC removal. Caveat to test: DQfD's hinge is nominally self-limiting at margin 0.1 yet our gap reaches 0.89, so the gate must be an indicator on ordering applied before the C51 cross-entropy projection (which can overshoot a hinge), possibly with small hysteresis epsilon against flip-flopping. Second, independent transplant: reset a fraction of online BiGym episodes from mid-demo states so the ~24-step success reward tail is reachable early under sparse reward.

#### Critic Regularized Regression — Wang et al., 2020 (NeurIPS) 【high】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15134>
- **方法**：Offline RL where the policy objective is filtered/weighted BC: maximize E_{(s,a)~B}[ f(Q,pi,s,a) * log pi(a|s) ], with f non-negative and monotone in the critic's estimate; f=1 recovers plain BC. Two main filters: CRR-binary f = 1[A_hat(s,a) > 0] (hard per-sample inclusion) and CRR-exp f = exp(A_hat(s,a)/beta) (soft continuous weighting). The advantage is estimated sample-based: draw m actions a^j ~ pi(.|s) and set A_hat_mean = Q(s,a) - (1/m) sum_j Q(s,a^j), or the pessimistic A_hat_max subtracting the max sampled Q. The critic is distributional, trained purely by a TD divergence loss D(Q(s_t,a_t), r_t + gamma E_{a~pi} Q_target(s_{t+1},a)) — imitation never enters the critic loss. Findings: binary/max filters win on mixed-quality data (binary-max uses only 2-5% of actions on humanoid); exp wins on complex tasks with abundant good data.
- **为何有效**：Per-sample imitation weight proportional to the critic's own judgment concentrates BC gradient on actions the critic rates well but does not yet prefer, and vanishes elsewhere, so imitation never fights the critic's correct rankings; beta gives a continuous, state-conditional dial between full BC and pure filtering. Crucially the value function stays clean because the filter lives in the policy objective while the critic sees only TD targets.
- **可借鉴**: Two transplants. (a) Architectural, the deeper lesson: CRR corrupts nothing because TD-trained values and imitation-shaped action preference are separate objects. In critic-only CQN-AS that maps to a two-head critic: a C51 Q head trained ONLY by TD/MC targets (plus the no-BC dense-floor recipe of Finding 4), and a separate per-level bin-prior head trained by the (gated) margin/CE loss; act by argmax over Q(s,b) + c * prior_logit(s,b). This addresses Finding 1 by construction — the margin loss can never touch the Q head, so its sibling gaps stay return-ordered — while the prior head preserves the off-manifold decision margins of Finding 2 (flag: the prior head resembles a factored BC policy used only inside argmax scoring — the borderline proposal/prior case, no sampling, argmax-Q still decides). (b) Machinery: CRR's sample-based advantage over m candidates gives a chunk-level soft gate for the margin loss, w = f(A_hat_demo) with A_hat_demo = E[Q(demo chunk)] - mean over m candidate chunks (reuse the replay-argmax candidate machinery from Finding 4); note the sign must be INVERTED versus CRR (use exp(-A_hat_demo/beta) or 1[A_hat_demo < eps]) because our demos are near-expert and the failure is imitating where the critic already agrees, whereas CRR's filter exists to exclude bad actions from mixed data. CRR's distributional critic makes the whole recipe directly compatible with our C51 atoms.

#### When Life Gives You BC, Make Q-functions: Extracting Q-values from Behavior Cloning for On-Robot Reinforcement Learning (Q2RL) — Dodeja et al., 2026 (RSS) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05172>
- **方法**：Starts from a frozen pre-trained BC policy. Q-Estimation extracts its Q-function analytically with no extra training, via the soft-optimality assumption that pi_BC is Boltzmann in an implicit Q: Q_hat_BC(s,a) = V_BC(s) + alpha*log pi_BC(a|s) + alpha*H[pi_BC(.|s)], where V_BC comes from Monte Carlo returns of 50-100 BC rollouts.
- **为何有效**：The frozen BC-derived Q is a trustworthy value floor on demo-manifold states, so behavior falls back to demo-like actions exactly where the online critic is still untrained, and hands control to RL only where RL's value genuinely exceeds the floor.
- **可借鉴**: A behavior-policy-level gate, orthogonal to the loss-level gates above: during online collection, score the current critic's argmax chunk against a demo-prior chunk (the frozen offline no-BC critic's argmax from the sealed 66.4% recipe of Finding 4, or the nearest-demo action chunk) and execute whichever the frozen offline critic values higher. This attacks Finding 2 from the data side — online-visited states stay near the demo manifold while off-manifold decision margins are still forming, instead of compounding argmax errors across ~240 factored bin decisions — and, like Q2RL, keeps the imitation influence entirely out of the training loss (Finding 1 untouched by construction). The MC-return-based V_BC also validates our existing MC lower bound as a floor mechanism. Flag: if the demo-prior is a learned BC proposal network this is the borderline actor-as-proposal case — acceptable only because a Q comparison, not the proposal, decides the executed action. Rated medium because it is verified on actor-critic SAC and does not itself repair the margin-loss corruption.
- *另见类别*：recent 2025-2026 work

#### Discrete Sequential Prediction of Continuous Actions for Deep RL (SDQN) — Metz et al., 2017 (arXiv preprint) 【medium】
<https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.05035>
- **方法**：The original per-dimension autoregressive Q decomposition that Q-Transformer scales up. An N-dim continuous action is selected one discretized dimension at a time in an inner 'lower MDP' with zero discounting inside a timestep; a separate 'upper' Q^U(s,a) is trained on the full action with the ordinary Bellman TD loss.
- **为何有效**：Sequential 1-D argmax with each dimension conditioned on the previous choices makes the chain of maxima equal (in the limit) to the intractable joint max — a guarantee an INDEPENDENT per-factor argmax does not have.
- **可借鉴**: Two transplants. (1) An auxiliary joint-sequence Q head in CQN-AS: a small scalar head predicting Q of the EXECUTED full chunk (trained on the same max(MC, Bellman) target), with an SDQN-style MSE consistency loss tying the factored per-level/per-dim Q of the executed bins to it — giving all ~240 factored decisions one shared, return-grounded calibration anchor; this attacks failure mode 1 (factored Qs encode density not return — the joint head only ever sees real return targets) and failure mode 2 (a shared anchor resists per-factor margin decay off-manifold). (2) Their ordering ablation answers the recipe's 'autoregressive per-dim maximization order' open question for free: order is empirically benign, so if Q-Transformer-style cross-dim conditioning is adopted, any fixed order suffices — do not spend experiments on it.


## 2. 类别级综合

**1.1 Critic-only / value-based RL——target 与 loss 质量**：This category maps our two core measured pathologies onto known, separately-solvable problems with off-the-shelf operator/loss machinery. First, margin collapse without BC (failure mode 2) is literally the action-gap problem: gap-increasing operators — Advantage Learning's alpha*(V-Q) target devaluation, Munchausen's log-policy bonus with its provable (1+alpha)/(1-alpha) gap amplification, and clipped AL's guard for unreliable rankings — widen top1-top2 margins using the critic's OWN value ordering, offering a principled replacement for the DQfD BC-margin loss that props margins by demo frequency and thereby destroys return ordering (failure mode 1). Second, our working no-BC recipe (finding 4) is a rediscovery of the lower-bound-learning family (SIL's one-sided (R-V)_+ losses, Optimality Tightening's k-step lower bounds), which supplies principled upgrades: exceedance-prioritized replay, max-over-k lower bounds, and — the genuinely new tool — OT's trajectory-derived UPPER bounds to cap the demo-bin value inflation behind the invented 0.89 Q-gap. Third, on loss quality, the evidence (HL-Gauss, plus 2026 LLM-critic replication) says compute the scalar target first and classify it with Gaussian-smoothed cross-entropy rather than running C51's distributional projection: this removes the support-saturation trap (failure mode 3) at its root, keeps plasticity under the non-stationary online targets, and turns our hand-built floor distributions into one-line HL-Gauss targets, with QR-DQN held as the structural fallback for support management. The highest-value composite experiment this category suggests: HL-Gauss loss on max-over-k(MC, Bellman) targets with an OT upper-bound cap, plus a per-factor Munchausen/AL gap term, plus SIL-style (R-Q)_+ prioritization — every component critic-only and compatible with the fixed coarse-to-fine action-sequence argmax.

**1.7 2025–2026 前沿（action-chunking RL、demo 进价值空间）**：The 2025-2026 literature has independently converged on the exact diagnosis our lab measured: demos should enter the critic through value-space signals — one-sided return inequalities (LQL) or MC-return warm-start targets (Fill-in-the-Blanks) — never through action-imitation or margin losses, which several offline-to-online papers now explicitly identify as fine-tuning poison; this validates and formalizes our no-BC dense reward-Q recipe, with LQL's hinge-squared LB/UB penalties being the principled successor to our ad-hoc max(MC, Bellman) target. Second, the chunked-critic sub-field (SEAR, ACH, AQC, VAST) agrees that a monolithic fixed-K chunk Q is the bottleneck — the winning structure is per-prefix multi-horizon TD targets plus receding-horizon execution with per-state (discount-normalized) length selection, and SEAR demonstrates this beating CQN-AS head-to-head by >20% IQM, meaning our critic's per-timestep outputs are underexploited as backup targets and our full-K open-loop execution needlessly multiplies exposure to per-factor margin fragility. Third, margin/action-gap health is now treated as an architecture and loss-design problem rather than something to buy with BC margins: the mean-expansion layer enlarges action gaps by making sibling-relative residuals the learned quantity, and pairwise progress-ranking losses train exactly the ordering property that action selection consumes — together forming a two-pronged replacement for what the DQfD margin was (badly) doing for finding 2. Finally, decision-time gating between a frozen demo proposal and the argmax path under the learned Q (Q2RL) offers an execution-level safety net that protects online data quality while a margin-free critic matures — composable with all of the above and with our sealed 66.4% recipe.

**1.3 Demo-driven RL——带显式模仿正则（如何加权/门控/退火）**：The through-line of this category: every successful demo-driven RL method keeps demo influence OUT of the Q-function's value estimates. Demo adherence is delivered either through the sampling distribution (DQfD/DDPGfD's permanent prioritized demo data, R2D3's tiny per-element demo ratio, RLPD's 50/50 symmetric sampling — with R2D3 explicitly showing that too much demo weight causes demo-manifold overfitting, exactly our failure mode 1), through an actor loss the critic never sees (TD3+BC, ROT, AWAC — and where the weight is explicit, modern machinery makes it closed-loop: Q-gated in ROT, advantage-faded in AWAC, return-feedback-controlled in Zhao et al.), or at decision time with argmax-Q as the final judge (IBRL, Policy Decorator). Even DQfD, the direct ancestor of our margin loss, restricted it to demo transitions, used a margin calibrated to its value scale, and relied on n=10-step TD plus prioritized replay to keep Q grounded in actual returns — the margin was a demo-state action-classifier, never a value judge. For critic-only CQN-AS the composite prescription is: delete the margin from the Q-head (fixes fake Q-gaps, mode 1); replace its stabilizing role with (a) demo-priority/ratio sampling + n-step returns, (b) LayerNorm + a small min-of-2 critic ensemble to stop off-manifold value extrapolation (the measured mechanism behind margin collapse, mode 2), and (c) a frozen BC bin-proposal head whose candidate chunk competes with the greedy chunk under argmax-Q at both acting and bootstrap time — collapsing the 240-factored-decision fragility to a 2-candidate ranking problem. Any residual margin term should be gated per-state by demo MC-return vs critic greedy value and globally by success-rate feedback, so annealing is automatic rather than a schedule that collapses.

**1.5 Critic 稳定性 / 归一化 / 容量工艺**：This literature has converged on a coherent stack for making a lone value network trustworthy, and each layer of the stack maps onto a measured CQN-AS failure. First, normalization in the critic (LayerNorm in RLPD/SimBa/BRO, BatchNorm in CrossQ, hyperspherical in SimbaV2) bounds value extrapolation on out-of-distribution inputs — RLPD's ablation (complete failure on sparse-reward expert-demo Adroit without critic LayerNorm) is a published replication of our failure mode 2, where off-demo-manifold decision margins collapse to noise. Second, statistical pessimism from small ensembles with random-subset-min targets (REDQ/DroQ/RLPD) is the field's adaptive replacement for our hand-crafted CQL-style counterfactual floor distributions, and it is what unlocks UTD>>1 so a single-critic UTD=1 setup is leaving sample efficiency on the table. Third, our regime — 10k offline updates on 60 demos before online data matters — is a textbook heavy-priming/primacy-bias setup, and the cure (shrink-and-perturb or head resets at the offline-to-online boundary, replay retained: Nikishin, BBF, BRO) plus norm-growth control (NaP, SimbaV2) is directly transplantable. Fourth, BBF proves the whole recipe (annealed n-step 10-to-3, resets, weight decay, SPR auxiliary loss, EMA targets) composes inside a distributional C51-lineage value-only agent architecturally akin to CQN-AS, with n-step annealing being a principled version of our MC-lower-bound hack for propagating the 24-28-step sparse reward tail. Finally SimbaV2's return-statistics-based reward scaling generalizes our hand-tuned q_reward_scale=0.07 fix for C51 support saturation. The composite claim: much of what our BC margin loss currently provides (margin stability off-manifold) may be obtainable from normalization + ensemble pessimism + resets + representation auxiliaries, which unlike BC-margin do not corrupt return ordering.

**1.6 Off-policy target、多步回报与悲观主义**：This category delivers one loud, convergent message: our nstep=1 + BC-margin configuration is a known-bad half-recipe. Every successful demo-driven value method uses UNCORRECTED multi-step returns on demo data (DQfD n=10, R2D3 n=5, Rainbow n=3, Peng's Q(lambda)), and Kozuno et al. prove the resulting bias toward the behavior policy is benign-to-desirable when the behavior is expert — while Retrace-style safe correction would cut demo traces exactly where the critic disagrees with the demo, negating the benefit; Q-chunking shows our action-sequence critic makes K-step backups UNBIASED for free by treating the chunk as one atomic action. Second, our hard task-minimum floor on counterfactual sibling bins is an infinite-strength, uncalibrated CQL: it manufactures the fake 0.89 Q-gaps of finding 1, and Cal-QL's one-line fix — floor the push-down at the state's behavior MC return-to-go instead of a global minimum — plus WSRL's warmup-recalibration phase directly target the offline-to-online collapse of finding 2. Third, bootstrap corruption from OOD sibling bins should be attacked at the max itself: BCQ/EMaQ restrict bootstrapping to behavior-supported actions via generative candidates, and EMaQ's autoregressive proposal + expected-max-over-N is a near-exact structural match to coarse-to-fine bin factorization, replacing the exponentially margin-sensitive ~240-factor argmax of finding 2. Finally, DR3 warns that TD on 60 demos has an implicit feature co-adaptation failure (rank collapse, sibling aliasing) that no target-side fix addresses — a plausible representational substrate for finding 1 that needs its own explicit regularizer.

**1.2 Value-based RL 在连续/因子化动作空间（含 CQN 家族）**：This category converges on one diagnosis and three cures for our situation. Diagnosis first: the stock CQN-AS recipe (RL loss scale 0.1 vs BC 1.0, tiny sigma=0.01 exploration noise, target-net acting) is engineered around a demo-manifold-scoring critic — our finding 1 is the original design working as intended, so a no-BC line must replace the margin's function, not re-tune it. Cure one is coupling the factors: DecQN's mean-decomposed joint TD, BDQ's branch-averaged shared target plus 1/(N+1) trunk-gradient rescaling, and ARSQ's autoregressive dimension conditioning all exist because ~240 independently-bootstrapped heads drift apart in scale and recombine frequent-but-suboptimal bins — exactly our measured margin decay and sibling-ordering coin flip. Cure two is shrinking the decision problem: QT-Opt's CEM and QC's Best-of-N-from-a-flow-prior replace the exponentially-fragile composite argmax with a joint ranking over a few dozen on-manifold candidates, which a critic with unreliable global values but decent local ordering can still win; GQN's grow-from-coarse schedule is the same idea applied to our level hierarchy for free. Cure three is pessimism by construction instead of by penalty: IQL's in-sample expectile backup never queries unexecuted sibling bins at all, offering a principled replacement for both the margin loss and our hand-built counterfactual floor, with tau as the pessimism knob. Finally, QC's decomposition of its own gains says our single cheapest unclaimed win is the chunk-conditioned h-step backup — we already pay for a sequence critic yet still bootstrap at nstep=1, forfeiting the one piece of QC that is unambiguously bias-free and directly addresses sparse-reward credit propagation.

**1.4 Demo-driven RL——无显式 BC loss（核心类别）**：This category shows demos can drive value-based RL through four BC-free channels, all compatible with a critic-only coarse-to-fine architecture: batch composition (RLPD/HIL-SERL symmetric 50/50 demo sampling, with HIL-SERL growing the privileged buffer from on-policy corrections), value targets (SQIL's hardwired rewards and Cal-QL's MC-calibrated pessimism — the exact family our sealed dense-floor recipe already lives in, now with principled refinements like per-state calibrated floors), action proposals with Q deciding (IBRL's frozen-BC proposal in both exploration and the TD-target max — the sharpest fix for our measured off-manifold margin collapse), and start-state distribution (JSRL roll-ins, Salimans/Backplay reverse resets, and RFCL's per-demo reverse+forward curriculum, which beat RLPD/Cal-QL on high-precision randomized-init manipulation and makes failure mode 1 structurally impossible). The stabilizers the RLPD line credits — critic LayerNorm bounding OOD extrapolation, 10-critic ensembles with subset-min targets, UTD 10-20 — are precisely what CQN-AS currently lacks (single critic, UTD=1), and they target the same extrapolation mechanism behind our margin-collapse measurement. WSRL and Cal-QL jointly give a mechanistic account of our observed no-BC collapse — sudden distribution shift forcing a global Q recalibration that reshuffles the ~240-factor composite argmax — and prescribe tested fixes: frozen-policy warmup rollouts at the offline-to-online seam and return-scale clamps. The highest-leverage experiments for beating (not just matching) official: LayerNorm+ensemble+UTD on the current recipe, IBRL-style demo proposals in target and exploration, WSRL warmup at the seam, and RFCL state-reset curricula as the fully loss-free endgame.

**1.8 补检（gap critic 指定）**：The backfill closes the survey's biggest gap: Q-Transformer is published, real-robot-validated precedent for essentially every mechanism in the lab's sealed no-BC recipe — a quadratic floor regressing all non-dataset bins to the minimal attainable return, MC-return-floored Bellman targets, and mixed demo+failure data — and it supplies tested answers to the recipe's open questions: the floor should be always-on (alpha=1, no annealing, applied to failed online episodes too), softmax/margin-shaped conservatism specifically degenerates into a behavior-density critic (their ~8% ablation is our finding-1 pathology reproduced), and n-step targets that skip intermediate per-dim maximization are safe under the floor and 4x cheaper. Cal-QL adds the one principle Q-Transformer's purely-offline setting cannot: at the offline→online seam, floor pessimism must be calibrated — never push a counterfactual bin below the state's behavior MC return-to-go — or the value scale detaches from reality and produces exactly the unlearning dip we measure as margin decay (finding 2). SDQN, the decomposition's origin, shows dimension ordering is empirically benign but that factored per-dim values need an explicit consistency loss against a joint full-action Q to stay return-calibrated. Collectively the category's lesson for CQN-AS: keep the floor, calibrate it with MC return-to-go, condition the factored argmax autoregressively (or at least anchor it with a joint-sequence Q head), and buy propagation speed with floor-protected n-step — all changes to targets and auxiliary losses, none touching the coarse-to-fine critic-only skeleton.

**1.8 补检（gap critic 指定）**：This category's collective lesson is that CQN-AS's fragility is not Q-learning in a huge action space per se — it is HOW the max is computed. Composing ~240 independent per-factor bin decisions makes the policy's decision margin a product of fragile per-factor margins (our Finding 2), whereas AQL, QMLE, and IDQL all converged on the same alternative: evaluate a small set of COMPLETE joint candidates and take one argmax, so the margin is a single joint Q-gap. The three papers supply complementary machinery: AQL gives the supervised-amortized proposal (NLL of best-found action + entropy, no policy gradient, discretized proposals beat continuous ones — validating our bins); QMLE adds replay-cached best-argmax reuse and proposal ensembling, which is the formalized, monotone version of our recipe's ad-hoc 'candidate backup from replay argmax', plus the action-in architecture argument for a joint chunk-scoring head; IDQL shows the candidate-ranking critic should be trained in-sample only (expectile-V bootstrap), removing the OOD queries the BC-margin loss currently papers over, and shows the demo-manifold signal belongs in the proposal, not in Q. The unifying transplant: relocate the BC/demo-manifold information from the Q-loss into the candidate proposal distribution, train the critic on return-carrying in-sample targets (our no-BC recipe), and let argmax-Q over N joint action-sequence candidates decide — all three keep the critic as the decider, so the borrow is borderline-actor only in that a policy-shaped proposal network exists.

**1.8 补检（gap critic 指定）**：The static q_reward_scale=0.07 patch sits at one point on a design spectrum these three papers map out, and all three converge on the same diagnosis: a value network with a fixed output range must have its target scale managed by the algorithm, not by a per-task constant. Pop-Art is the canonical adaptive answer — EMA target statistics plus an exact output-preserving head rescale — and transplants to CQN-AS with one honest complication: for a categorical head the POP step becomes an atom-shift plus C51 re-projection rather than a weight rescale, and sparse rewards demand a scale floor (or frozen mu=0) so near-zero-return phases don't amplify noise. DreamerV3 shows the cheapest robust fix is often static after all: atoms spaced uniformly in symlog space make one fixed support cover many orders of magnitude with resolution concentrated where sparse-reward targets live, deleting the hyperparameter with no drifting state, while its max(1,S) percentile scaling is the sparse-reward-safe form for any scalar normalization that remains. DySEL (2026) closes the loop by making the support endpoints themselves trainable under an explicit edge-mass coverage constraint — notably, the '96% of targets clip to the top atom' statistic we already measure is exactly its training signal. A key CQN-AS-specific fact eases all of this: any state-independent monotone/affine renormalization is shared across sibling bins, so the ~240-factor composite argmax and its decision margins (failure mode 2) are invariant to scale adaptation — these mechanisms can only recondition learning, not reorder actions. Recommended order of attack: symlog-spaced atoms first (static, zero-risk of drift), Pop-Art or DySEL-style adaptation only if cross-task or within-run return drift is then still observed.

**1.8 补检（gap critic 指定）**：The backfill category resolves the Finding-1/Finding-2 tension with one principle: imitation signal should be state-conditional — present with full strength exactly where the critic's own ranking fails, and hard-zeroed where it already succeeds — rather than globally weighted (Zhao-style) or globally removed. The gate can live at three levels, and they compose: a per-factor loss indicator on argmax-vs-demo disagreement (Nair's Q-filter, translated to sibling-bin ordering), a soft sample-based advantage weight with an anneal dial beta (CRR, with the filter sign inverted for near-expert demos), and a behavior-time arbitration between the critic's argmax chunk and a frozen demo-prior chunk (Q2RL), which fixes the data distribution instead of the loss. CRR and Q2RL additionally teach a structural lesson: methods that never let imitation enter the TD-trained value function avoid value corruption by construction, suggesting a two-head CQN-AS critic (TD-only C51 Q head + margin-trained bin-prior head combined only at argmax time). One measured caveat governs implementation: DQfD's hinge is already nominally self-limiting at margin 0.1 yet our sibling gaps reach 0.89, so any gate must be a hard indicator evaluated against the C51 cross-entropy projection, and its effect on the 0.89-gap and off-manifold margin metrics should be probed directly. Nair's resets-from-demo-states is a free, orthogonal sparse-reward exploration transplant for BiGym regardless of which gate is chosen.

**调研最重要的三个外部事实**：
1. **原版 CQN-AS 的 loss 权重是 RL 0.1 : BC 1.0**（CQN-AS 原文）——F1
   （critic 只编码 demo 频率）是原设计的**既定属性**，不是我们 fork 的 bug。
   所以任何 no-BC 线必须**替代** margin 的功能，而不是重新调权。
2. **SEAR（2026）在 Metaworld 最难 20 任务上以 >20% IQM 正面击败 CQN-AS**，
   靠的是 per-prefix 多地平线 TD target + 随机前缀长度重规划——直接证明
   瓶颈在 target 结构（我们已付钱买了 per-timestep 序列 critic 却只做
   nstep=1 backup），不在 critic-only 前提本身。
3. **RLPD 的消融在 sparse-reward + demo 的 Adroit 上"无 critic LayerNorm
   即完全失败"**——是我们 F2（off-manifold margin 塌缩 = OOD 值外推）的
   已发表复现；normalization + 小 ensemble + UTD 是该机制的标准解。
4. **Q-Transformer（CoRL 2023）就是 no-BC dense 配方的已发表先例**
   （counterfactual-sibling 悲观正则 ≈ 我们的 floor；MC-floored target ≈
   我们的 max(MC, Bellman)），且给了三个差异点：(a) floor **always-on、
   全数据**（含失败 episode——他们 20k 条失败数据把策略推高 70% over IL）
   ——**注意这与本地证据直接冲突**：我们测得 positive-only 是必需稳定器
   （plain dense 全数据臂 online margin 塌缩 0.356→0.066）。差异可能在
   他们跨维自回归（无独立因子脆弱性）或 sigmoid [0,1] Q 头；这是一个
   便宜的判决性实验（自回归条件 × floor 制度 2×2）；(b) softmax/margin
   形状的惩罚被他们消融为有害（= F1 的独立确认）；(c) timestep 内跨维
   自回归条件（我们 ~240 因子独立 argmax 缺 joint-max 保证的原理性修复）。

### Route R2 —【新·第一优先】target 结构升级：chunk 内 per-prefix 多地平线 backup
【对准 F3/F5；SEAR、Q-chunking、DQfD/R2D3 n-step、Peng's Q(λ)、LQL】

序列 critic 每个 (k,l) 头现在都做独立 nstep=1 backup。改为：
- **chunk 原子 backup**（QC 已证无偏 & 免费）：K 步实付奖励 + γ^K bootstrap
  ——chunk 视为单动作，demo 数据上无 off-policy 修正问题；
- **per-prefix 目标**（SEAR）：一条 replay 段给出 K 个地平线的 target
  （G^(k) = Σ_k 折扣奖励 + γ^k·Q(s_{t+k}, 下一 chunk)），K 个 backup
  几乎免费（forward pass 已有全部头）；
- 可选第三档：LQL 的单边 hinge 不等式（实付回报只在 Q 低估时上推），
  是现配方 max(MC, Bellman) 的原理化版本，也是 demo 高度 off-policy 时
  唯一安全的多步用法；
- 补检增援：Q-Transformer 的 dimension-skipping n-step target（floor
  在场时中间 max 的高估被有界，多步安全）。
1.6 综合的结论一致：demo 数据上用**不修正的多步回报**（DQfD n=10、R2D3
n=5）是每个成功 demo-driven 值方法的标配，Retrace 式修正反而在 critic
与 demo 不一致处截断 trace、抵消收益。24–28 步成功尾巴（F3 的根源）由
多地平线直接传播，不再依赖 51-atom 支撑的表达力。
**注意与 A18/A19 的交互**：prefix 目标改变的是 horizon 结构，与
"裁剪 vs 校准"支撑几何正交；BC 版与 no-BC 版各测一次。

### Route R1 — 用 action-gap 机制替代 BC-margin 的真实功能
【对准 F2，机制最干净；AL 算子、Munchausen、Mean-Expansion Layer、clipped AL】

已测定：BC-margin 的实际作用不是"教会价值"而是**维持 off-manifold 决策
margin**（F2）。文献里有两类专为此设计、不需要 demo 标签的机制：
- **算子类**：Advantage Learning（对非贪婪执行动作的 target 下压
  α·(V−Q)，最优性保持且 gap 增大；Atari 37/60 胜 DQN）、Munchausen
  log-policy bonus（可证 (1+α)/(1−α) 的 gap 放大 + 隐式 KL 自正则）、
  clipped AL（排序不可靠时的保护）。落点：C51 投影前对标量 target 移位，
  几十行。
- **架构类**：Mean-Expansion Layer（ICML 2026 spotlight）——参数-free 末层
  把兄弟 bin 的共享 baseline 架构性析出，网络学的残差**就是** action gap；
  TD 误差摊到全部兄弟上，overestimation 同降。对我们每个因子化 5-bin 头
  逐一插入（注意：原文只验证了 scalar/quantile，C51 需在期望 Q 或
  atom-logit 层面适配）。
两者都作用于策略访问的**每一个状态**（包括 BC 够不着的 off-manifold
online 状态——demo 标签缺位处正是 F2 塌缩发生的地方）。
机制验收：margin-collapse 探针（online-success 状态 top1−top2 @10k→30k）。

### Route R4 — 模仿移出 loss、进入决策先验（proposal + critic 选择）
【对准 F1+F2；IBRL、Q2RL、QC Best-of-N、EMaQ/BCQ、QT-Opt】

critic loss 里彻底没有模仿项（用 no-BC 配方训练），demo 依从性改由决策时
实现：BC/flow 策略提议 N 个候选 chunk，critic argmax 选择。文献支撑充分：
IBRL（冻结 BC proposer 同时进探索与 TD-target max）、Q2RL（RSS 2026：
冻结 BC 提议 vs RL 提议按 Q 门控执行，contact-rich 任务 1–2 小时到 100%，
"critic off-manifold 未成熟时 gate 回退 demo 流形 chunk"正是 F2 的执行级
保险丝）、EMaQ（自回归 proposal + expected-max-over-N 与 coarse-to-fine
因子化结构近乎同构）。机制收益：~240 因子决策的复合 argmax（对单因子
margin 指数敏感，F2 之根）坍缩为"N 个整 chunk 中选一"——margin 脆弱性从
per-factor 降到 per-chunk，且 critic 不再需要编码流形（proposal 集已限制
在流形上），只需局部排序能力，恰是 no-BC 配方已能提供的。仓库已有全部
机件：`flow_policy` rerank（stage 146/152）、QC 移植
（`robobase/method/q_chunking.py`）、`top2_joint_beam`（cqn_as.py:79——
categorical 策略自身的 top-k 束即可当 proposal 集，不必依赖弱 flow
采样器）。
变体：(a) proposal 只约束 bootstrap max（BCQ/EMaQ 式，替换现有
`critic_replay_max`）；(b) proposal 同时用于决策时；(c) 冻结 proposer
（IBRL 式）vs 温和 EMA 更新。
补检增援：**AQL**（DeepMind 2020）给出完整形式化——N 个整 chunk 候选
（自回归 proposal 以 NLL-of-best-found + entropy 监督训练，非 policy
gradient）+ replay 动作与 demo 动作作为免费候选 + bootstrap max 也在同一
候选集上取——把现有 `critic_replay_max` 收编为特例；**Q-Transformer**
的 timestep 内跨维自回归条件是另一互补形态（改分解而非改候选集）。
F1 警戒（AQL borrow 原话）：候选排序只在 Q 真的载有回报排序时有用——
此路线必须骑在 no-BC/return-trained critic 上，配 BC-margin critic 只会
重新选出 demo 高频 chunk。
**本仓已有前作（cqn-flow.md §29，Stage 146 系列）**：rerank 机制已确立
（M=8 flow + Q-rerank 61.3% vs flow-BC 单独 41.3%，5/6 seed-pair 正向）；
EMA 权重根除 flow 后期塌 0（146b：64.0%）；瓶颈在 flow 采样器精度
（M1 41.3% vs categorical 62.7%）与训练预算，距 clean 72.0% 还差 8pp。
**新意所在**：当时判官是 BC 训练的 critic（F1 判决它不会判案）；本路线换
no-BC/gap-算子 critic 当判官，且 proposal 集可以来自 categorical 策略自身
的 top-k 束（不必依赖弱 flow 采样器）——146 的残差瓶颈两个都绕开。

### Route R3 — margin 的外科手术：只留 L0 / Q-gated 自适应权重
【对准 F1，最便宜；ROT、DQfD 原文、Adaptive BC Regularization】

保留 margin 但剥夺它雕刻细层价值的权力：
(a) margin/FOSD 都只施加于 L0（粗层管"在哪个区域"，L1/L2 交给纯 TD 学
局部排序——sibling 探针正是在 L1/L2 上测到硬币）。落点
`cqn.py:1439-1467` 两项各乘 level mask；
(b) ROT 式 soft Q-filter：只在 Q(policy) < Q(demo) 的状态上开模仿项；
(c)【补检新增，本路线最锋利形态】Nair et al. 2018 的 Q-filter 按
**每个因子决策**门控：仅当该 (timestep, dim, level) 槽位的 argmax bin ≠
demo bin 时才施加 margin 推力，demo bin 一旦赢得排序立即硬零梯度
（不许再推到 0.1 缓冲）——正确排序的因子上 loss 无法继续吹胀伪 gap
（F1 的 0.89 vs 0.009），而 online off-manifold 分歧处 gate 保持打开
（F2 需要的 margin 恰好保住）。需在 C51 投影前做指示函数门 + 小滞回 ε。
文献警示（1.3 综合）：DQfD 原文的 margin 只是 demo 状态上的**动作分类器**
，从来不是价值判官，且靠 n=10-step TD + 优先回放保持 Q 接地——我们的
配方把 margin 用成了价值雕刻刀（RL:BC = 0.1:1.0）还配 nstep=1，是
"known-bad half-recipe"。注意负面清单：return-gated margin（A17）配对
+0.7~+1.7 未过门——(b) gating 依据不同（Q 比较 vs 回报门），区分对待。

### Route R6 — two-head 解耦：决策头 × 校准头
【对准 F3 张力 + F1 验收；HL-Gauss、Cal-QL、pairwise ranking、SimbaV2 reward scaling】

A18/A19 定案：裁剪粗目标 → 决策 margin 抗漂移；校准目标 → 排序好但决策
易碎。**二者要分头拿，不必二选一**：
- 决策头：保持现配方（窄支撑裁剪 / 或 + Route R1 的 gap 机制），只管 argmax；
- 校准头：宽支撑或 scalar 头，回归 scaled 回报（MC/TD 混合），只管
  价值判断——qselect3 竞技场、eval-time 判官、以后的 planning。
文献工具箱：HL-Gauss（先算标量 target 再高斯平滑分类，绕开 C51 投影的
支撑饱和陷阱——但 A18/A19 警示"去饱和"对决策头有害，故只用于校准头）；
Cal-QL（sibling floor 从全局 task-minimum 升级为 per-state 的 demo MC
return-to-go 校准参照，修 F1 的伪 0.89 Q-gap）；pairwise ranking 辅助
loss（Robot Critics 2026：同状态兄弟 bin / 候选 chunk 按实付 MC 回报做
二元比较交叉熵——直接训练 argmax 消费的那个量"相对排序"）；SimbaV2 的
return-statistics reward scaling（把手调 0.07 换成自适应）。
补检增援（F3 工具对）：**Pop-Art**——EMA (μ,σ) 在 C51 投影前归一化标量
target，支撑永远覆盖目标分布；关键性质：归一化是全兄弟 bin 共享的单调
仿射映射，**可证不改变 argmax 与 margin 排序**（只会缩放不会重排）——
这是它与 A18 天真缩放的本质区别，但"分离度缩进漂移噪声带"的 A18 教训
仍需实测检验；**DySEL**（2026）——可学习支撑端点，coverage 约束的驱动
信号正是我们已在记录的"96% demo 目标裁顶 atom"统计（诊断变训练信号）；
no-BC floor 须改为相对学习端点定义，否则与 coverage 约束打架。
dueling 已把 V/A 分开，此路线是它的延伸。验收天然双轨：密封不回退（决策头）
+ qselect3 q > max(solo)（校准头，F1 的终极验收）。
**本仓已有权衡面（cqn-flow.md §28，Stage 147/148）**：canonical 在线校准锚
任何有效剂量都两位数 pp 损伤行为（0.02→−14.7，0.1→−40）；冻结模仿特征上的
post-hoc sidecar 头 held-out ρ 只到 0.56–0.58（特征装不下价值信息）；
联合训练 decoupled 锚到 ρ=0.749 但行为 −9.3pp；pooled sidecar（6× 数据）
已过 ρ≥0.8 门槛交付。**教训**：校准头若共享 encoder 且回传梯度，就要付
行为税；本路线设计必须显式选择 stop-gradient 边界（校准头不回传 encoder /
或接受轻税），并从 pooled-sidecar 的达标形态出发而不是从零。

### Route R7 — demo 影响走数据通道：RFCL 反向+前向课程【对准 F2+F4+F5，零模仿梯度】

no-BC 线明确记录：loss 通道已扫尽，**数据通道未测**。文献给出比
Salimans-Chen/Backplay 强得多的现代形态：**RFCL（ICLR 2024）**——
per-demo reset 点由后往前推进（reverse stage）+ 全随机初始分布的前沿
课程（forward stage），在**高精度 + 高初始随机化**（正是 BiGym 的 regime）
上击败 RLPD/Cal-QL/JSRL，代码库中零 BC loss。demo 影响只走初始状态分布：
F1 结构性不可能，demo 不再是 off-policy replay（F5）而是 on-policy 起点。
BiGym 状态灌注机件已有（`capture_bigym_branch_state` 全套），成本是
工程（reset 注入 + 课程调度）而非算法风险。廉价近似先试 JSRL 式 demo
前缀 roll-in（无需状态恢复，但 BiGym 随机初始会让开环前缀漂移——正是
RFCL 状态重置消除的弱点）。

### Route R5 — 现代工艺红利包（F5 清扫，与任一路线并行）
【RLPD、REDQ/DroQ、BBF、primacy bias、CrossQ/SimBa/SimbaV2、SPR、WSRL】

RLPD/SimBa/BBF 系确立的、我们一项没吃的：critic **LayerNorm**（RLPD 消融
：sparse+demo 的 Adroit 上没有它完全失败 = F2 的已发表复现）、小
ensemble（2×，random-subset-min target）、UTD>1、demo/online 对称 50/50、
offline→online 边界的 shrink-and-perturb / head reset（10k 次 60-demo
更新是教科书级 primacy-bias 设置；BBF/BRO 证明 reset + 保留 replay 可行）、
SPR 式自预测辅助 loss（3-cam 84×84 CNN 的表征质量）、WSRL 的 warmup
rollout 相位（offline→online 接缝处先用冻结策略滚数据再开梯度——
Cal-QL/WSRL 对 F2 的机制解释：突然分布位移迫使全局 Q 重校准，argmax
大洗牌）。RLPD 教训"打包才起效"：先整包 screen，过门再拆解归因。
风险：与 dense-floor 配方的交互未知。

### 优先级与理由（文献综合后定版）

| 序 | 路线 | 成本 | 对准 | 理由 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R2（prefix/chunk 多地平线 target） | 低 | F3+F5 | SEAR 正面击败 CQN-AS 的头号成分；架构已付费未使用；BC/no-BC 两版都适用 |

| 4 | R4（proposal + Q 门控执行） | 中 | F1+F2 | 机件全在（beam/flow/QC）；Q2RL 提供最新执行级保险丝设计 |

### 卡 R2a — chunk/prefix 多地平线 target（Route R2）

- **假设**：把每个 (k,l) 头的 nstep=1 backup 换成 (i) chunk 原子 K 步
  backup（QC 式）或 (ii) SEAR 式 per-prefix 目标（G^(k) = Σ折扣奖励 +
  γ^k bootstrap），可加速 24–28 步成功尾巴的传播，30k 行为超过各自
  same-start 对照。
- **臂**：先在**官方 BC 配方**上做（主线夺冠路径）：对照 vs (i) vs (ii)，
  但 4 slot 限制下首发 (ii) 两种子 + 对照复用已有 same-start 数据；
  no-BC 版跟进。
- **落点**：`cqn_as.py` target 构造处；replay 已支持 K 步（QC 移植时
  加过 `replay.nstep=K` 通路，见 Q_CHUNKING_JAX.md）。奖励在 chunk 内
  实付，无 off-policy 修正问题。
- **门（30k, 配对, n=2）**：配对均值 ≥ +5pp 或（±5pp 内且 value-fidelity
  探针 Q-vs-RTG Spearman/时序排序显著改善）。
- **风险**：与 temporal ensemble 的执行语义交互（QC 关 ensemble）；
  与 truncate_demo_at_success 的尾部奖励截断交互——wiring 阳性对照必须
  打印实际 K 步回报统计。

### 卡 R1a — action-gap 算子替代 margin（Route R1）

- **假设**：在 chosen-bin C51 loss 上加 Advantage-Learning 式 gap 项（或
  Munchausen log-policy 项）能在 **无 BC** 制度下减缓 online off-manifold
  top1−top2 margin 塌缩（F2），30k 行为不低于 no-BC 对照。
- **臂**：base = sibling Stage-42 no-BC 配方（66.4% 配方）same-start
  offline-10k 检查点分叉；+AL(α∈{0.3, 0.9}) 或 +Munchausen(α=0.9, τ 按
  C51 期望 Q 改写)，先single-α 两种子。
- **落点**：`cqn.py` critic loss（chosen/all expected-Q 已在
  `cqn.py:1450-1467` 现成计算）；四层配置链全动；阳性对照 = 加项后
  `bc_margin_gap` 诊断分布位移 + action trace 变化。
- **门（30k, 配对, n=2）**：margin 探针塌缩速率显著慢于对照（10k→30k
  top1−top2 保持 ≥2× 对照），且 30k 50-ep 配对均值 ≥ 对照 −2pp。
  行为门与机制门必须**同时**过。
- **停止规则**：任一种子 NaN / 熵泵征兆（全局价值塌缩，参照 label
  smoothing 事故）立即停。

### 卡 R3a — margin/FOSD 只施加于 L0（Route R3）

- **假设**：把 BC-margin hinge 与 FOSD 项都用 level mask 限制在 L0
  （L1/L2 交给纯 TD），critic 在 L1/L2 学出高于硬币的 sibling 回报排序
  （F1），密封行为不回退（BC 仍在，只是撤出细层）。
- **臂**：官方 BC 配方 same-start 分叉：full-margin 对照 vs L0-only；
  两种子。落点：`cqn.py:1439-1467` 两项各乘 level mask（axis 1），
  mean 改为 masked mean。
- **门（30k, 配对, n=2）**：sibling 探针（固定共享状态集、配对重跑，
  遵守 A21 仪器警告）L1/L2 sign-acc ≥0.60（对照 ≈0.50），且 30k 行为
  配对 ≥ −2pp。
- **风险预案**：若 L1/L2 纯 TD 因 F3（支撑饱和）学不到东西，此臂与
  `q_reward_scale=0.07` 组合成 R3b（只 scale 细层 TD 目标不动 L0）。
